# Grade 1 Islamic Education visual additions

Scope: 66 regular Grade 1 lessons, Arabic N and English NN, all three terms. IG excluded.

15 lessons receive one additive visual slide each. The other51 retain all slides; grade1-review.json records the lesson-specific decision. Original teacher-deck.html files are the source. No religious text, learning activity, assessment or media tag is rewritten.

All vector shapes and text are authored for this project. No downloaded images, stock assets or third-party image licence dependencies were introduced. All text remains editable.

## Fact checks

- Prayer count relationship: Fajr2, Dhuhr4, Asr4, Maghrib3, Isha4 obligatory rak'ahs. Islamic Relief UK, scholar-reviewed5January2026. Opened23November2026. https://www.islamic-relief.org.uk/resources/knowledge-base/five-pillars-of-islam/salah/how-to-pray-salah/
- Quraysh seasonal destinations: winter Yemen, summer Ash-Sham. Quran.com Ibn Kathir abridged tafsir, opened23November2026. https://quran.com/quraysh/2/tafsirs/en-tafisr-ibn-kathir
- Cow/milk and hen/egg pairings are the exact direct relationships in learn2 of each language's I Love the Creatures of My Lord lesson. No new biological mechanism is claimed.
- Animal food/water, cleanliness tools/actions, story admission, eating-stage examples and tree beneficiaries each come from the named adjacent teaching sections, identified in visuals.py.

## Boundaries

No portraits of prophets or companions. No divine, angelic, Paradise, Hell or other unseen depictions. No imagined sacred historical scene. No inferred ritual procedure. No invented geographic route. Visible qualifiers distinguish examples from complete lists and dots from a time scale.

Source-text issues are recorded separately in source-text-issues.json. Three grade1 issues were later minimally corrected under specific user authorization and separately verified live. New concerns remain held and are not illustrated. The camel-water error is independently checked against the Library of Congress zoology answer: https://www.loc.gov/everyday-mysteries/categories/zoology/item/how-much-water-does-a-camels-hump-hold/

## QA

Original-slide preservation and exact module reproduction are checked per changed deck. Every added slide receives individual local and deployed full-size pixel inspection. grade1-review.json records local/live status. Published milestone only after live QA completes.
