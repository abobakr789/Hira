# Grade 8 Arabic definitions, first correction batch

24 November 2026. Five lessons, fifteen HTML files. Original media and religious text retained.

- Sustainable development: meeting present needs without harming future generations, including economic, social and environmental dimensions. Replaces a national-unity-only definition. Source: https://www.un.org/sustainabledevelopment/blog/2023/08/what-is-sustainable-development/
- Hard and soft power: hard power uses coercion or material incentives, not only war. Soft power does not replace it. Nye dates his term to 1990. Sources: https://www.nature.com/articles/palcomms20178 and https://www.belfercenter.org/publication/future-power
- Net migration: arrivals minus departures over the same period. May be positive or negative. Source: https://population.un.org/wpp/assets/Files/WPP2024_Methodology.pdf
- Marwan II: defeated at the Zab, later killed in Egypt. Source: https://pbe.kcl.ac.uk/data/D53/F55.htm
- Astrolabe is not a compass. It measures celestial altitude; the maritime form helps latitude. Compass indicates direction; sundial uses the Sun's shadow for time. Source: https://www.rmg.co.uk/stories/space-astronomy/what-mariners-astrolabe
