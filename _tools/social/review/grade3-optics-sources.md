# Ibn al-Haytham accuracy corrections

Accessed 24 November 2026.

T3L15: remove absolute first-eye-anatomy claim. Galen described anatomy earlier; Ibn al-Haytham advanced optics and vision through experiments and synthesis. Charles G. Gross academic article: https://muslimheritage.com/ibn-haytham-eye-brain/ . A 2007 medical overview uses a stronger first-accurate-description claim, but the narrower formulation avoids disputed priority: https://www.annsaudimed.net/doi/full/10.5144/0256-4947.2007.464 .

Camera obscura: used in his optical experiments, not a modern photographic camera. UNESCO partner account: https://www.1001inventions.com/yearoflight/ . Book of Optics and importance for experimental optics: https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/spotlight-international-year-light .

Photography: 1827 Niepce heliograph is the earliest surviving camera-obscura photograph, not the first picture ever. Collection source: https://www.hrc.utexas.edu/niepce-heliograph/ .

Removed unsupported optics-to-Newton-gravity link. Preserved existing images and religious quotation. Corrected derived summaries, questions, objectives and answer keys consistently. Original source wording is not reproduced as an authoritative current fact when it conflicts with this evidence.
