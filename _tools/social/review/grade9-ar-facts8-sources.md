# Grade 9 Arabic social studies: density and population issues
Reviewed 24 November 2026.

UN-Habitat distinguishes planned density with sufficient services from overcrowding and inadequate housing. High density alone is not a diagnosis of an urban problem. https://unhabitat.org/sites/default/files/2020/11/covid19_spatialplanning_eng1.pdf

Clarified density versus overcrowding throughout the definition, exercise and answer key. Retained worksheet cloze answers. Replaced simple claims that migration causes unemployment or that unemployed people cause crime with a multi-factor account. Historic unemployment percentages should not stand in for an analysis of the current labour market. The existing ILO-based definition and dated-data teaching point remain.
