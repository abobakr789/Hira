# Grade 8 history verification
Checked 24 November 2026. These sources support corrections, not a claim that every textbook statement is independently verified.

- Muhammad Ali's medal story is disputed. Ferris State University's Jim Crow Museum and NBC Sports discuss conflicting accounts. The lesson now identifies the dispute and asks pupils about peaceful responses and checking evidence.
  - https://jimcrowmuseum.ferris.edu/question/2005/august.htm
  - https://www.nbcsports.com/olympics/news/muhammad-ali-olympic-gold-medal-boxing
- The Metropolitan Museum dates Islam's arrival in northern Africa to the middle of the seventh century. The lesson no longer presents 685–715 as its first arrival in Egypt.
  - https://www.metmuseum.org/pt/essays/trade-and-the-spread-of-islam-in-africa
- World History Encyclopedia identifies Mansa Musa with Mali, a reign around 1312–1337 and pilgrimage in 1324. The lesson distinguishes him from the later Songhai Empire and corrects reversed dates in teacher guidance.
  - https://www.worldhistory.org/Mansa_Musa_I/
- Germany's Political Archive, the Berlin General Act and Britannica distinguish conference rules for colonial claims from the later imposition of many borders. No African representatives attended. The teaching material no longer states that the conference itself drew every border.
  - https://archiv.diplo.de/arc-en/the-political-archive/general-act-2684414
  - https://kids.britannica.com/scholars/article/Berlin-Conference/78808
  - https://kids.britannica.com/scholars/article/Scramble-for-Africa/639065
- The UN records Ghana's independence on 6 March 1957. The lesson says many West African countries became independent in 1960, not that independence began then.
  - https://www.un.org/sg/en/content/former-secretary-general/statements/2007-03-06/secretary-generals-message-the-50th-anniversary-of-ghanas-independence
- Nelson Mandela Foundation's prison timeline gives imprisonment from 1962 to 1990. The narrower 1964–1982 period was principally Robben Island.
  - https://www.nelsonmandela.org/prison-timeline
- Britannica records Italy's occupation of Ethiopia in 1936–1941. The exception to long-term European colonial rule is now visibly qualified.
  - https://kids.britannica.com/scholars/article/Italian-East-Africa/43017

The revision also removes the implication that Europeans discovered an uninhabited or politically empty Africa. Textbook extracts are labeled adapted with verified corrections. Original images, audio, video and embedded resources remain unchanged.
