# Grade 9 non-native social studies: financial literacy and archaeological interpretation
Reviewed 24 November 2026.

- CGAP, microcredit and over-indebtedness: borrower protection, variable outcomes, debt distress. https://www.cgap.org/blog/are-we-over-indebting-poor-clients-challenge-for-microcredit
- OECD, responsible business conduct: voluntary initiatives coexist with mandatory due-diligence laws. https://www.oecd.org/en/topics/due-diligence-for-responsible-business-conduct.html
- Abu Dhabi Culture, Umm an-Nar: island settlement dated 2500–2000 BCE, chambered communal tombs and long-distance trade. Island-settlement dates are not silently replaced with broader culture-period boundaries from other sources. https://abudhabiculture.ae/en/cultural-heritage/tangible/archaeology/umm-an-nar
- NYU Abu Dhabi, 2024 Magan boat collaboration: experimental reconstruction with reed bundles, date-palm fibre rope, wooden frames and bitumen waterproofing. Explicitly a modern experiment, not a surviving ancient ship. https://nyuad.nyu.edu/en/news/latest-news/arts-and-culture/2024/july/magan-boat-research-collaboration.html

Teaching qualifications: the budgeting thirds are a classroom example, not a universal prescription. NGO status alone does not establish transparency. Mesopotamia comprised changing city-states and kingdoms rather than one timeless empire. Shared tombs cannot by themselves prove complete social equality. Debt hardship is not proof of irresponsibility.
- Abu Dhabi Culture, Hafit tombs: the earlier Bronze Age Hafit phase preceded the Umm an-Nar phase. https://abudhabiculture.ae/en/cultural-heritage/tangible/unesco-world-heritage-site/hafit-tombs
- Archaeology magazine, differing treatment of two women in Umm an-Nar tombs: social interpretation is more complex than uniform equality. Broader culture chronology around 2700–2000 BCE is distinct from the island settlement dates. https://archaeology.org/issues/online/collection/digs-uae-women-burials/
