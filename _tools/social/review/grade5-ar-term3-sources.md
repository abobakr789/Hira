## Decisions

- Markets: present self-sufficiency, barter and monetary exchange as systems to compare, not a universal mandatory sequence. Electronic commerce is defined by how an order is placed, not by electronic payment alone. Date Global Village's 10.5 million visitors to season 29, ending May 2025. Replace the incorrect 2017 Mall of the World relocation date with the documented 2016 planning decision.
- Natural vegetation: truffles are fungi, not annual plants. Annual plants complete their life cycle in one growing season; perennial plants live for many years. Keep the 1997 award year but remove the unverified month. Natural vegetation can include plants people later protect or irrigate; the definition is about spontaneous origin.
- Shipbuilding: do not equate qarat timber with bamboo. Retain the lesson's named material without inventing a botanical identification. Replace unrelated model answers with shipbuilding-specific answers.
- Ibn Sina: describe the Canon's long use in European university medical education, not every medical school worldwide. Explicitly qualify 980 CE as the textbook date: Stanford's 2025 specialist revision places his birth around 970. Retain the source-based biographical account with that visible caveat.
- Cities: use grid, radial and linear as examples of patterns, without claiming one universal oldest or most common pattern. A city has people, homes, services and varied activities, not residential functions alone. Sharjah Fort dates to 1823, and Julfar/Kush's early settlement is fifth century CE, not BCE.
