# Year 5 Arabic geography and astronomy corrections
Verified 24 November 2026. Two lessons, ten HTML resources. Grade review remains in progress.

## Geography, SS-Y5-T2-L01
- Oman Ministry of Heritage and Tourism: Jabal Shams is Oman's tallest peak, distinct from nearby Jabal Akhdar. https://experienceoman.om/en/destinations/al-dakhiliyah
- Qatar eNature species record: Thamam, Panicum turgidum, is a perennial grass. https://www.enature.qa/specie/thamam/
- Saudipedia: Al Nufud Al Kabir is a sand desert, not a plateau. https://saudipedia.com/en/al-nufud-al-kabir-desert
- Removed the Al Ain oasis as a plateau example. The model answer uses Najd. Replaced an unsupported terrain causal chain with a population factors panel.
- Added an explicitly schematic comparison of mountain, plateau and plain. Repaired inherited nested slide markup and unrelated silver exercises and gold/diamond answer keys.

## Astronomy, SS-Y5-T3-L02
- University of Cambridge Whipple Museum: an astrolabe measures celestial altitude with its alidade and degree scale. It is an astronomical calculating instrument, not a magnetic compass. https://www.whipplemuseum.cam.ac.uk/explore-whipple-collections/astronomy/medieval-astrolabe/parts-astrolabe
- National Museums Scotland: astrolabe functions include stellar altitude and time calculations. https://www.nms.ac.uk/discover-catalogue/a-1000-year-old-star-catcher
- NASA, 21 May 2025: whether the universe is finite or infinite is unknown. The lesson no longer asserts infinity as an established fact. Another NASA search result used infinity without this qualification; the explicit uncertainty statement is the safer instructional treatment. https://www.nasa.gov/science-research/astrophysics/how-big-is-space-we-asked-a-nasa-expert-episode-61/
- University of St Andrews MacTutor: al-Khwarizmi prepared astronomical tables and wrote two works on the astrolabe. Removed the unsupported attribution of a linear instrument invention. https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/Biographies/Al-Khwarizmi/
- UAE Space Agency: established in 2014. Removed the unsupported first-Arab-agency claim. https://space.gov.ae/en/about-us/about-the-agency
- UAE Space Agency: Hope launched on 20 July 2020. https://space.gov.ae/en/about-us/initiatives-and-projects/initiatives-and-projects-listing/emirates-mars-mission-hope-probe
- Added a labelled altitude-angle diagram, a tables/books panel and 2014/2020 timeline. Removed unrelated vocabulary icons, repaired the hook model, matching explanation, exercises and printed keys.
