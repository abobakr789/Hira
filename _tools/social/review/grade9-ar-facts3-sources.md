# Grade 9 natural systems and food-security definitions
24 November 2026. Three lessons, eight HTML. Original media preserved. Local pixels pass for four core/enrichment slides, vocabulary and matching dependencies, and two printed worksheets. Live pending.

- World Bank/UN metadata: net migration is immigrants minus emigrants; rate of natural increase is birth rate minus death rate. Clarified rate versus count and internal redistribution versus national population growth. https://databank.worldbank.org/metadataglossary/world-development-indicators/series/SM.POP.NETM
- USGS deserts: aridity rather than heat defines desert; typical annual rainfall threshold 250mm, not 25mm; vegetation is sparse rather than absent. https://pubs.usgs.gov/gip/deserts/what/
- FAO distinguishes food security from self-sufficiency and explains trade's contribution and risks. https://www.fao.org/4/x7353e/x7353e10.htm
- FAO four dimensions: availability, access, utilization, stability. Original PDF downloaded and text extracted after readable fetch returned encoded content. https://openknowledge.fao.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/479ba332-4529-4c32-89ec-3912cd8b14ee/content
