Checked 24 November 2026, Asia/Dubai. Lessons 2, 4, 8 and 14.

- UN Treaty Collection, UAE accession record. Primary depositary record: accession 3 January 1997, not signature. High confidence. https://treaties.un.org/Pages/showActionDetails.aspx?clang=_en&objid=08000002800026f9
- UAE Legislation, Federal Law No. 3 of 2016, Child Rights (Wadeema). Issued 8 March 2016, effective 15 June 2016. Distinguish issuance from entry into force. High confidence. https://www.uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/1176
- Japan Ministry of Education, 1989 government policy report. Dedicated moral education introduced in 1958; first major revision in 1989. The book incorrectly framed 1989 as introduction. High confidence. https://www.mext.go.jp/b_menu/hakusho/html/hpae198901/hpae198901_2_021.html
- Earth Charter International, History. The 1987 UN commission call preceded the civil-society initiative launched in 1994 and final charter launched June 2000. It is not a UN treaty. High confidence. https://earthcharter.org/about-the-earth-charter/history/
- Pew Research Center, Region: Asia-Pacific, 27 January 2011. Historical estimate of 23.3 million Muslims in China in 2010, not a 2000 census count. High confidence as a dated estimate. https://www.pewresearch.org/religion/2011/01/27/future-of-the-global-muslim-population-regional-asia/
- Pew Research Center, Islam in China, 30 August 2023. Explains ethnicity-proxy and religious-identity differences. Its adult-only estimates must not be directly compared to historical all-age totals. No new current total added. High confidence. https://www.pewresearch.org/religion/2023/08/30/islam/
