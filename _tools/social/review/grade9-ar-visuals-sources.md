# Grade 9 Arabic explanatory panels
24 November 2026. Four panels replace decorative icons. Existing lesson colors, placement and media are retained. No people or religious figures depicted.

- Population identity: births minus deaths plus net international migration. Internal migration redistributes a national total. World Bank net-migration metadata: https://databank.worldbank.org/metadataglossary/world-development-indicators/series/SM.POP.NETM
- Water strategy: 21% demand reduction shown as a hypothetical index 100 to 79, not actual measured water use. Strategy target source: https://u.ae/en/about-the-uae/strategies-initiatives-and-awards/strategies-plans-and-visions/environment-and-energy/the-uae-water-security-strategy-2036
- Food security: four complementary dimensions, not a sequence. FAO: https://openknowledge.fao.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/479ba332-4529-4c32-89ec-3912cd8b14ee/content
- Tax bases: contrast direct/indirect examples while explicitly preserving the UAE no-personal-wage-income-tax context. https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/finance-and-investment/taxation and https://www.oecd.org/en/data/indicators/tax-on-corporate-profits.html and https://www.oecd.org/en/data/indicators/tax-on-property.html
