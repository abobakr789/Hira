# Grade 9 Arabic social studies: energy, taxation and Expo
Reviewed 24 November 2026. Energy/tax definitions and dependent questions corrected, not merely the main explanation.

- UAE government, nuclear energy: four Barakah units, 5.6 GW, all commercially operational by September 2024; first Arab-world nuclear power plant, not the world's largest. https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/environment-and-energy/water-and-energy/types-of-energy-sources/nuclear-energy
- EIA, oil timeline: petroleum use since antiquity. https://www.eia.gov/kids/history-of-energy/timelines/oil-petroleum.php
- US Department of Energy, hydropower history: water wheels long predate steam engines. https://www.energy.gov/cmei/water/history-hydropower
- EIA, petroleum explained: oil is a hydrocarbon mixture formed with heat and pressure over millions of years. https://www.eia.gov/energyexplained/oil-and-petroleum-products/
- UAE government, taxation: individuals do not pay personal income tax, while VAT, excise and corporate tax have different bases. https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/finance-and-investment/taxation
- OECD, corporate income and property taxes: direct tax examples are broader than payroll deductions. https://www.oecd.org/en/data/indicators/tax-on-corporate-profits.html and https://www.oecd.org/en/data/indicators/tax-on-property.html
- IMF quarterly government-finance guide: revenue and borrowing are separate accounting categories. https://www.imf.org/external/pubs/ft/gfs/guide/0213.pdf
- BIE, World Expos: five-year cadence and up to six months apply to World Expos, not every Expo category. https://www.bie-paris.org/site/en/about-world-expos
- BIE and DEWA Expo logo accounts support the four-thousand-year civilisation wording; no unsupported replacement with a conflicting artefact date. https://bureauinternationaldesexpositions.bie-paris.org/site/en/news-announcements/bie-activity/expo-2020-dubai-unveils-new-logo and https://freejna.dewa.gov.ae/expo2020/logo-story.html

Removed undated current oil-share claim and unverified job projections presented as realised Expo outcomes. Assessment now tests the difference between predictions and measured results. Historic 2018 energy statistics remain explicitly dated.
