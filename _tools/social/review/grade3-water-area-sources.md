## Verified relationships
- Groundwater is stored in pores and cracks in soil and rock. The water table is the upper surface of the saturated zone, not a large underground pool. USGS Water Science School, fetched 24 November 2026: https://www.usgs.gov/water-science-school/science/aquifers-and-groundwater
- Freshwater means low dissolved solids, not automatic drinking safety. USGS freshwater definition, fetched 24 November 2026: https://www.usgs.gov/water-science-school/science/freshwater-lakes-and-rivers-and-water-cycle
- A square kilometre is a square one kilometre on each side. NIST, fetched 24 November 2026: https://www.nist.gov/pml/owm/metric-si/si-units-area
- Calculated exactly: 1,000 m times 1,000 m equals 1,000,000 square metres. Replaced the unsupported comparison with ten football fields with this exact description, rather than substitute another field-count estimate.
