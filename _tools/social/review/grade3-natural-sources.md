# Year 3 natural resources review

Reviewed 24 November 2026. Original full readings and media retained. Derived activities corrected and visible notes added where the reading overgeneralizes.

- Royal Society of Chemistry, lithium element profile: lithium is an element, found in minerals and brines; battery use. https://periodic-table.rsc.org/element/3/lithium
- US Energy Information Administration, sources of energy: renewable energy is naturally replenished, not a fixed few years for all resources. https://www.eia.gov/energyexplained/what-is-energy/sources-of-energy.php
- US Department of Energy, wind advantages and challenges: no fuel combustion in generation, but noise, wildlife and other effects remain. https://www.energy.gov/eere/wind/advantages-and-challenges-wind-energy
- FAO irrigation guidance: efficient irrigation reduces loss, not zero loss. https://www.fao.org/4/y4263e/y4263e08.htm

The harvest output in the reading is explicitly limited to that farm. Fish consumption is not determined by distance from water alone. These qualifications avoid treating a specific example or broad generalisation as a universal rule.

Literal teaching diagrams show wind turbine, solar panel, irrigation near roots and stacked growing shelves. No external media removed or replaced. Identity, slide framework and colours retained.
