# Grade 8 Arabic history and attribution corrections

24 November 2026. Sheikh Zayed became ruler of Abu Dhabi in 1966, not 1977. Dante was born in 1265, not 1256. Renaissance is French, not Latin. The standard introductory Renaissance period is fourteenth to sixteenth century, although periodization varies. Replace the claim that every medieval church institution opposed all learning with a distinction between conflict and educational work; Gelati is a documented counterexample. Arab and Muslim scholars developed geographical science and instruments, but latitude/longitude and the astrolabe predate them and the compass has Chinese origins. Remove the unsupported continent-wide map count from the dependent activity and quiz. Correct Calcutta to Calicut.
