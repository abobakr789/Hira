# Grade 2 Social Studies accuracy review

Reviewed 24 November 2026 (Asia/Dubai). Scope: 13 lessons, 29 HTML resources, 42 slides and 19 print pages. Existing source passages, quotations and media were preserved. Where textbook wording conflicts with present evidence, visible clarification distinguishes the original passage from the supported interpretation. Unsupported generated rankings and absolute claims were removed rather than replaced with another ranking.

## Sources and decisions

- UAE Government fact sheet, official: https://u.ae/en/about-the-uae/fact-sheet . T1L07 distinguishes the UAE's land borders with Saudi Arabia and Oman from Qatar's proximity across the Gulf.
- WAM, official announcement: https://www.wam.ae/en/article/140t1kp-celebration-historic-signing-declaration-the-union . T1L13 separates signing the declaration and constitution on 18 July 1971, formation of the state on 2 December 1971, and designation of Union Pledge Day in 2024.
- WHO drinking-water fact sheet, dated 19 November 2026: https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/drinking-water . T2L02 gives the dated 2024 estimate of 2.1 billion without safely managed drinking water and explains that definition, rather than calling all these people without any clean water.
- US Department of Energy, official: https://www.energy.gov/cmei/systems/articles/how-wind-can-help-us-breathe-easier . T2L05 replaces zero-pollution claims with lower emissions.
- US Department of Energy, official: https://www.energy.gov/cmei/systems/renewable-systems-integration and https://www.energy.gov/cmei/systems/solar-integration-solar-energy-and-storage-basics . T2L05 separates renewable resources from variable electricity output and removes an unsupported 100 percent smart-city claim.
- UAE Government, official: https://u.ae/en/about-the-uae/the-seven-emirates/abu-dhabi . T2L11 distinguishes Abu Dhabi city, the capital, from the emirate's area.
- UAE Government, official: https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/visa-and-emirates-id/emirates-id . T2L13 explains that Emirates ID applies to citizens and residents, not citizenship alone.
- Dubai RTA, operator: https://www.rta.ae/wps/portal/rta/ae/public-transport/metro/about-metro . T3L02 describes driverless service without an unverified current global length rank.
- UAE Ministry of Culture heritage register, official: https://moc.gov.ae/en/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D8%AC%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%88%D8%B7%D9%86%D9%8A/al-bannay-traditional-builder/ . T3L11 clarifies that stone and mud houses existed before the Union. Post-Union development concerns expansion of modern housing and services.
- Ras Al Khaimah heritage resource: https://rakheritage.rak.ae/chapters/the-qawassim/ and independent reporting: https://www.thenationalnews.com/uae/heritage/how-ras-al-khaimah-got-its-name-1.861834 . T3L14 labels the tent-and-lantern naming story as a traditional account, not settled history. Objectives, activities, answers and assessment all retain that distinction.
- NOAA NCEI, official scientific guidance: https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/magnetic-declination . T3L15 specifies the north-seeking end of a compass needle and magnetic north, with interference caveat. The unverified compass-invention attribution in generated model content is replaced by navigation writings. The prior Ibn Majid source clarification remains intact.
- Emirates Post, official historical stamp account: https://www.emiratespostshop.ae/shop/history-of-civil-aviation-in-abudhabi/ . T3L18 distinguishes Al Bateen construction beginning September 1966 from first aviation in Abu Dhabi; runway completion was 1967. Original textbook narrative remains, with visible clarification. Generated summaries and exercises use construction wording.

T3L04 removes undated 100 percent food self-sufficiency figures. T3L05 removes a world food-consumption ranking whose metric and date were not established. These removals do not introduce a competing numerical claim.
