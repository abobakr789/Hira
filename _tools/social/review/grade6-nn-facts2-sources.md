# Grade 6 English factual review, second batch

Verified 24 November 2026. Six lessons, 16 HTML files, 18 changed slide views and 14 print pages. Textbook quotations retained with visible source-check notes. Supporting activities no longer repeat unsupported historical allegations as established fact.

## Health
- L06: WHO explains childhood obesity's biological, behavioural, social and environmental causes. Avoid blaming pupils or families. https://www.who.int/news-room/questions-and-answers/item/noncommunicable-diseases-childhood-overweight-and-obesity
- L06: CDC gives 9-12 hours per 24 hours for ages 6-12, and 8-10 for ages 13-18. The book's combined age band is preserved as a quotation but qualified, including the printed and popup answer keys. https://www.cdc.gov/physical-activity-education/staying-healthy/sleep.html
- L06: Healthy habits do not guarantee freedom from all disease. NIDDK explains type 1 diabetes as autoimmune disease. https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/diabetes/overview/what-is-diabetes/type-1-diabetes
- L08: NHS advises medical help for unusual breathlessness and emergency help for severe breathing difficulty. The classroom guidance tells pupils to involve a trusted adult, without diagnosing the fictional child. No country-specific telephone number has been imported. https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/shortness-of-breath/

## History and geography
- L11: Britannica dates Attila's rule to 434-453, contradicting the book's within-a-decade-after-410 wording. https://kids.britannica.com/students/article/Attila/273003
- L12: Dumbarton Oaks distinguishes the 1014 victory from the conquest of Bulgaria by 1018, and describes specific eastern territorial acquisitions. No claim that all modern Armenia and Georgia were annexed is added. https://www.doaks.org/resources/online-exhibits/gods-regents-on-earth-a-thousand-years-of-byzantine-imperial-seals/rulers-of-byzantium/basil-ii-976-1025
- L13: World History Encyclopedia describes Venetian naval support against the Normans, including their defeat in 1084. The book's broken-promise premise is flagged and the classroom debate uses a hypothetical instead. https://www.worldhistory.org/Alexios%5FI%5FKomnenos/
- L13: Venezia Unica describes the city's canals as routes for boats. https://tripplanner.veneziaunica.it/en/content/vaporetto
- L13: The book's exclusive-European-trading-partner claim is qualified rather than taught as verified. The DOI page for a relevant 2016 study did not return readable content. A University of Konstanz source edition supplies broader context but does not settle every date, commodity and privilege in the textbook's claim. https://ojs.ub.uni-konstanz.de/transmed/index.php/tmh/en/article/download/70/162/174
- L15: Smith College gives a 5-12% estimate for northern Europe during the Great Famine; this is not a uniform percentage for all Europe. https://www.science.smith.edu/climatelit/the-great-famine/
- L15: Britannica gives a rough estimate of 25 million European Black Death deaths. The book's 200-million figure must not be equated with a third of Europe's population. https://kids.britannica.com/students/article/Black-Death/574643
- L15: Research ties the Great Famine to torrential rain, saturation, floods and crop failure, with social pressures also relevant. https://www.nature.com/articles/s43247-020-00016-3
