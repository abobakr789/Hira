# Grade 9 Arabic social studies: mountain heights and geographic extent
Reviewed 24 November 2026.

- Moroccan National Tourist Office: Toubkal 4,167 m. https://www.visitmorocco.com/en/discover-morocco/geography-morocco
- Kurdistan official tourism and Erbil governorate: Halgurd 3,607 m, not 3,067 m. https://bot.gov.krd/erbil-province/parks-attractions/halgurd-mountain and https://www.erbil.gov.krd/app/en/geography
- SOFF/Comoros country hydromet diagnostic, October 2024: Comoros lies around 11–13 degrees south. The textbook's two-degree-south limit cannot include all Arab countries. https://www.un-soff.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Rapport_CHD_Comores.pdf

Geographical continuity does not mean an absence of natural obstacles: retain the regional connection while acknowledging mountains, seas and deserts. All height occurrences, quick answers and worksheets were aligned. The original cloze bank and answer positions are preserved.
