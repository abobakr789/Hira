# Grade 5: final seven Arabic term-one lessons

Reviewed 24 November 2026. Lessons SS-Y5-T1-L01, L05, L07, L12, L13, L16 and L18.

## Verified sources

- UAE Government, Constitution of the UAE, https://u.ae/about-the-uae/the-constitution-of-the-uae . Primary official source. Temporary constitution in 1971, permanent in 1996; Article 45 names five federal authorities. Used for L13 learning and enrichment corrections. Rights are not presented as contingent on performance of duties.
- GCC Secretariat, first session and foundation archive, https://www.gcc-sg.org/ar/JointGulf/Projects/ArabYouthCenter/Pages/default.aspx . Primary official source. First summit and signature in Abu Dhabi on 25 May 1981; coordination, integration and links between peoples.
- GCC Secretariat, 25th summit final statement, https://www.gcc-sg.org/ar/AboutUs/Data/SupremeData/Pages/TheClosingStatementoftheTwenty24.aspx . Primary official source. Zayed summit held 20-21 December 2004; final statement 21 December. Original lesson date kept as the closing date, not a claim that the summit lasted one day.
- Dubai Zaman, founding history, https://www.dubaizaman.ae/content/dubaizaman/ar-ae/programs/159/31679.html . Government broadcaster historical account. Supports the 1976 bilateral meeting. Its opening paragraph contradicts its later account by naming Riyadh for the 1981 foundation. Rejected that location claim in favor of the GCC primary archive naming Abu Dhabi.
- Ministry of Culture, Folk Tales (Khararif), https://moc.gov.ae/en/element_type/folk-tales-khararif/ . Official cultural source for oral transmission, local language and heritage in L07.
- British Museum, Gulf-type stamp seal, https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/W_1932-1008-178 . Primary collection record: grey steatite, seal use, administrative technology and trade links. L05 now distinguishes the stone seal from its clay impression across learning, vocabulary and printable concept card.
- UNESCO, Qal'at al-Bahrain, https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1192/ . Primary heritage listing for Dilmun trade and settlement context.
- Al Bayan, Salem Suhail Khamis, 20 August 2015, https://www.albayan.ae/across-the-uae/news-and-reports/2015-08-20-1.2440635 . Historical reporting quoting Watani Al Emarat's director. Supports joining Ras Al Khaimah police aged 16 and death on 30 November 1971. L16 uses first Emirati martyr, not first soldier.
- UAE Cabinet, Martyrs' Families Office, 8 September 2015, https://uaecabinet.ae/en/news/mohammad-bin-zayed-orders-setting-up-of-an-office-for-martyrs-families . Primary official announcement. L18 visibly labels the office example as historical (2015), avoiding a current-organizational-name claim.
