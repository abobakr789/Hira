# Year 5 Arabic ancient-history corrections
Verified 24 November 2026. Four lessons, seventeen HTML resources. Grade review remains open.

## Evidence and decisions
- Metropolitan Museum of Art, 22 December 2014, Alphabet Origins: early alphabetic writing predates the later Phoenician system; West Semitic speakers adapted Egyptian signs. Removed claims that Phoenician directly originated most world languages. Writing systems and spoken languages are distinguished. https://www.metmuseum.org/exhibitions/listings/2014/assyria-to-iberia/blog/posts/alphabet
- Metropolitan Museum of Art, 1 October 2004: Phoenician maritime trade spread a writing system subsequently adopted by Greeks. https://www.metmuseum.org/essays/the-phoenicians-1500-300-b-c
- Britannica: Phoenician developed from North Semitic, had 22 consonant letters, and spread by traders. https://kids.britannica.com/scholars/article/Phoenician-alphabet/59754
- Britannica: North Semitic relationship to Canaanite and Sinaitic inscriptions. https://kids.britannica.com/scholars/article/North-Semitic-alphabet/56220
- British Museum, 11 October 2022: hieroglyphic writing combines phonetic and nonphonetic signs. Corrected language/script terminology and the picture-only account. https://www.britishmuseum.org/blog/eureka-finding-key-ancient-egypt
- British Museum, 21 January 2021: cuneiform began before 3200 BCE. Replaced an exact 3000 date with beginnings around 3200 and qualified the absolute oldest-civilisation claim. Older museum summaries use around 3000; precision is not implied. https://www.britishmuseum.org/blog/how-write-cuneiform
- UAE National Cultural Map: Saruq Al Hadid has Bronze and Iron Age material, discovered in 2002, and was an important Iron Age industrial centre. It should not be attributed automatically to Magan. https://culturalmap.gov.ae/en/facilities/saruq-al-hadid-archaeological-site
- Department of Culture and Tourism Abu Dhabi, The Bronze Age Sites of Hili: local towers are documented, but their exact function is uncertain; imported ceramics and beads support external contact. Replaced the unsupported Mesopotamian-tower similarity argument with imported-pottery evidence. https://abudhabiculture.ae/-/media/abudhabiculture/images/2026/publications/the-bronze-age-sites-of-hili.pdf?rev=32ee95821d86454f80307ad9e9738cda
- The National, 4 July 2022, reporting archaeological research: copper from southeast Arabia was exchanged for Mesopotamian ceramic vessels and other goods. Corrected the reversed return-copper claim. https://www.thenationalnews.com/uae/heritage/2022/07/04/new-artefacts-offer-glimpse-into-uae-life-more-than-4000-years-ago/
