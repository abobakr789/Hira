Verified 24 November 2026. Existing Hira layout, colors and original media preserved. These are teaching diagrams, not photographs or maps of actual terrain.

- NOAA, What are latitude and longitude? Education reference. Parallels locate north/south of equator; meridians east/west of zero longitude. Hypothetical coordinate grid not a geographic boundary map. https://oceanservice.noaa.gov/education/tutorial_nautical_charts/nautical_charts02_information_2b.html
- National Geographic, Plateau. Geographic education reference: relatively flat elevated surface relative to surrounding land. https://education.nationalgeographic.org/resource/plateau/
- National Geographic, Plain. Broad relatively flat terrain, not defined by a universal altitude ceiling. Corrected low-altitude generalization in L02 deck, worksheet and source card. https://education.nationalgeographic.org/resource/plain/
- UAE government, Fact sheet. Flag arrangement: red hoist, horizontal green/white/black. Literal flag rather than a generic flag icon. No claim to reproduce official production color tolerances. https://u.ae/en/about-the-uae/fact-sheet
- UAE government, Founders of the Union. 18 February 1968 bilateral accord, 2 December 1971 six-emirate federation, 10 February 1972 Ras Al Khaimah accession. Timeline is a selection, not all meetings. https://u.ae/en/about-uae/founders-of-the-union
