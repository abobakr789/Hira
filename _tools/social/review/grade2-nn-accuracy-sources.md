# Grade 2 English-track accuracy and safety review

Reviewed 24 November 2026, Asia/Dubai. Nine lessons, 21 HTML resources, 25 slides and 13 print pages. Original source passages and media remain. Scientific disagreements in book passages have visible notes, while generated teaching content is corrected directly.

## Sources and decisions

- Environment Agency - Abu Dhabi, official biodiversity entry: https://www.ead.gov.ae/en/discover-our-biodiversity/mammals/dugong . L12 distinguishes the global IUCN Vulnerable category from the textbook's loose use of endangered. The animal is an air-breathing marine mammal that eats seagrass. Incorrect seal icons removed. A literal dugong photo replaces the misleading image, from the same official entry: https://www.ead.gov.ae/-/media/Project/EAD/EAD/Biodiversity/NewImages/Mammals/Dugong-resized.jpg . Photo saved locally as dugong-ead.webp. Existing original media were not removed or changed.
- GIA, gemological educational source: https://www.gia.edu/gia-news-research/differences-between-pearl-types . L13 notes that natural pearls form around material such as a shell fragment or parasite, not normally a grain of sand. Natural pearls were prized by royalty and other wealthy people, not exclusively kings or queens. The book passage remains clearly separate from the correction.
- San Diego Zoo, zoological educational source: https://animals.sandiegozoo.org/animals/camel . L15 notes that camel humps store fat, not water. Correct Arabian oryx photo reused from the existing lesson asset, replacing an unrelated superhero icon. Hospitality is distinguished from physical desert resources; Bedouins are not defined as existing only in the past.
- FAO, technical agricultural publication: https://www.fao.org/4/t0681E/t0681e03.htm . L16 retains the book's green Khalal and yellow Bessar naming for book-reference exercises, but visibly explains the Kimri/Khalal or Bisr naming used by other sources. Rutab is soft; Tamr has lower moisture. No wholesale relabelling of quoted book text.
- UNESCO World Heritage Centre, official heritage entry: https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1207 . L16 broadens falaj from a channel only from wells to a channel carrying water to farms and settlements. Underground water, springs and gravity-fed channels are covered by the source.
- UAE Government, official: https://u.ae/en/about-the-uae/human-rights-in-the-uae/fostering-tolerance-and-co-existence . L17 uses more than 200 nationalities, not countries. Book passages have visible clarification; model answers, quizzes, parent note and generated concept card use nationalities directly.
- FEI, official event announcement dated 29 July 2026: https://inside.fei.org/media-updates/butheeb-uae-selected-replacement-host-fei-endurance-world-championship-2026 . L18 current UAE-hosted championship context is supported for 2026, not treated as a permanent host claim. Racing-camel wording is restricted to camel racing, rather than implying that all present-day camel riding uses robots. Unrelated robot proxy icon removed.

## Other lesson-specific decisions

- L01 true/false wording specifies the sheep's initial response, since their response changes within the story.
- L08 group line-up uses eyes-open silent hand signals, not eyes-closed movement. Story text remains, with a clear child-safety rule about observing bees from a safe distance with a trusted adult and not touching hives or tasting honey from a hive.
- L09 teamwork model describes keeping to the plan as helpful, not a guarantee of winning.
- Misleading cactus proxy removed from touched oasis vocabulary views. No new decorative generic visual was added.

Model answers were also inspected in their revealed state, and changed vocabulary definitions on flipped card backs.
