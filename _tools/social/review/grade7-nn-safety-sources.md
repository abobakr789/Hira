# Grade 7 English: health and emergency safety verification
Checked 24 November 2026, Asia/Dubai. Lessons 3, 7 and 8. Original media unchanged. This is classroom discussion, not first-aid training.

## Source ledger
- WHO, Poliomyelitis, 2 April 2025. Official global health fact sheet. Polio can cause irreversible paralysis; no cure; vaccine prevention. Used to remove the misleading untreated/cured framing from Zhao's story. High confidence. https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/poliomyelitis
- St John Ambulance, Choking, clinically reviewed 28 April 2025. Clinical first-aid guidance. Assess effective cough first; back blows followed by abdominal thrusts for appropriate conscious casualties; repeat the cycle and summon emergency help. The previous book sequence assessed after intervention and omitted back blows in repeats. High confidence. https://www.sja.org.uk/first-aid-advice/choking/
- NHS, How to stop a child from choking. National health guidance. Infants under 1 year use chest thrusts rather than abdominal thrusts. Effective coughing should be encouraged; no blind finger sweep. High confidence. https://www.nhs.uk/baby/first-aid-and-safety/first-aid/how-to-stop-a-child-from-choking/
- St John Ambulance, Recovery position, clinically reviewed 28 April 2025. Recovery position is for an unresponsive person breathing normally; monitor. High confidence. https://www.sja.org.uk/first-aid-advice/recovery-position/
- St John Ambulance, How to do CPR, clinically reviewed 18 September 2026. Clinical guidance. Scene safety, response, early emergency call, airway and normal breathing check up to 10 seconds; gasps are not normal breathing; CPR for an unresponsive person not breathing normally. High confidence. https://www.sja.org.uk/first-aid-advice/cpr/
- NHS inform, First aid, updated 25 March 2026. Government health guidance. Abdominal thrusts unsuitable for infants and pregnancy; normal breathing determines recovery position versus CPR. High confidence. https://www.nhsinform.scot/tests-and-treatments/emergencies/first-aid/
- London Fire Brigade, Houses and converted flats escape plan. Fire-service operational guidance. Get out, stay out, call when safe; do not fight fires; hot doors stay closed; keep low in smoke; trapped people call and signal. Used for the home-fire scenario, not a blanket rule overriding a building's specific evacuation plan. High confidence. https://www.london-fire.gov.uk/safety/the-home/escape-plan/escape-plan-houses-and-converted-flats/
- UAE Government, Handling emergencies. Official local emergency numbers and fire guidance. Ambulance 998; civil defence 997; police 999. No lifts or returning for belongings. Local numbers replace UK source numbers. High confidence. https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/justice-safety-and-the-law/Safety/handling-emergencies

## Editorial decisions
Safety-adapted readings are labelled visibly and are not represented as verbatim original text. Matching prompts, model answers, quiz choices, worksheets and concept cards are reconciled with the revised readings. Practical first aid requires a qualified instructor. No classroom blows, thrusts or CPR on pupils. The source book's full content remains available in the repository source materials; unsafe procedural summaries are not repeated as instructions in these lesson resources.
