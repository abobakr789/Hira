# Grade 7 English factual updates, first batch
Checked 24 November 2026, Asia/Dubai. Lessons 1, 6, 11, 12 and 13. Original media unchanged.

- UAE Government, Ministry of Education Strategic Plan 2017–2021, dated 30 December 2024. Official archived strategy. The plan refers to 2017–2021, not a recent/current plan. High confidence. https://u.ae/en/about-the-uae/strategies-initiatives-and-awards/strategies-plans-and-visions/strategies-plans-and-visions-untill-2021/ministry-of-education-strategic-plan-2017-2021
- UNEP, UN declares war on ocean plastic, 23 February 2017. Official dated press release. Supports the old 8 million tonnes estimate and conditional 2050 plastic/fish projection; neither is a current measurement or certain outcome. High confidence for source and date, projection explicitly qualified. https://www.unep.org/en/news-and-stories/press-release/un-declares-war-ocean-plastic
- UNEP World Water Quality Alliance, Riverine plastic pollution, updated 26 April 2023. Technical review. Estimates vary by method and scope. Ocean-only inputs cannot be replaced without qualification by estimates covering all aquatic ecosystems. High confidence. https://www.unep.org/interactives/wwqa/technical-highlights/riverine-plastic-pollution
- UNESCO World Heritage Centre, The Great Wall. Heritage register. Earlier walls joined around 220 BCE; building continued to Ming dynasty; system across periods exceeds 20,000 km. High confidence for these claims. Other legacy prose on this page was not used. https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/438/
- UNESCO World Heritage Centre, The Grand Canal. Heritage register. Sections from 5th century BCE, unified in Sui period, over 2,000 km by 13th century; links Beijing and Zhejiang. High confidence. https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1443/
- Imperial War Museums, War against Japan. Museum history. Full-scale China-Japan conflict in July 1937, Pearl Harbor 7 December 1941, US entry 8 December. Corrects a single worldwide start-date framing and incomplete Allied roster. High confidence. https://www.iwm.org.uk/history/second-world-war/war-against-japan
- US National Archives, Hawaii Statehood, reviewed 10 March 2023. Primary-record exhibition. Statehood 21 August 1959; Hawaii was a territory during World War II. High confidence. https://www.archives.gov/legislative/features/hawaii
- Britannica Kids, Confucius. Reference biography. 551–479 BCE, not 497 BCE. High confidence. https://kids.britannica.com/kids/article/Confucius/352992
- Britannica Students, Laozi. Reference biography. Life is uncertain and traditional stories largely legendary; 604 BCE is not a firm historical date. High confidence for uncertainty. https://kids.britannica.com/students/article/Laozi/275375
- Needham Research Institute, Joseph Needham. Institutional biography. Trained in biochemistry and worked in chemical embryology. Science and Civilisation in China began publication in 1954; seven volumes divided into parts. High confidence. https://www.nri.cam.ac.uk/joseph-needham/
- Columbia University, Asia for Educators, Song Dynasty printing. University teaching resource. Woodblock prints whole pages; Bi Sheng used baked-clay characters for 11th-century movable type; later wood/metal developments are separate. High confidence. https://afe.easia.columbia.edu/songdynasty-module/tech-printing.html
