## Confidence and limits

Geography and modern eligibility corrections have high confidence from official sources. Historical accounts are presented as attributed accounts, not experimentally verified facts. The birthplace of Ibn Majid and precise dates or duration of Ibn Firnas's flight were not replaced with one disputed version. Existing media were not changed. All changed slides and print pages receive local and deployed visual inspection, recorded in the accompanying JSON manifest.
