(a) The conversation in the cave of Hira

Spec 1.2 a

⏱ 8 min

In Ramadan, when he was about 40, the Prophet ﷺ was alone in the cave of Hira. The angel came to him (Bukhari 3, from A'ishah).

1️⃣ «Read!»

The angel said «Read!». He replied, «I am not a reader.»

2️⃣ Pressed three times

The angel held him tightly until he was exhausted, then repeated the command — three times.

3️⃣ The first verses

Then the angel recited: «Read in the name of your Lord who created…» (96:1–5).

4️⃣ Home to Khadijah

He returned trembling: «Cover me!». Khadijah comforted him and took him to her cousin Waraqah ibn Nawfal, who said it was the same angel who came to Musa.

(b) Why the revelation matters

Spec 1.2 a · b

🗣️ Word of Allah

Muslims believe the Qur'an is Allah's own words, revealed over about 23 years.

🧭 Guidance

🛡️ Preserved

🌙 Laylat al-Qadr

Muslims seek this night in the last ten nights of Ramadan: «better than a thousand months».

(c) How the Qur'an was compiled

Spec 1.2 c

⏱ 8 min

✍️ In his lifetime

Verses were memorised by many companions and written on materials such as palm leaves, bones and parchment, as the Prophet ﷺ directed.

📚 Abu Bakr

After many huffaz were killed at the Battle of Yamamah, Umar urged Abu Bakr to collect the Qur'an; Zayd ibn Thabit gathered it into one collection (Bukhari).

📖 Uthman

When people recited differently in new lands, Uthman had standard copies made from Abu Bakr's collection and sent them to the main cities (Bukhari).

✅ Why it matters

One agreed text for all Muslims — the Qur'an recited today is the same text.

We do: a (c) Explain question (6 marks)

Point → develop → link

⏱ 8 min

(c) Explain why the first complete copy of the Qur'an was made. (6) — June 2023

Point 1 + why

Many companions who had memorised the Qur'an were killed at the Battle of Yamamah. This was a danger because the Qur'an was mainly preserved in people's memories, so parts could be lost.

Point 2 + why

The verses were written on scattered materials such as palm leaves and bones. Collecting them in one place, checked by Zayd ibn Thabit, protected them from being lost or changed.

Point 3 + why

One agreed text meant that all Muslims could follow the same Qur'an. This kept the community united, and Uthman later used this collection to send standard copies to every region.

Plan a (d) answer: June 2024, Q2(d)

12 marks · four elements

⏱ 6 min

«The Night of Power is the most important event in Islam.» Assess this statement. Refer to Qur'an 97:1–5.

✅ Agree (with the text)

❌ Another point of view

97:1: the Qur'an — the foundation of Islam — was sent down on this night.

The Hijrah allowed Islam to become a community and a state; it starts the Islamic calendar.

97:3: it is «better than a thousand months»; the revelation began the Prophet's ﷺ mission.

Other events also shaped Islam, e.g. the Mi'raj when salah was made obligatory.

⚖️ Conclusion: Without the Night of Power there would be no Qur'an, so it is the foundation; but other events such as the Hijrah were needed for Islam to grow.

Model (d) answer · Level 3

Read the tags

⏱ 6 min

Text + agree

Many Muslims agree. Qur'an 97:1 says «We sent it down during the Night of Decree», so the Qur'an, the foundation of Islam, began on this night. Verse 3 says it is «better than a thousand months», showing how highly Allah values it.

Reason

It also began the Prophet's ﷺ mission: from the cave of Hira came 23 years of revelation. Without it there would be no Qur'an, no Sunnah and no Shari'ah.

Other view

However, others argue that the Hijrah was more important. In Madinah Islam became a community and a state, and Umar chose it as the start of the Islamic calendar for this reason.

Reason

Some would also point to the Mi'raj, when the five daily prayers were made obligatory, as the event that shapes Muslims' daily lives most.

Conclusion

Overall, the Night of Power is the most important event because everything else depends on the revelation it began. The Hijrah and the Mi'raj were essential too, but they built on the message first revealed that night.
