Sources & concepts: Features of Planning in the Prophet's ﷺ Life
Islamic Education · Grade 11 · Non-Arabic speakers · Unit Two · Textbook V1
For all
Objective: To explain the Prophet's ﷺ planning method and extract its features from examples in the Seerah.
📖 Read the text
وَأَعِدُّواْ لَهُم مَّا ٱسۡتَطَعۡتُم مِّن قُوَّةٖ وَمِن رِّبَاطِ ٱلۡخَيۡلِ تُرۡهِبُونَ بِهِۦ عَدُوَّ ٱللَّهِ وَعَدُوَّكُمۡ وَءَاخَرِينَ مِن دُونِهِمۡ لَا تَعۡلَمُونَهُمُ ٱللَّهُ يَعۡلَمُهُمۡۚ وَمَا تُنفِقُواْ مِن شَيۡءٖ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ يُوَفَّ إِلَيۡكُمۡ وَأَنتُمۡ لَا تُظۡلَمُونَ ﴿60﴾
[Al-Anfal: 60–60]
📘 Key words
Takhtitplanning
Mu'akhahbrotherhood
💡 Main ideas
1. Planning: setting clear goals and suitable means within a time frame, while relying on Allah.
2. In the Hijrah: choosing the companion, guide, route and timing, and giving roles to Abu Bakr, 'Ali and Asma'.
3. In Madinah: building the mosque, brotherhood between Muhajirun and Ansar, the Charter of Madinah and organising relations.
4. The UAE: the UAE Centennial 2071 and strategic plans, because success is the fruit of planning.
✅ Lessons we take
1. Plan, then trust Allah.
2. Choose the most qualified people.
🇦🇪 National identity: The UAE plans for the future through the UAE Centennial 2071.
✉️ Lesson message: I plan for success ️
