Sources & concepts: Lessons to Be Learned: Surat Al-Mulk (25–30)
Islamic Education · Grade 6 · Non-Arabic speakers · Say, Indeed the Guidance of Allah Is the Only Guidance · Textbook V2 p.174
For all
Objective: To explain from Surat Al-Mulk (25–30) the ruling on praying for harm against others and infer the link between faith and trust in Allah.
📖 Read the text
وَيَقُولُونَ مَتَىٰ هَٰذَا ٱلۡوَعۡدُ إِن كُنتُمۡ صَٰدِقِينَ ﴿25﴾ قُلۡ إِنَّمَا ٱلۡعِلۡمُ عِندَ ٱللَّهِ وَإِنَّمَآ أَنَا۠ نَذِيرٞ مُّبِينٞ ﴿26﴾ فَلَمَّا رَأَوۡهُ زُلۡفَةٗ سِيٓـَٔتۡ وُجُوهُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ وَقِيلَ هَٰذَا ٱلَّذِي كُنتُم بِهِۦ تَدَّعُونَ ﴿27﴾ قُلۡ أَرَءَيۡتُمۡ إِنۡ أَهۡلَكَنِيَ ٱللَّهُ وَمَن مَّعِيَ أَوۡ رَحِمَنَا فَمَن يُجِيرُ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ مِنۡ عَذَابٍ أَلِيمٖ ﴿28﴾ قُلۡ هُوَ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنُ ءَامَنَّا بِهِۦ وَعَلَيۡهِ تَوَكَّلۡنَاۖ فَسَتَعۡلَمُونَ مَنۡ هُوَ فِي ضَلَٰلٖ مُّبِينٖ ﴿29﴾ قُلۡ أَرَءَيۡتُمۡ إِنۡ أَصۡبَحَ مَآؤُكُمۡ غَوۡرٗا فَمَن يَأۡتِيكُم بِمَآءٖ مَّعِينِۭ ﴿30﴾
[Al-Mulk: 25–30]
📘 Key words
زلفة (Zulfah)near
Ghawransunk deep
معين (Ma'in)flowing
💡 Main ideas
1. The disbelievers ask «When is this promise?» Knowledge of it is with Allah; the Prophet ﷺ is a clear warner.
2. When they see it near, their faces will turn grim.
3. «We believe in the Most Merciful and in Him we trust; you will know who is clearly astray.»
4. «Have you thought: if your water sank into the ground, who would bring you flowing water?»
✅ Lessons we take
1. Water is a blessing.
🇦🇪 National identity: «If your water sank away» reminds us of water's value; the UAE works on water security and conservation.
✉️ Lesson message: I trust in the Most Merciful
