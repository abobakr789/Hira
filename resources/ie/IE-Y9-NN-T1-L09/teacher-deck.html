Key learning points

Building understanding

1

Hajj was made obligatory in 9 AH, once in a lifetime for those able: «Pilgrimage to the House is a duty owed to Allah by those able to find a way.»

2

Its pillars: ihram, standing at 'Arafah, tawaf al-ifadah and sa'y. «Hajj is 'Arafah.»

3

Types: ifrad (hajj alone), qiran ('umrah and hajj together) and tamattu' ('umrah then hajj in the hajj months).

4

The Prophet's ﷺ hajj: Mina on the 8th, then 'Arafah, then Muzdalifah, then stoning Jamrat al-'Aqabah, sacrifice, shaving and tawaf al-ifadah, then the days of Tashriq in Mina.

Hiraa quick challenge

5 questions

1

The types of ihram for Hajj are:

a) ifrad, tamattu' and qiran · b) fard, nafl and sunnah · c) big and small · d) first and second

Answer: a

2

Conditions for Hajj being obligatory include:

a) Islam, maturity, sanity and ability · b) Great wealth · c) Flying · d) Being over 40

Answer: a

3

«Hajj is 'Arafah» shows that:

a) Standing at 'Arafah is the greatest pillar of Hajj · b) Hajj is one day · c) 'Arafah is in Madinah · d) Tawaf doesn't matter

Answer: a

4

Why is ability a condition for Hajj?

a) Islam does not burden anyone beyond capacity · b) Hajj is only for the rich · c) To reduce pilgrims · d) No reason

Answer: a

5

Difference between tamattu' and qiran?

a) Tamattu': 'Umrah then Hajj with two ihrams; qiran: both with one ihram · b) No difference · c) Qiran is only 'Umrah · d) Tamattu' is only Hajj

Answer: a
