🎯 Describe the leadership of Islam after the Prophet's ﷺ death, the roles and achievements of the early leaders, and explain the importance of their period for the Islamic community, assessing a statement using Qur'an 9:117.
📖 Set passage · Qur'an 9:117
لَّقَد تَّابَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَى ٱلنَّبِيِّ وَٱلۡمُهَٰجِرِينَ وَٱلۡأَنصَارِ ٱلَّذِينَ ٱتَّبَعُوهُ فِي سَاعَةِ ٱلۡعُسۡرَةِ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ مَا كَادَ يَزِيغُ قُلُوبُ فَرِيقٖ مِّنۡهُمۡ ثُمَّ تَابَ عَلَيۡهِمۡۚ إِنَّهُۥ بِهِمۡ رَءُوفٞ رَّحِيمٞ ﴿117﴾
(117) Allah has already forgiven the Prophet and the Muhajireen and the Ansar who followed him in the hour of difficulty after the hearts of a party of them had almost inclined [to doubt], and then He forgave them. Indeed, He was to them Kind and Merciful.
(a) Leadership after the Prophet ﷺ
• 🏛️ Saqifah: The Ansar met at the hall of Banu Sa'idah; after discussion, Abu Bakr was chosen and the companions gave him their pledge (Bukhari).
• 📖 Abu Bakr's first words: He calmed the people with 3:144: Muhammad ﷺ was a messenger; Allah is alive.
• 🗣️ Consultation: The Ansar proposed «a leader from us and a leader from you»; after discussion Umar pledged to Abu Bakr, then the Muhajirun and the Ansar (Bukhari).
• 🕌 The general pledge: The next day the people gave Abu Bakr the pledge in the mosque (Bukhari); the four caliphs followed: Abu Bakr, Umar, Uthman, Ali.

(b) The four rightly guided caliphs
• Abu Bakr (632–634): Kept the community united in the Riddah wars; ordered the Qur'an to be collected into one book.
• Umar (634–644): Expanded the state into Syria, Iraq, Egypt and Jerusalem; began the Hijri calendar; organised administration; known for justice.
• Uthman (644–656): Sent standard copies of the Qur'an to the main cities; continued expansion.
• Ali (656–661): Famous for knowledge and courage; led during a time of civil conflict.
(c) Why their period matters
• 📖 The Qur'an preserved: Collected under Abu Bakr and standardised under Uthman.
• 🤝 Unity: The community survived the shock of the Prophet's ﷺ death and the Riddah.
• 🌍 Spread: Islam spread far beyond Arabia.
• 🏛️ Government: They built administration, a calendar and a treasury.
• ⚖️ A model: Their justice, consultation and simplicity became a model of leadership.
• 🧩 Lessons: The conflict at the end teaches the importance of unity.
