[Ya-Sin: 13–16]
“And present to them an example: the people of the city, when the messengers came to it” (13) “When We sent to them two but they denied them, so We strengthened them with a third, and they said, "Indeed, we are messengers to you” (14) “They said, "You are not but human beings like us, and the Most Merciful has not revealed a thing. You are only telling lies” (15) “They said, "Our Lord knows that we are messengers to you” (16)
