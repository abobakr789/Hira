🎯 Describe the Prophet's ﷺ final pilgrimage and sermon and the events at his death, explain the themes of his later teaching and his achievements in society, religion and politics, and assess a statement using Qur'an 5:3.
📖 Set passage · Qur'an 5:3
حُرِّمَتۡ عَلَيۡكُمُ ٱلۡمَيۡتَةُ وَٱلدَّمُ وَلَحۡمُ ٱلۡخِنزِيرِ وَمَآ أُهِلَّ لِغَيۡرِ ٱللَّهِ بِهِۦ وَٱلۡمُنۡخَنِقَةُ وَٱلۡمَوۡقُوذَةُ وَٱلۡمُتَرَدِّيَةُ وَٱلنَّطِيحَةُ وَمَآ أَكَلَ ٱلسَّبُعُ إِلَّا مَا ذَكَّيۡتُمۡ وَمَا ذُبِحَ عَلَى ٱلنُّصُبِ وَأَن تَسۡتَقۡسِمُواْ بِٱلۡأَزۡلَٰمِۚ ذَٰلِكُمۡ فِسۡقٌۗ ٱلۡيَوۡمَ يَئِسَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ مِن دِينِكُمۡ فَلَا تَخۡشَوۡهُمۡ وَٱخۡشَوۡنِۚ ٱلۡيَوۡمَ أَكۡمَلۡتُ لَكُمۡ دِينَكُمۡ وَأَتۡمَمۡتُ عَلَيۡكُمۡ نِعۡمَتِي وَرَضِيتُ لَكُمُ ٱلۡإِسۡلَٰمَ دِينٗاۚ فَمَنِ ٱضۡطُرَّ فِي مَخۡمَصَةٍ غَيۡرَ مُتَجَانِفٖ لِّإِثۡمٖ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ غَفُورٞ رَّحِيمٞ ﴿3﴾
(3) Prohibited to you are dead animals, blood, the flesh of swine, and that which has been dedicated to other than Allāh, and [those animals] killed by strangling or by a violent blow or by a head-long fall or by the goring of horns, and those from which a wild animal has eaten, except what you [are able to] slaughter [before its death], and those which are sacrificed on stone altars, and [prohibited is] that you seek decision through divining arrows. That is grave disobedience. This day those who disbelieve have despaired of [defeating] your religion; so fear them not, but fear Me. This day I have perfected for you your religion and completed My favor upon you and have approved for you Islām as religion. But whoever is forced by severe hunger with no inclination to sin - then indeed, Allāh is Forgiving and Merciful.
(a) The Farewell Pilgrimage and Sermon
• 🕋 The pilgrimage (10 AH): His only Hajj, with a huge crowd. He taught the rites by example: «Take your rites from me» (Muslim).
• 🩸 Life and property: «Your blood and your property are sacred to you», he declared at Arafah (Muslim).
• 🚫 Riba and revenge: He abolished riba and the blood feuds of the time before Islam (Muslim).
• 👩 Women: «Fear Allah concerning women» — he stressed their rights (Muslim).
• 📖 Hold firm: «I have left among you the Book of Allah; if you hold fast to it you will not go astray» (Muslim).
• 🌍 Equality: No Arab is better than a non-Arab except by taqwa (Ahmad).

(c) His achievements
• 🕌 Religion: He ended idol worship in Arabia, taught tawhid and left the Qur'an and Sunnah.
• 🤝 Society: He protected women, orphans, slaves and the poor, and replaced tribal pride with brotherhood.
• 🏛️ Politics: He united warring tribes into one state with law and leadership.
• 🌟 Example: His later years showed mercy, justice and humility — a model for all Muslims.
(d) The events at his death
• 🤒 Illness: In 11 AH he fell ill and stayed in A'ishah's room.
• 🕌 Abu Bakr leads the prayer: He told the companions: «Tell Abu Bakr to lead the people in prayer» (Bukhari).
• 💔 His death: He died in Madinah in Rabi' al-Awwal 11 AH (632 CE) and was buried in A'ishah's room.
• 📖 Abu Bakr's words: «Whoever worshipped Muhammad, Muhammad has died; whoever worships Allah, Allah is alive and does not die» — then he recited 3:144 (Bukhari).
