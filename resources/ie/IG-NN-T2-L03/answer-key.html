Silver

1: 📜 Tawrat: Given to Musa: «We sent down the Tawrat, in which was guidance and light» (5:44); law for the Children of Israel. · 🎶 Zabur: Given to Dawud (4:163). «We have written in the Zabur… the land is inherited by My righteous servants» (21:105). · ✨ Injil: Given to Isa: «guidance and light, confirming the Tawrat» (5:46); he taught Tawhid and good character. · 📃 Scrolls of Ibrahim: «The former scriptures, the scriptures of Ibrahim and Musa» (87:18–19): reminders that the Hereafter is better and lasting. · 🔗 One chain: All the books came from Allah with the same message of Tawhid; believing in them is an article of faith. · 📖 The Qur'an completes them: It confirms the earlier books and is a criterion over them (5:48). · 🛡️ Preserved: Muslims believe the Qur'an alone is preserved in its original form: «We will be its guardian» (15:9). · 🕌 For the early community: The Qur'an gave the early Muslims law, worship and identity, and linked them to the prophets before.
2 (2 marks): They contained guidance and light (5:44, 5:46); they came from Allah; the Qur'an confirms them.
3 (3 marks): The Injil was revealed to Isa as «guidance and light» (5:46). It taught Tawhid — the worship of Allah alone — and confirmed the Tawrat. It taught good character and morals, and prepared people for the coming of a later messenger.
4 (3 marks): It was revealed by Allah to Musa (1); the Qur'an calls it «guidance and light» (5:44) (1); it contained law for the Children of Israel and taught Tawhid (1).

Gold

1–2 (model answer): Belief in the revealed books is one of the articles of faith, so it is part of being a Muslim. All the books came from Allah with the same message of Tawhid (4:163), which shows that Islam continues the religion of all the prophets. The Qur'an confirms them and is the final, preserved book (5:48; 15:9), so Muslims respect the earlier revelations while following the Qur'an.

Diamond

Indicative content: Agree: verse 39 — prophets «fear none but Allah»: persecution tested and strengthened faith (Bilal's «Ahad, Ahad»; Sumayyah's sacrifice); hardship bound the believers together and prepared them for Madinah. Other view: the opposition also caused suffering and loss — Sumayyah was killed and some Muslims had to leave for Abyssinia; verse 38 shows that strength comes from following «the established way of Allah», not from persecution itself; Allah's help and the Prophet's ﷺ leadership kept them firm. Conclusion: a justified judgement, e.g. opposition revealed and deepened their faith, but it was faith in Allah, not the opposition itself, that made them strong. Mark with the Level 1–3 descriptors (Level 3 needs the text, another view and a justified conclusion).
