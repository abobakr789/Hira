Key learning points

Building understanding

📖

1

Taqlid means following someone in speech, action or belief; without evidence or awareness it is blind imitation.

⚠️

2

The Qur'an condemns it: «When told to follow what Allah has revealed, they say: Rather, we follow what we found our fathers doing.»

🔍

3

Causes: weak personality and lack of self-confidence, ignorance of religion, emptiness and no goals, and fascination with celebrities.

🛡️

4

Dangers: losing one's identity, a culture of consumerism and the death of creativity. The cure: awareness, self-confidence and a good role model: «Do not be an imma'ah.»

Hiraa quick challenge

5 questions

1

Ibn Mas'ud said: «Be a scholar or a learner, and do not be...»

a) a mindless follower (imma'ah) · b) rich · c) poor · d) a traveller

Answer: a

2

Causes of blind imitation include:

a) weak personality, ignorance and wanting attention · b) self-confidence · c) knowledge · d) thoughtful reading

Answer: a

3

Difference between following a good example and blind imitation:

a) Following is aware of what is good; blind imitation is thoughtless · b) No difference · c) Imitation is better · d) Following is forbidden

Answer: a

4

What is the difference between learning from others and blind imitation?

a) Learning takes what is useful with awareness; blind imitation takes everything without thought · b) No difference · c) Learning is forbidden · d) Imitation is better

Answer: a
