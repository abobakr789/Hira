✨Surat Al-Buruj: Allah protects patient believers, punishes wrongdoers, and the Qur'an is preserved in the Preserved Tablet.
📖 I read the text
وَٱلسَّمَآءِ ذَاتِ ٱلۡبُرُوجِ ﴿1﴾ وَٱلۡيَوۡمِ ٱلۡمَوۡعُودِ ﴿2﴾ وَشَاهِدٖ وَمَشۡهُودٖ ﴿3﴾ قُتِلَ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡأُخۡدُودِ ﴿4﴾ ٱلنَّارِ ذَاتِ ٱلۡوَقُودِ ﴿5﴾ إِذۡ هُمۡ عَلَيۡهَا قُعُودٞ ﴿6﴾ وَهُمۡ عَلَىٰ مَا يَفۡعَلُونَ بِٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ شُهُودٞ ﴿7﴾
[Al-Buruj: 1–7]
“By the sky containing great stars” “And [by] the promised Day” “And [by] the witness and what is witnessed” “Cursed were the companions of the trench” “[Containing] the fire full of fuel” “When they were sitting near it” “And they, to what they were doing against the believers, were witnesses”

💡 I learned
✨Allah swore by the sky with its great constellations and by the Day of Judgement.
🔥The People of the Ditch dug a pit of fire and threw the believers into it because they believed in Allah.
🌳Patient believers will have Gardens with rivers flowing beneath: the great success.
📜The Qur'an is glorious, preserved in the Preserved Tablet, and never changes.

☐I stay firm on the truth even when it is hard.
