🎯 Explain the meaning of 'Seal of the Prophets', the importance of the Prophet's ﷺ role in establishing Islam and the opposition he faced in Makkah, and assess a statement using Qur'an 33:38–40.
📖 Set passage · Qur'an 33:38–40
مَّا كَانَ عَلَى ٱلنَّبِيِّ مِنۡ حَرَجٖ فِيمَا فَرَضَ ٱللَّهُ لَهُۥۖ سُنَّةَ ٱللَّهِ فِي ٱلَّذِينَ خَلَوۡاْ مِن قَبۡلُۚ وَكَانَ أَمۡرُ ٱللَّهِ قَدَرٗا مَّقۡدُورًا ﴿38﴾ ٱلَّذِينَ يُبَلِّغُونَ رِسَٰلَٰتِ ٱللَّهِ وَيَخۡشَوۡنَهُۥ وَلَا يَخۡشَوۡنَ أَحَدًا إِلَّا ٱللَّهَۗ وَكَفَىٰ بِٱللَّهِ حَسِيبٗا ﴿39﴾ مَّا كَانَ مُحَمَّدٌ أَبَآ أَحَدٖ مِّن رِّجَالِكُمۡ وَلَٰكِن رَّسُولَ ٱللَّهِ وَخَاتَمَ ٱلنَّبِيِّۧنَۗ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ بِكُلِّ شَيۡءٍ عَلِيمٗا ﴿40﴾
(38) There is not to be upon the Prophet any discomfort concerning that which Allah has imposed upon him. [This is] the established way of Allah with those [prophets] who have passed on before. And ever is the command of Allah a destiny decreed.
(39) [Allah praises] those who convey the messages of Allah and fear Him and do not fear anyone but Allah. And sufficient is Allah as Accountant.
(40) Muhammad is not the father of [any] one of your men, but [he is] the Messenger of Allah and last of the prophets. And ever is Allah, of all things, Knowing.
📜 Hadith · Bukhari and Muslim (narrated by Abu Hurayrah)
«My example and the example of the prophets before me is like a man who built a house, made it fine and beautiful, except for the place of one brick in a corner… I am that brick, and I am the last of the prophets.»
(a) 'Seal of the Prophets'
• Last prophet: No prophet and no new revelation will come after him.
• Complete message: Islam is complete; the Qur'an is preserved, so no new book is needed.
• For all people: Earlier prophets came to their own nations; his message is for all humanity until the Last Day.
• What it means for Muslims: Any later claim to prophethood is rejected; Muslims follow the Qur'an and the Sunnah.

(b) Why his role mattered in establishing Islam
• 📖 Received & conveyed: He received the Qur'an over 23 years and delivered it (33:39) — without him there is no revelation to follow.
• 🧭 Explained & lived it: His Sunnah shows how to practise: «Pray as you have seen me praying» (Bukhari).
• ☝️ Called to Tawhid: He called Makkah away from idols to the worship of one God.
• 🤝 Built a community: In Madinah he united Muhajirun and Ansar around the mosque — the first Muslim society.
• ⚖️ Leader & lawgiver: He judged, led and organised the community by revelation (the Shari'ah).
• 🔒 Final messenger: His role completed the religion: «This day I have perfected for you your religion» (5:3).
(c) Opposition in Makkah: why?
• ☝️ Tawhid vs idols: Worshipping one God attacked the idols around the Ka'bah and the religion of their forefathers.
• 💰 Trade & wealth: Pilgrims came to Makkah for the idols; leaders feared losing this income.
• 👑 Power & status: The message made slaves and masters equal before Allah, challenging the leaders' authority.
• ⚖️ Judgement: Belief in accountability on the Day of Judgement condemned their injustice to the poor.
(c) Opposition in Makkah: how?
• 🗣️ Mockery & insults: They called him a madman and a poet (15:6; 37:36). Abu Lahab and Abu Jahl led the hostility.
• ⛓️ Torture of the weak: Bilal was tortured on the hot sand and kept saying «Ahad, Ahad» (One, One) until Abu Bakr freed him.
• 💔 The first martyr: Sumayyah, the mother of Ammar, was killed for her faith — remembered as the first martyr of Islam.
• 🏛️ Pressure on his family: Quraysh pressed his uncle Abu Talib to stop him; Abu Talib kept protecting him.
• ⛵ Migration to Abyssinia: Some Muslims sought safety with the just Christian king of Abyssinia (the Negus).
• 🧱 Response: Patience, continued preaching and no revenge. (The boycott comes in topic 1.4.)
