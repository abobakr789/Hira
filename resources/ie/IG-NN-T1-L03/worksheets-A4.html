🎯 Explain the meaning of 'Seal of the Prophets', the importance of the Prophet's ﷺ role in establishing Islam and the opposition he faced in Makkah, and assess a statement using Qur'an 33:38–40.
1Match each key term with its meaning (write the letter).
TermLetterMeaning
1) NabiA) The leading tribe of Makkah, guardians of the Ka'bah.
2) RasulB) A prophet: chosen by Allah to receive revelation.
3) RisalahC) Prophethood: Allah's guidance reaching people through His messengers.
4) Khatam an-NabiyyinD) A messenger: a prophet sent with a message to deliver.
5) TawhidE) Seal of the Prophets: the last prophet (33:40).
6) QurayshF) The oneness of Allah — the heart of every prophet's message.
📖 Set passage · Qur'an 33:38–40
مَّا كَانَ عَلَى ٱلنَّبِيِّ مِنۡ حَرَجٖ فِيمَا فَرَضَ ٱللَّهُ لَهُۥۖ سُنَّةَ ٱللَّهِ فِي ٱلَّذِينَ خَلَوۡاْ مِن قَبۡلُۚ وَكَانَ أَمۡرُ ٱللَّهِ قَدَرٗا مَّقۡدُورًا ﴿38﴾ ٱلَّذِينَ يُبَلِّغُونَ رِسَٰلَٰتِ ٱللَّهِ وَيَخۡشَوۡنَهُۥ وَلَا يَخۡشَوۡنَ أَحَدًا إِلَّا ٱللَّهَۗ وَكَفَىٰ بِٱللَّهِ حَسِيبٗا ﴿39﴾ مَّا كَانَ مُحَمَّدٌ أَبَآ أَحَدٖ مِّن رِّجَالِكُمۡ وَلَٰكِن رَّسُولَ ٱللَّهِ وَخَاتَمَ ٱلنَّبِيِّۧنَۗ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ بِكُلِّ شَيۡءٍ عَلِيمٗا ﴿40﴾
(38) There is not to be upon the Prophet any discomfort concerning that which Allah has imposed upon him. [This is] the established way of Allah with those [prophets] who have passed on before. And ever is the command of Allah a destiny decreed.
(39) [Allah praises] those who convey the messages of Allah and fear Him and do not fear anyone but Allah. And sufficient is Allah as Accountant.
(40) Muhammad is not the father of [any] one of your men, but [he is] the Messenger of Allah and last of the prophets. And ever is Allah, of all things, Knowing.
2Which verse of the passage says this? Write 38, 39 or 40.
StatementVerse
He is «the Messenger of Allah and last of the prophets».
Prophets «convey the messages of Allah» and fear no one but Allah.
Muhammad ﷺ follows «the established way of Allah» with the prophets before him.
