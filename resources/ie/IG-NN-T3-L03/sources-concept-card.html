📖 Set passage · Qur'an 2:183–185
يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ كُتِبَ عَلَيۡكُمُ ٱلصِّيَامُ كَمَا كُتِبَ عَلَى ٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبۡلِكُمۡ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَتَّقُونَ ﴿183﴾ أَيَّامٗا مَّعۡدُودَٰتٖۚ فَمَن كَانَ مِنكُم مَّرِيضًا أَوۡ عَلَىٰ سَفَرٖ فَعِدَّةٞ مِّنۡ أَيَّامٍ أُخَرَۚ وَعَلَى ٱلَّذِينَ يُطِيقُونَهُۥ فِدۡيَةٞ طَعَامُ مِسۡكِينٖۖ فَمَن تَطَوَّعَ خَيۡرٗا فَهُوَ خَيۡرٞ لَّهُۥۚ وَأَن تَصُومُواْ خَيۡرٞ لَّكُمۡ إِن كُنتُمۡ تَعۡلَمُونَ ﴿184﴾ شَهۡرُ رَمَضَانَ ٱلَّذِيٓ أُنزِلَ فِيهِ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانُ هُدٗى لِّلنَّاسِ وَبَيِّنَٰتٖ مِّنَ ٱلۡهُدَىٰ وَٱلۡفُرۡقَانِۚ فَمَن شَهِدَ مِنكُمُ ٱلشَّهۡرَ فَلۡيَصُمۡهُۖ وَمَن كَانَ مَرِيضًا أَوۡ عَلَىٰ سَفَرٖ فَعِدَّةٞ مِّنۡ أَيَّامٍ أُخَرَۗ يُرِيدُ ٱللَّهُ بِكُمُ ٱلۡيُسۡرَ وَلَا يُرِيدُ بِكُمُ ٱلۡعُسۡرَ وَلِتُكۡمِلُواْ ٱلۡعِدَّةَ وَلِتُكَبِّرُواْ ٱللَّهَ عَلَىٰ مَا هَدَىٰكُمۡ وَلَعَلَّكُمۡ تَشۡكُرُونَ ﴿185﴾
(183) O you who have believed, decreed upon you is fasting as it was decreed upon those before you that you may become righteous -
(184) [Fasting for] a limited number of days. So whoever among you is ill or on a journey [during them] – then an equal number of days [are to be made up]. And upon those who are able [to fast, but with hardship] – a ransom [as substitute] of feeding a poor person [each day]. And whoever volunteers excess – it is better for him. But to fast is best for you, if you only knew.
(185) The month of Ramadan [is that] in which was revealed the Qur'an, a guidance for the people and clear proofs of guidance and criterion. So whoever sights [the new moon of] the month, let him fast it; and whoever is ill or on a journey – then an equal number of other days. Allah intends for you ease and does not intend for you hardship and [wants] for you to complete the period and to glorify Allah for that [to] which He has guided you; and perhaps you will be grateful.
(a) Nature, role, significance and purpose
• 🌙 Nature: No food, drink or marital relations from dawn to sunset for the month of Ramadan: the fourth pillar.
• 🎯 Purpose: «…that you may become righteous» (2:183): taqwa and self-control.
• 🤝 Significance: Feeling hunger builds empathy with the poor; families and mosques share iftar.
• ✨ Reward: «Whoever fasts Ramadan out of faith and hoping for reward, his past sins are forgiven» (Bukhari, Muslim).

Classroom task: discuss the teaching, not personal medical details. Do not ask students to fast as an experiment. Health questions belong with a qualified clinician and trusted religious guidance.

(b) Those who are exempt
• 🤒 The ill: May break the fast and make it up later (2:184).
• ✈️ Travellers: May break the fast and make it up later (2:185).
• 👴 The elderly / chronically ill: Who cannot fast feed a poor person for each day (fidyah, 2:184).
• 🤰 Pregnant or breastfeeding: May leave the fast if it harms them or the baby, and make it up.
• 🩸 Menstruation: Women do not fast and make up the days later (Muslim).
• 🧒 Children: Not required before puberty, but often practise.
(c) Laylat al-Qadr for Muslims today
• 📖 The night of revelation: The Qur'an was first revealed on this night (97:1).
• ⏳ Better than 1000 months: Worship on it is worth more than a lifetime of worship (97:3).
• 🤲 Forgiveness: «Whoever stands in prayer on Laylat al-Qadr out of faith and hoping for reward, his past sins are forgiven» (Bukhari, Muslim).
• 🕌 Today: Muslims seek it in the last ten nights: extra prayer, Qur'an, du'a and i'tikaf in the mosque.
