Sources & concepts: Surat Quraysh
Islamic Education · Grade 2 · V1 p.148
For all
🐫Surat Quraysh reminds us of Allah's blessings on Quraysh, safety and food, so that they would worship the Lord of the Ka'bah.
📖 I read the text
لِإِيلَٰفِ قُرَيۡشٍ ﴿1﴾ إِۦلَٰفِهِمۡ رِحۡلَةَ ٱلشِّتَآءِ وَٱلصَّيۡفِ ﴿2﴾ فَلۡيَعۡبُدُواْ رَبَّ هَٰذَا ٱلۡبَيۡتِ ﴿3﴾ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَطۡعَمَهُم مِّن جُوعٖ وَءَامَنَهُم مِّنۡ خَوۡفِۭ ﴿4﴾
[Quraysh: 1–4]
“For the accustomed security of the Quraysh” “Their accustomed security [in] the caravan of winter and summer” “Let them worship the Lord of this House” “Who has fed them, [saving them] from hunger and made them safe, [saving them] from fear”
📘 My words
Ilaf
being used to travelling safely
Al-Bayt
the House: the Ka'bah
💡 I learned
🐫Quraysh travelled to trade: to Yemen in winter and to Syria in summer.
🛡️Allah kept their caravans safe on the road.
🍞Allah fed them when they were hungry and kept them safe from fear.
🕋So Allah told them to worship Him alone, the Lord of the Ka'bah.
✅ I apply
☐I thank Allah for the safety of my country.
☐I thank Allah for my food and never waste it.
☐I learn Surat Quraysh and recite it correctly.
