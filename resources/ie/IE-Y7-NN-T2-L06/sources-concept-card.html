Sources & concepts: Surat Ar-Rahman (1–25)
Islamic Education · Grade 7 · Non-Arabic speakers · But over Every Possessor of Knowledge Is One More Knowing · Textbook V2 p.12
For all
Objective: To infer from Surat Ar-Rahman (1–25) the signs of Allah's mercy and explain His greatness through His blessings.
📖 Read the text
ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنُ ﴿1﴾ عَلَّمَ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ ﴿2﴾ خَلَقَ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ ﴿3﴾ عَلَّمَهُ ٱلۡبَيَانَ ﴿4﴾ ٱلشَّمۡسُ وَٱلۡقَمَرُ بِحُسۡبَانٖ ﴿5﴾ وَٱلنَّجۡمُ وَٱلشَّجَرُ يَسۡجُدَانِ ﴿6﴾ وَٱلسَّمَآءَ رَفَعَهَا وَوَضَعَ ٱلۡمِيزَانَ ﴿7﴾ أَلَّا تَطۡغَوۡاْ فِي ٱلۡمِيزَانِ ﴿8﴾ وَأَقِيمُواْ ٱلۡوَزۡنَ بِٱلۡقِسۡطِ وَلَا تُخۡسِرُواْ ٱلۡمِيزَانَ ﴿9﴾ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ وَضَعَهَا لِلۡأَنَامِ ﴿10﴾
[Ar-Rahman: 1–10]
📘 Key words
بحسبان (Bi-husban)by precise calculation
برزخ (Barzakh)barrier
Ala'favours
💡 Main ideas
1. The Most Merciful taught the Qur'an, created man and taught him speech.
2. The sun and moon move by precise calculation, the stars and trees bow, and He set the balance so we do not transgress.
3. He laid out the earth for all creatures, with fruit, palms with sheathed clusters, grain and fragrant plants.
4. He released the two seas meeting, with a barrier between them; from them come pearls and coral.
✅ Lessons we take
1. The Qur'an is the greatest blessing.
2. The order of the universe shows Allah's greatness.
3. Justice is Allah's law in creation and in dealings.
4. Thank Allah in words and actions.
🇦🇪 National identity: Surat Ar-Rahman's many blessings call for thanks; the UAE protects its seas, palms and land.
