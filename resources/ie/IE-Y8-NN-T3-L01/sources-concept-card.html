Sources & concepts: The Path to Paradise: Surat Ya-Sin (55–68)
Islamic Education · Grade 8 · Non-Arabic speakers · Indeed in That Are Signs for a People Who Reason · Textbook V2 p.140
For all
Objective: To describe from Surat Ya-Sin (55–68) the state of the righteous in Paradise and infer the deeds that bring us closer to Allah.
📖 Read the text
إِنَّ أَصۡحَٰبَ ٱلۡجَنَّةِ ٱلۡيَوۡمَ فِي شُغُلٖ فَٰكِهُونَ ﴿55﴾ هُمۡ وَأَزۡوَٰجُهُمۡ فِي ظِلَٰلٍ عَلَى ٱلۡأَرَآئِكِ مُتَّكِـُٔونَ ﴿56﴾ لَهُمۡ فِيهَا فَٰكِهَةٞ وَلَهُم مَّا يَدَّعُونَ ﴿57﴾ سَلَٰمٞ قَوۡلٗا مِّن رَّبّٖ رَّحِيمٖ ﴿58﴾ وَٱمۡتَٰزُواْ ٱلۡيَوۡمَ أَيُّهَا ٱلۡمُجۡرِمُونَ ﴿59﴾ ۞أَلَمۡ أَعۡهَدۡ إِلَيۡكُمۡ يَٰبَنِيٓ ءَادَمَ أَن لَّا تَعۡبُدُواْ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنَۖ إِنَّهُۥ لَكُمۡ عَدُوّٞ مُّبِينٞ ﴿60﴾ وَأَنِ ٱعۡبُدُونِيۚ هَٰذَا صِرَٰطٞ مُّسۡتَقِيمٞ ﴿61﴾ وَلَقَدۡ أَضَلَّ مِنكُمۡ جِبِلّٗا كَثِيرًاۖ أَفَلَمۡ تَكُونُواْ تَعۡقِلُونَ ﴿62﴾ هَٰذِهِۦ جَهَنَّمُ ٱلَّتِي كُنتُمۡ تُوعَدُونَ ﴿63﴾ ٱصۡلَوۡهَا ٱلۡيَوۡمَ بِمَا كُنتُمۡ تَكۡفُرُونَ ﴿64﴾
[Ya-Sin: 55–64]
📘 Key words
فاكهون (Fakihun)rejoicing
امتازوا (Imtazu)stand apart
💡 Main ideas
1. The people of Paradise are busy in joy, reclining in shade with their spouses: «Peace, a word from a Merciful Lord.»
2. «Stand apart today, you wrongdoers!» Did I not command you, children of Adam, not to worship Satan?
3. «Today We seal their mouths, their hands speak to Us and their feet bear witness.»
4. «Whoever We give long life, We reverse in creation»: weakness in old age urges us to use our youth well.
✅ Lessons we take
1. The way to Paradise is worshipping Allah and refusing Satan.
2. Our limbs will testify — use them well.
🇦🇪 National identity: Youth are the UAE's wealth, and preparing for the Hereafter drives them to spend their lives on good.
✉️ Lesson message: I want Paradise
