Sources & concepts: Prohibition of Frightening People
Islamic Education · Grade 8 · Non-Arabic speakers · Indeed, Allah Loves the Doers of Good · Textbook V2 p.102
For all
Objective: To deduce from the hadith the sanctity of human life and justify the prohibition of frightening people.
📖 Read the text · Textbook V2 p.102
«لَا يُشِيرُ أَحَدُكُمْ عَلَى أَخِيهِ بِالسِّلَاحِ، فَإِنَّهُ لَا يَدْرِي، لَعَلَّ الشَّيْطَانَ يَنْزِغُ فِي يَدِهِ، فَيَقَعُ فِي حُفْرَةٍ مِنَ النَّارِ»
"None of you should point a weapon at his brother, for he does not know: Satan may push his hand and he may fall into a pit of the Fire."

Tarwi'frightening
💡 Main ideas
1. The Prophet ﷺ said: «None of you should point a weapon at his brother, for he does not know whether Satan may make it slip in his hand and he fall into a pit of the Fire.»
2. «It is not lawful for a Muslim to frighten a Muslim.» As-Salam is one of Allah's names, and a Muslim is one from whose tongue and hand others are safe.
3. Frightening includes scary pranks, playing with sharp objects, spreading alarming news and threats on social media.
4. Whoever frightens safe people sins and risks Allah's punishment, and UAE law protects people's safety and punishes threats.
✅ Lessons we take
1. Frightening people is forbidden, even as a joke.
2. Cyberbullying is a modern form of harm.
🇦🇪 National identity: UAE law criminalises frightening others and misusing weapons to keep people safe.
