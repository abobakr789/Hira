What humility is

Understanding the lesson

Humility means knowing your true worth and not looking down on others. Arrogance is «rejecting the truth and looking down on people» (Muslim).

Humbleness

Explain with questioning; highlight the evidence.

In the Qur'an

Understanding the lesson

Allah's servants «walk on the earth gently» (25:63), and «do not turn your cheek away from people or walk proudly» (31:18).

The Prophet's ﷺ humility

Understanding the lesson

He sat on the ground, ate with servants, mended his clothes, greeted children and said: «No one humbles himself for Allah except that Allah raises him» (Muslim).

Hiraa quick challenge

5 questions

1

«Arrogance is rejecting the truth and looking down on people»: «ghamt an-nas» means:

a) looking down on people · b) helping people · c) praising people · d) visiting people

Answer: a

2

«Batar al-haqq» means:

a) rejecting the truth · b) accepting it · c) searching for it · d) defending it

Answer: a

3

What is the difference between humility and humiliation?

a) Humility is gentleness with dignity; humiliation is weakness and disgrace · b) No difference · c) Humility is weakness · d) Humiliation is good character

Answer: a

4

How was Sheikh Zayed a role model of humility?

a) He stayed close to his people, sat with them and listened · b) He kept away from people · c) He refused to talk to ordinary people · d) He visited no one

Answer: a
