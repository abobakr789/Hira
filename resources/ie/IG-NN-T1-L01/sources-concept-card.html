🎯 Describe the Prophet's ﷺ ancestry, early influences, work and family life, and explain how his early life prepared him to be the Messenger of Allah, using Qur'an 66:1–3.
📖 Set passage · Qur'an 66:1–3
يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّبِيُّ لِمَ تُحَرِّمُ مَآ أَحَلَّ ٱللَّهُ لَكَۖ تَبۡتَغِي مَرۡضَاتَ أَزۡوَٰجِكَۚ وَٱللَّهُ غَفُورٞ رَّحِيمٞ ﴿1﴾ قَدۡ فَرَضَ ٱللَّهُ لَكُمۡ تَحِلَّةَ أَيۡمَٰنِكُمۡۚ وَٱللَّهُ مَوۡلَىٰكُمۡۖ وَهُوَ ٱلۡعَلِيمُ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ ﴿2﴾ وَإِذۡ أَسَرَّ ٱلنَّبِيُّ إِلَىٰ بَعۡضِ أَزۡوَٰجِهِۦ حَدِيثٗا فَلَمَّا نَبَّأَتۡ بِهِۦ وَأَظۡهَرَهُ ٱللَّهُ عَلَيۡهِ عَرَّفَ بَعۡضَهُۥ وَأَعۡرَضَ عَنۢ بَعۡضٖۖ فَلَمَّا نَبَّأَهَا بِهِۦ قَالَتۡ مَنۡ أَنۢبَأَكَ هَٰذَاۖ قَالَ نَبَّأَنِيَ ٱلۡعَلِيمُ ٱلۡخَبِيرُ ﴿3﴾
(1) O Prophet, why do you prohibit [yourself from] what Allāh has made lawful for you, seeking the approval of your wives? And Allāh is Forgiving and Merciful.
(2) Allāh has already ordained for you [Muslims] the dissolution of your oaths. And Allāh is your protector, and He is the Knowing, the Wise.
(3) And [remember] when the Prophet confided to one of his wives a statement; and when she informed [another] of it and Allāh showed it to him, he made known part of it and ignored a part. And when he informed her about it, she said, "Who told you this?" He said, "I was informed by the Knowing, the Aware."
(a) Birth and early influences
• 👶 Orphan: His father Abdullah died before his birth; his mother Aminah died when he was six.
• 🧓 Guardians: His grandfather Abd al-Muttalib cared for him, then his uncle Abu Talib, who protected him all his life.
• 🐑 Shepherd: He worked as a shepherd as a young man — learning patience and care for the weak.
• ⛺ Bahira: On a trade journey to Syria with Abu Talib, the monk Bahira saw signs of prophethood in the boy and told Abu Talib to protect him (Tirmidhi).

(a) Work, marriage and family
• 💼 Trader: He traded honestly for Khadijah and became known as al-Sadiq al-Amin (the truthful, the trustworthy).
• 💍 Khadijah: Impressed by his honesty, Khadijah proposed marriage; he was 25. She was his only wife until her death.
• 👨‍👧 Children: With Khadijah: al-Qasim, Zaynab, Ruqayyah, Umm Kulthum, Fatimah and Abdullah. Later, Ibrahim was born to Mariyah. The sons died in childhood.
• 🏠 At home: «He used to be in the service of his family» — A'ishah (Bukhari). «The best of you is the best to his family» (Tirmidhi).
(b) Why his early life mattered
• 🤝 Trust: Even opponents knew he never lied, so they could not easily reject his message as a lie.
• 💛 Compassion: Being an orphan helped him understand the poor, orphans and the weak.
• ⚖️ Fairness: Trade taught him honesty in dealings and knowledge of people — later the basis of his teaching on fair trade.
• 🏡 Role model: As husband and father he showed Muslims how to live family life with kindness.
