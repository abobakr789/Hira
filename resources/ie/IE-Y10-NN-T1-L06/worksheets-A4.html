1Evaluate and discuss: Friends pressure you to abandon your values. What do you learn from the youths of the cave, and how was their withdrawal different from isolating from everyone?
Hint: Good company, steadfastness, and withdrawing from trial — not from society.
2Analyse the situation: what is wrong, what is the right action, and what is the evidence?
Young men rejected what their people did, left everything and sheltered in a cave, sleeping over three hundred years. What drove them, and what do they say to youth today?
