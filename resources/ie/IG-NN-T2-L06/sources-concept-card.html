📖 Set passage · Qur'an 2:97–98
قُلۡ مَن كَانَ عَدُوّٗا لِّجِبۡرِيلَ فَإِنَّهُۥ نَزَّلَهُۥ عَلَىٰ قَلۡبِكَ بِإِذۡنِ ٱللَّهِ مُصَدِّقٗا لِّمَا بَيۡنَ يَدَيۡهِ وَهُدٗى وَبُشۡرَىٰ لِلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ ﴿97﴾ مَن كَانَ عَدُوّٗا لِّلَّهِ وَمَلَٰٓئِكَتِهِۦ وَرُسُلِهِۦ وَجِبۡرِيلَ وَمِيكَىٰلَ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ عَدُوّٞ لِّلۡكَٰفِرِينَ ﴿98﴾
(97) Say, "Whoever is an enemy to Gabriel - it is [none but] he who has brought it [i.e., the Qur’ān] down upon your heart, [O Muḥammad], by permission of Allāh, confirming that which was before it and as guidance and good tidings for the believers."
(98) Whoever is an enemy to Allāh and His angels and His messengers and Gabriel and Michael - then indeed, Allāh is an enemy to the disbelievers.
(a) The nature of angels
• ✨ Created from light: Angels were created from light (Muslim).
• 🙇 Always obedient: «They do not disobey Allah in what He commands them» (66:6); they have no free will to sin.
• 👁️ Unseen: Belief in them is part of belief in the unseen and one of the six articles of faith.
• 🛠️ Many roles: They carry revelation, record deeds, protect, and question in the grave.

(b) The principal angels
• 📜 Jibrail: Brought revelation to the prophets, including the whole Qur'an to Muhammad ﷺ (2:97).
• 🌧️ Mikail: Responsible for rain and provision for creation; named in 2:98.
• 📯 Israfil: Will blow the trumpet at the end of the world and for the resurrection (39:68).
• ⏳ The angel of death: «The angel of death will take you, who has been entrusted with you» (32:11); known in tradition as Izrael.
(a) Why angels matter for Muslims
• 📖 Revelation: Without Jibrail there would be no Qur'an — the link between Allah and the prophets.
• ✍️ Accountability: Recording angels (82:10–12) remind Muslims every deed counts.
• 🛡️ Support: Angels helped the believers at Badr (8:9), giving hope that Allah supports the faithful.
• ⏳ Death and judgement: The angel of death and Israfil remind Muslims that life is short and judgement is coming.
