What sincerity is

Understanding the lesson

Ikhlas means doing deeds for Allah alone, not for praise. «They were only commanded to worship Allah, sincere to Him in religion» (98:5).

Sincerity (Ikhlas)

Showing off (riya')

Understanding the lesson

The Prophet ﷺ called showing off «the minor shirk». Allah says: «I am the most free of need of partners; whoever does a deed for Me and someone else, I leave him and his partner.»

How to be sincere

Understanding the lesson

Remember Allah sees you, hide some good deeds, pray for sincerity, and act the same whether people see you or not.

Hiraa quick challenge

5 questions

1

Sincerity (ikhlas) is:

a) Doing deeds for Allah alone · b) Doing many deeds · c) Doing deeds in front of people · d) Leaving deeds

Answer: a

2

A sign of sincerity is:

a) Acting the same in private and in public · b) Waiting for praise · c) Only acting on camera · d) Boasting

Answer: a

3

How do we show sincerity to our country?

a) Doing our work well and being loyal to its leadership · b) Only talking about it · c) Travelling abroad · d) Working only for myself

Answer: a

4

You volunteered in a clean-up and a friend tells you to post photos for likes. What keeps sincerity?

a) Check my intention: if I post, it is to encourage others not for praise, and keep most deeds hidden · b) Post only for likes · c) Stop volunteering · d) Fake the photos

Answer: a
