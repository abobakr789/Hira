Sources & concepts: Tayammum and Wiping over Footwear
Islamic Education · Grade 7 · Non-Arabic speakers · He Is, of All Things, Knowing · Textbook V1 p.98
For all
Objective: To compare tayammum and wiping over socks and perform both correctly.
📖 Read the text
يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ لَا تَقۡرَبُواْ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَأَنتُمۡ سُكَٰرَىٰ حَتَّىٰ تَعۡلَمُواْ مَا تَقُولُونَ وَلَا جُنُبًا إِلَّا عَابِرِي سَبِيلٍ حَتَّىٰ تَغۡتَسِلُواْۚ وَإِن كُنتُم مَّرۡضَىٰٓ أَوۡ عَلَىٰ سَفَرٍ أَوۡ جَآءَ أَحَدٞ مِّنكُم مِّنَ ٱلۡغَآئِطِ أَوۡ لَٰمَسۡتُمُ ٱلنِّسَآءَ فَلَمۡ تَجِدُواْ مَآءٗ فَتَيَمَّمُواْ صَعِيدٗا طَيِّبٗا فَٱمۡسَحُواْ بِوُجُوهِكُمۡ وَأَيۡدِيكُمۡۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ كَانَ عَفُوًّا غَفُورًا ﴿43﴾
[An-Nisa: 43–43]
📘 Key words
Sa'idclean earth
Khuffleather sock
💡 Main ideas
1. Tayammum: wiping the face and hands with clean earth, with intention, when there is no water or it cannot be used.
2. How: strike the earth once with the hands, then wipe the face and palms.
3. It is broken by anything that breaks wudu, or by finding water before praying.
4. Wiping over khuffs: one day and night for a resident, three days for a traveller, if they were put on in a state of purity.
✅ Lessons we take
1. Islam is easy, not harsh.
2. Concessions are mercy, not laziness.
3. Prayer is never dropped because there is no water.
🇦🇪 National identity: Concessions like tayammum are mercy for the sick and travellers, as the state makes services easy for all.
✉️ Lesson message: My religion is ease ️
