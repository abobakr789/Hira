Sources & concepts: Surat Al-Asr
Islamic Education · Grade 2 · V1 p.20
For all
⏰Surat Al-'Asr: all people are in loss, except those who believe, do good, and urge one another to truth and patience.
📖 I read the text
وَٱلۡعَصۡرِ ﴿1﴾ إِنَّ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ لَفِي خُسۡرٍ ﴿2﴾ إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّـٰلِحَٰتِ وَتَوَاصَوۡاْ بِٱلۡحَقِّ وَتَوَاصَوۡاْ بِٱلصَّبۡرِ ﴿3﴾
[Al-'Asr: 1–3]
“By time” “Indeed, mankind is in loss” “Except for those who have believed and done righteous deeds and advised each other to truth and advised each other to patience”
📘 My words
Al-'Asr
time
Khusr
loss
As-Salihat

💡 I learned
⏰Allah swore by Al-'Asr, which means time, because it matters so much in our lives.
📉People lose when they waste their time on what is not good.
✅The winners are the believers who do good deeds.
🤝And who remind each other of the truth and of patience.
✅ I apply
☐I use my time to study and do useful things.
☐I kindly remind my friends to do good.
☐I am patient when things are hard.
🇦🇪 My country: We use our time for learning and work, as Emiratis did building their country.
