🎯 Explain the principal sources of Shari'ah (Qur'an and Sunnah), its importance for the early community, ijma and qiyas, and the five categories from halal to haram, and assess a statement using Qur'an 5:48.
📖 Set passage · Qur'an 5:48
وَأَنزَلۡنَآ إِلَيۡكَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ بِٱلۡحَقِّ مُصَدِّقٗا لِّمَا بَيۡنَ يَدَيۡهِ مِنَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ وَمُهَيۡمِنًا عَلَيۡهِۖ فَٱحۡكُم بَيۡنَهُم بِمَآ أَنزَلَ ٱللَّهُۖ وَلَا تَتَّبِعۡ أَهۡوَآءَهُمۡ عَمَّا جَآءَكَ مِنَ ٱلۡحَقِّۚ لِكُلّٖ جَعَلۡنَا مِنكُمۡ شِرۡعَةٗ وَمِنۡهَاجٗاۚ وَلَوۡ شَآءَ ٱللَّهُ لَجَعَلَكُمۡ أُمَّةٗ وَٰحِدَةٗ وَلَٰكِن لِّيَبۡلُوَكُمۡ فِي مَآ ءَاتَىٰكُمۡۖ فَٱسۡتَبِقُواْ ٱلۡخَيۡرَٰتِۚ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ مَرۡجِعُكُمۡ جَمِيعٗا فَيُنَبِّئُكُم بِمَا كُنتُمۡ فِيهِ تَخۡتَلِفُونَ ﴿48﴾
(48) And We have revealed to you, [O Muḥammad], the Book [i.e., the Qur’ān] in truth, confirming that which preceded it of the Scripture and as a criterion over it. So judge between them by what Allāh has revealed and do not follow their inclinations away from what has come to you of the truth. To each of you We prescribed a law and a method. Had Allāh willed, He would have made you one nation [united in religion], but [He intended] to test you in what He has given you; so race to [all that is] good. To Allāh is your return all together, and He will [then] inform you concerning that over which you used to differ.
(a–b) The sources and the early community
• 📖 Qur'an: The first source: the word of Allah, «a criterion» over earlier books (5:48).
• 🧭 Sunnah: The second source: the Prophet ﷺ explained and applied the Qur'an.
• 🏛️ The early community: In Madinah the Shari'ah united the tribes under one law covering worship, family, trade and justice.
• ⚖️ Authority: «Obey Allah and obey the Messenger and those in authority» — disputes are referred to Allah and the Messenger (4:59).

(c) Ijma and qiyas
• 🤝 Ijma: When qualified scholars agree on a ruling, it becomes a source of law, e.g. the companions agreed to collect the Qur'an into one book.
• 🛡️ Why ijma matters: It keeps rulings united and stable and protects them from individual error.
• 🔍 Qiyas: When a new case is not mentioned, scholars compare it with a case that has the same cause ('illah): drugs are haram because, like wine, they intoxicate (5:90).
• 🌍 Why qiyas matters: It lets the Shari'ah guide Muslims on new issues while staying rooted in the Qur'an and Sunnah.
(d) Halal, haram and the five categories
• ✅ Fard: Compulsory: rewarded if done, sinful if left — the five daily prayers.
• 🌟 Mandub: Recommended: rewarded, not sinful if left — extra prayers, charity beyond zakah.
• ⚪ Mubah: Permitted: neither rewarded nor punished — choosing what to eat that is halal.
• 🟠 Makruh: Disliked: better avoided, not sinful — e.g. wasting water in wudu'.
• ⛔ Haram: Forbidden: sinful if done — alcohol, theft, backbiting.
• 🔑 Halal vs haram: Halal = permitted; haram = forbidden. The categories guide every action of a Muslim.
