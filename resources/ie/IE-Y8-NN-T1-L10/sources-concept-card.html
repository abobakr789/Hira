Sources & concepts: Prayers for Certain Occasions (Eclipse, Rain and Guidance)
Islamic Education · Grade 8 · Non-Arabic speakers · And Whoever Relies on Allah, He Is Sufficient for Him · Textbook V1 p.97
For all
Objective: To explain how to perform the eclipse, istikharah and rain prayers and deduce the wisdom behind them.
📖 Read the text
وَهُوَ ٱلَّذِي يُنَزِّلُ ٱلۡغَيۡثَ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ مَا قَنَطُواْ وَيَنشُرُ رَحۡمَتَهُۥۚ وَهُوَ ٱلۡوَلِيُّ ٱلۡحَمِيدُ ﴿28﴾
[Ash-Shuraa: 28–28]
📘 Key words
Kusufsolar eclipse
Istisqa'asking for rain
Istikharahasking Allah for the best choice
💡 Main ideas
1. He ﷺ said: «The sun and moon are two of Allah's signs; they do not eclipse for anyone's death or life. When you see it, call on Allah and pray.»
2. The eclipse prayer is two rak'ahs, each with two standings and two bowings, with long recitation.
3. The rain prayer: two rak'ahs asking for rain; people go out humbly, give charity and seek forgiveness.
4. Istikharah: two rak'ahs then the reported du'a when unsure about a permissible matter, along with consulting experienced people.
✅ Lessons we take
1. Turn to prayer when signs and needs appear.
2. Seeking forgiveness brings rain.
3. Istikharah is entrusting your choice to Allah.
🇦🇪 National identity: The UAE holds rain prayers in times of drought, alongside water conservation projects.
✉️ Lesson message: I pray for special causes
