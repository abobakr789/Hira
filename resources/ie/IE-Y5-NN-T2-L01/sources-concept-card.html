Sources & concepts: Surat Abasa
Islamic Education · Grade 5 · Non-Arabic speakers · Do Good Deeds, for Allah Will See Your Deeds · Textbook V1 p.124
For all
Objective: To prove from Surat 'Abasa that Islam is a religion of equality and explain Allah's power in creating plants.
📖 Read the text
عَبَسَ وَتَوَلَّىٰٓ ﴿1﴾ أَن جَآءَهُ ٱلۡأَعۡمَىٰ ﴿2﴾ وَمَا يُدۡرِيكَ لَعَلَّهُۥ يَزَّكَّىٰٓ ﴿3﴾ أَوۡ يَذَّكَّرُ فَتَنفَعَهُ ٱلذِّكۡرَىٰٓ ﴿4﴾ أَمَّا مَنِ ٱسۡتَغۡنَىٰ ﴿5﴾ فَأَنتَ لَهُۥ تَصَدَّىٰ ﴿6﴾ وَمَا عَلَيۡكَ أَلَّا يَزَّكَّىٰ ﴿7﴾ وَأَمَّا مَن جَآءَكَ يَسۡعَىٰ ﴿8﴾ وَهُوَ يَخۡشَىٰ ﴿9﴾ فَأَنتَ عَنۡهُ تَلَهَّىٰ ﴿10﴾
['Abasa: 1–10]
📘 Key words
عبس ('Abasa)frowned
As-Sakhkhahthe deafening blast
💡 Main ideas
1. 'Abdullah ibn Umm Maktum, who was blind, came seeking knowledge. The Prophet ﷺ frowned, busy inviting Quraysh's chiefs, and Allah gently corrected him.
2. Islam treats people equally: a poor believer seeking knowledge deserves attention.
3. Look at your food: Allah sent rain and grew grain, grapes, olives, palms and fruit.
4. On the Day of Judgement a person will flee from his brother, mother and father; each will be busy with himself.
✅ Lessons we take
1. Respect everyone.
🇦🇪 National identity: 'Abasa teaches respecting every person, as the UAE does with everyone living on its land.
