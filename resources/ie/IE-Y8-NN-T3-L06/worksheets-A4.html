[Ya-Sin: 69–72]
“And We did not give Prophet Muhammad, knowledge of poetry, nor is it befitting for him. It is not but a message and a clear Qur'an” (69) “To warn whoever is alive and justify the word against the disbelievers” (70) “Do they not see that We have created for them from what Our hands have made, grazing livestock, and [then] they are their owners” (71) “And We have tamed them for them, so some of them they ride, and some of them they eat” (72)
