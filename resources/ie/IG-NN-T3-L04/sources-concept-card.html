📖 Set passage · Qur'an 9:58–60
وَمِنۡهُم مَّن يَلۡمِزُكَ فِي ٱلصَّدَقَٰتِ فَإِنۡ أُعۡطُواْ مِنۡهَا رَضُواْ وَإِن لَّمۡ يُعۡطَوۡاْ مِنۡهَآ إِذَا هُمۡ يَسۡخَطُونَ ﴿58﴾ وَلَوۡ أَنَّهُمۡ رَضُواْ مَآ ءَاتَىٰهُمُ ٱللَّهُ وَرَسُولُهُۥ وَقَالُواْ حَسۡبُنَا ٱللَّهُ سَيُؤۡتِينَا ٱللَّهُ مِن فَضۡلِهِۦ وَرَسُولُهُۥٓ إِنَّآ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ رَٰغِبُونَ ﴿59﴾ ۞إِنَّمَا ٱلصَّدَقَٰتُ لِلۡفُقَرَآءِ وَٱلۡمَسَٰكِينِ وَٱلۡعَٰمِلِينَ عَلَيۡهَا وَٱلۡمُؤَلَّفَةِ قُلُوبُهُمۡ وَفِي ٱلرِّقَابِ وَٱلۡغَٰرِمِينَ وَفِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ وَٱبۡنِ ٱلسَّبِيلِۖ فَرِيضَةٗ مِّنَ ٱللَّهِۗ وَٱللَّهُ عَلِيمٌ حَكِيمٞ ﴿60﴾
(58) And among them are some who criticize you concerning the [distribution of] charities. If they are given from them, they approve; but if they are not given from them, at once they become angry.
(59) If only they had been satisfied with what Allāh and His Messenger gave them and said, "Sufficient for us is Allāh; Allāh will give us of His bounty, and [so will] His Messenger; indeed, we are desirous toward Allāh," [it would have been better for them].
(60) Zakāh expenditures are only for the poor and for the needy and for those employed for it and for bringing hearts together [for Islām] and for freeing captives [or slaves] and for those in debt and for the cause of Allāh and for the [stranded] traveler - an obligation [imposed] by Allāh. And Allāh is Knowing and Wise.
(a) Zakah: nature and purpose
• 🏛️ Nature: The third pillar: 2.5% of savings held above the nisab for one lunar year, paid every year.
• 🧼 Purification: «Take from their wealth a charity by which you purify them» (9:103): it cleans wealth and the heart from greed.
• 👥 The eight groups: The poor, the needy, collectors, reconciling hearts, freeing captives, debtors, the cause of Allah, travellers (9:60).
• 🤲 Purpose: Wealth belongs to Allah; Zakah reduces poverty and narrows the gap between rich and poor.

(b) Khums: nature and purpose
• 🖐️ Nature: «One fifth» (8:41): Shi'a Muslims pay 20% of their surplus income after a year's expenses.
• 📚 Two shares: Sahm al-Imam: for religious work, scholars and schools; Sahm as-Sadat: for poor descendants of the Prophet ﷺ.
• 🧭 Paid through: Usually given to a qualified religious scholar (marja') to distribute.
• 🎯 Purpose: Purifies wealth, supports religious learning and cares for those in need.
(c–d) Why they matter — locally and globally
• 🙏 Obedience: Commanded with Salah: «establish prayer and give zakah» (2:43).
• 💗 The heart: Defeats greed and teaches gratitude.
• ⚖️ Justice: Wealth circulates and the poor have a right to it.
• 🤝 Ummah: Builds brotherhood between rich and poor.
• 🌍 Global need: Muslim charities send Zakah to famine, war and disaster areas worldwide.
• 🕊️ Humanity: Sadaqah and aid help all people in need, Muslim or not.
