⚖️Surat Al-Mutaffifin: woe to those who give short measure, while the righteous are in bliss.
📖 I read the text
وَيۡلٞ لِّلۡمُطَفِّفِينَ ﴿1﴾ ٱلَّذِينَ إِذَا ٱكۡتَالُواْ عَلَى ٱلنَّاسِ يَسۡتَوۡفُونَ ﴿2﴾ وَإِذَا كَالُوهُمۡ أَو وَّزَنُوهُمۡ يُخۡسِرُونَ ﴿3﴾ أَلَا يَظُنُّ أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ أَنَّهُم مَّبۡعُوثُونَ ﴿4﴾ لِيَوۡمٍ عَظِيمٖ ﴿5﴾ يَوۡمَ يَقُومُ ٱلنَّاسُ لِرَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ ﴿6﴾
[Al-Mutaffifin: 1–6]
“Woe to those who give less [than due]” “Who, when they take a measure from people, take in full” “But if they give by measure or by weight to them, they cause loss” “Do they not think that they will be resurrected” “For a tremendous Day” “The Day when mankind will stand before the Lord of the world

💡 I learned
⚖️The cheaters take their full right, but give others less.
⚠️They will be raised on a mighty Day, when people stand before the Lord of the worlds.
📕The record of the wicked is in Sijjin; the record of the righteous is in 'Illiyyin.
🌳The righteous are in bliss, on couches, drinking from Tasnim.
✅ I apply
☐I am honest when buying and selling.
☐I give people their full right.
☐I work to be among the righteous.
🇦🇪 My country: UAE authorities inspect market scales to protect consumers from cheating.
🏅 I am honest and fair
