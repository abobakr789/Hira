(a) Tawhid: the oneness of Allah

Spec 2.1 a

⏱ 8 min

☝️ One God

Allah is One, with no partner, child or equal (112).

🧱 The foundation

Tawhid is the first part of the Shahadah and the message of every prophet.

🙏 Worship Him alone

All worship — prayer, du'a, sacrifice — is for Allah alone.

🌍 Why it matters

It gives life one purpose, makes all people equal servants of Allah, and frees them from fear of anything else.

Spec 2.1 b · d

⏱ 8 min

💪 Omnipotent

«Allah is over all things competent» (2:20): nothing is beyond His power.

🌌 Creator

«Allah is the Creator of all things» (39:62): He made and sustains everything.

💚 Merciful

⚖️ Just

«Allah does not do injustice, even as much as an atom's weight» (4:40): everyone will be judged fairly.

(c) Shirk and nifaq

Spec 2.1 c

⏱ 8 min

🚫 Shirk

Associating partners with Allah, e.g. worshipping idols or believing others share His power. «Allah does not forgive association with Him» (4:48) — the gravest sin if one dies on it.

🎭 Nifaq

Hypocrisy: appearing to believe while hiding disbelief. «The hypocrites will be in the lowest depths of the Fire» (4:145).

⚠️ Signs of a hypocrite

«When he speaks he lies, when he promises he breaks it, and when he is trusted he betrays» (Bukhari).

🛡️ Why it matters

Both destroy Tawhid and sincerity; Muslims guard their hearts against them.

(c) Explain the meaning and importance of Tawhid for Muslims. (6)

Point 1 + why

Tawhid means Allah is One with no partner or equal (112:1–4). It is important because it is the first part of the Shahadah, so it is the foundation of Islam.

Point 2 + why

Because Allah is One, all worship must be for Him alone. This shapes every act of a Muslim's life, from prayer to du'a, and guards against shirk, the gravest sin.

Point 3 + why

Tawhid makes all people equal servants of the same God. This is why Islam rejects racism and pride, and why Muslims trust Allah alone in hardship.

Plan a (d) answer: June 2025, Q4(d)

12 marks · four elements

⏱ 6 min

«Believing in the oneness of Allah is what makes a proper Muslim.» Assess this statement. Refer to Qur'an 112:1–4.

✅ Agree (with the text)

❌ Another point of view

112:1: «He is Allah, One» — Tawhid is the first pillar and the basis of faith.

A proper Muslim must also act on belief: the five pillars, e.g. Salah and Sawm.

112:3–4: nothing is equal to Him; belief in Him alone rules out shirk, the gravest sin.

Good character and sincerity (avoiding nifaq) are also needed.

⚖️ Conclusion: Tawhid is the essential foundation, but a proper Muslim shows it through worship, good character and sincerity.

Model (d) answer · Level 3

Read the tags

⏱ 6 min

Text + agree

Many Muslims agree. Surah 112 begins «He is Allah, [who is] One», and the Shahadah begins «there is no god but Allah». Without Tawhid nothing else in Islam has meaning.

Reason

Verses 3–4 say He «neither begets nor is born, nor is there to Him any equivalent». Believing this protects a Muslim from shirk, which Allah does not forgive if one dies on it (4:48).

Other view

However, others argue that belief alone is not enough. A proper Muslim must act on it through the pillars, such as Salah five times a day and fasting in Ramadan.

Reason

The Qur'an also warns about the hypocrites who claim faith but do not live by it (4:145), so sincerity and character matter as well.

Conclusion

Overall, belief in the oneness of Allah is what makes a person Muslim, but being a 'proper' Muslim means living that belief in worship and character. So the statement is true as a foundation, but incomplete.
