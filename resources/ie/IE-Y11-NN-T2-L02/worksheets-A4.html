[Al-Hujurat: 6–6]
“O you who have believed, if there comes to you a disobedient one with information, investigate, lest you harm a people out of ignorance and become, over what you have done, regretful” (6)
Sanad: chainMawdu': fabricated
1An authentic (sahih) hadith is:
one with a connected chain of upright, precise narrators without irregularity or defectone narrated by a single personone famous among peopleeverything attributed to the Prophet ﷺ
2A fabricated (mawdu') hadith is:
one falsely attributed to the Prophet ﷺauthenticgoodmass-transmitted
3«Dabt» for hadith scholars means:
precision and accuracy in memoryuprightnessnarrating a lottravel
