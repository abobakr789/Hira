Silver

1: 🏛️ The second pillar: Compulsory five times a day for every adult Muslim. · 💬 Direct link: A direct connection with Allah with no intermediary; it is remembrance of Him. · 🛡️ Protection: «Prayer prohibits immorality and wrongdoing» (29:45). · 🕌 Unity and discipline: Muslims face one Qiblah and pray in rows; prayer in congregation is 27 times better (Bukhari, Muslim). · 💧 Ablution: Wudu': wash the face, arms to the elbows, wipe the head, wash the feet (5:6). · 🕰️ Times: Fajr (dawn), Dhuhr (midday), Asr (afternoon), Maghrib (sunset), Isha (night). · 🧭 Direction: Facing the Qiblah, the Ka'bah in Makkah (2:144). · 🧎 Movements: Standing, bowing (ruku'), standing up, prostrating twice (sujud), sitting.
2 (2 marks): Any two: face · arms to the elbows · feet (the head is wiped) (5:6).
3 (3 marks): Muslims first perform wudu' and face the Qiblah towards the Ka'bah. They begin with «Allahu akbar», recite al-Fatihah standing, then bow and prostrate twice. They sit for the tashahhud and finish by turning their head right and left saying «as-salamu 'alaykum».
4 (3 marks): Children before puberty are not required to pray, though they are taught from seven (1); women during menstruation are exempt (1); the sick are not exempt but may pray sitting or lying down (1).

Gold

1–2 (model answer): Salah is the second pillar and the first deed a Muslim is commanded to keep five times a day, so it shapes the whole day around Allah. It is a direct connection with Allah that strengthens faith and protects from sin (29:45). Praying in congregation, especially at Jumu'ah (62:9), unites Muslims in rows facing one Qiblah, showing equality.

Diamond

Indicative content: Agree: verse 39 — prophets «fear none but Allah»: persecution tested and strengthened faith (Bilal's «Ahad, Ahad»; Sumayyah's sacrifice); hardship bound the believers together and prepared them for Madinah. Other view: the opposition also caused suffering and loss — Sumayyah was killed and some Muslims had to leave for Abyssinia; verse 38 shows that strength comes from following «the established way of Allah», not from persecution itself; Allah's help and the Prophet's ﷺ leadership kept them firm. Conclusion: a justified judgement, e.g. opposition revealed and deepened their faith, but it was faith in Allah, not the opposition itself, that made them strong. Mark with the Level 1–3 descriptors (Level 3 needs the text, another view and a justified conclusion).
