🎯 Explain the importance of the Prophet's ﷺ teachings, deeds and sayings, describe how the Sunnah was recorded and explain its role in Muslim belief and practice, using Qur'an 33:36.
📖 Set passage · Qur'an 33:36
وَمَا كَانَ لِمُؤۡمِنٖ وَلَا مُؤۡمِنَةٍ إِذَا قَضَى ٱللَّهُ وَرَسُولُهُۥٓ أَمۡرًا أَن يَكُونَ لَهُمُ ٱلۡخِيَرَةُ مِنۡ أَمۡرِهِمۡۗ وَمَن يَعۡصِ ٱللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُۥ فَقَدۡ ضَلَّ ضَلَٰلٗا مُّبِينٗا ﴿36﴾
(36) It is not for a believing man or a believing woman, when Allah and His Messenger have decided a matter, that they should [thereafter] have any choice about their affair. And whoever disobeys Allah and His Messenger has certainly strayed into clear error.
(a) Why his teachings and example matter
• ✅ Commanded: «Whatever the Messenger has given you — take» (59:7); obeying him is obeying Allah (33:36).
• 🌟 Best example: «There has certainly been for you in the Messenger of Allah an excellent example» (33:21).
• 📖 Explains the Qur'an: «We revealed to you the message that you may make clear to the people what was sent down to them» (16:44).
• ❤️ Love: Following his way is a sign of loving Allah and His Messenger.

(b) How the Sunnah was recorded
• 🗣️ Memory: Companions memorised his words and actions and taught them to others.
• ✍️ Writing: Some wrote hadith in his lifetime, e.g. Abdullah ibn Amr (Bukhari).
• 📜 Collection: About a century later, the caliph Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz ordered scholars to gather hadith (Bukhari).
• 🔍 Checking: Scholars checked each isnad (chain) and matn (text) and graded hadith, e.g. sahih; al-Bukhari and Muslim compiled the most trusted collections.
(c) The role of the Sunnah in belief and practice
• 🕌 Worship: It shows how to pray: «Pray as you have seen me praying» (Bukhari); also zakah amounts and the rites of Hajj.
• ⚖️ Law: It is the second source of Shari'ah after the Qur'an.
• 🤝 Character: It teaches manners, honesty and kindness — the Muslim identity.
• 🧩 Unity: One shared example gave the early community a clear, common way of life.
