Silver

1: ✅ Commanded: «Whatever the Messenger has given you — take» (59:7); obeying him is obeying Allah (33:36). · 🌟 Best example: «There has certainly been for you in the Messenger of Allah an excellent example» (33:21). · 📖 Explains the Qur'an: «We revealed to you the message that you may make clear to the people what was sent down to them» (16:44). · ❤️ Love: Following his way is a sign of loving Allah and His Messenger. · 🗣️ Memory: Companions memorised his words and actions and taught them to others. · ✍️ Writing: Some wrote hadith in his lifetime, e.g. Abdullah ibn Amr (Bukhari). · 📜 Collection: About a century later, the caliph Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz ordered scholars to gather hadith (Bukhari). · 🔍 Checking: Scholars checked each isnad (chain) and matn (text) and graded hadith, e.g. sahih; al-Bukhari and Muslim compiled the most trusted collections.
2 (2 marks): Sahih al-Bukhari; Sahih Muslim (also accept: Sunan Abi Dawud, Jami' al-Tirmidhi, Sunan al-Nasa'i, Sunan Ibn Majah).
3 (3 marks): The Sunnah is the way of the Prophet ﷺ: his sayings, actions and approvals. It is recorded in hadith, each with a chain of narrators (isnad) and a text (matn). It is the second source of Islam after the Qur'an and explains how to apply it.
4 (3 marks): They studied the isnad, the chain of narrators back to the Prophet ﷺ (1); they checked that each narrator was honest and had a good memory, and that the chain was connected (1); they compared the matn with the Qur'an and other hadith and graded it, e.g. sahih (1).

Gold

1–2 (model answer): Allah commands Muslims to obey the Messenger (33:36; 59:7), so following the Sunnah is an act of obedience to Allah. The Sunnah explains the Qur'an (16:44): without it Muslims would not know how to pray or give zakah, so it turns belief into practice. The Prophet ﷺ is the best example (33:21), so his character guides how Muslims behave with family, neighbours and in business.

Diamond

Indicative content: Agree: verse 39 — prophets «fear none but Allah»: persecution tested and strengthened faith (Bilal's «Ahad, Ahad»; Sumayyah's sacrifice); hardship bound the believers together and prepared them for Madinah. Other view: the opposition also caused suffering and loss — Sumayyah was killed and some Muslims had to leave for Abyssinia; verse 38 shows that strength comes from following «the established way of Allah», not from persecution itself; Allah's help and the Prophet's ﷺ leadership kept them firm. Conclusion: a justified judgement, e.g. opposition revealed and deepened their faith, but it was faith in Allah, not the opposition itself, that made them strong. Mark with the Level 1–3 descriptors (Level 3 needs the text, another view and a justified conclusion).
