Key learning points

Building understanding

1

Love of homeland is natural: the Prophet ﷺ said of Makkah: «How good you are as a land and how beloved to me; had my people not driven me out, I would not have lived elsewhere.»

2

Protecting the homeland is a religious duty: «Two eyes the Fire will not touch: an eye that wept for fear of Allah and an eye that guarded in the way of Allah.»

3

National service in the UAE: a 2014 law that builds belonging, discipline, fitness and readiness to protect the nation and its achievements.

4

Love of country: obeying those in authority, respecting laws, protecting property, excelling in study and sacrifice, like the UAE's martyrs.
