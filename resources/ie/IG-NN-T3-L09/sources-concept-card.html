📜 Set passage · Hadith «Every one of you is a guardian»
“Every one of you is a guardian and is responsible for his charges. The ruler who has authority over people is a guardian and is responsible for them; a man is a guardian of his family and is responsible for them… a woman is a guardian of her husband's house and children and is responsible for them.”
Bukhari (narrated by Abdullah ibn Umar)
(a) Leadership in Islam
• 🤲 A trust: Leadership is an amanah from Allah, not a privilege; leaders will be questioned (Bukhari).
• ⚖️ Justice: «When you judge between people, judge with justice» (4:58).
• 🗣️ Consultation: «Their affairs are by consultation among them» (42:38); «consult them in the matter» (3:159).
• 🕌 Religious and political: Imams and scholars guide in faith; rulers keep order and justice; both serve the people.

(b–c) Challenges today · citizenship
• 🌍 Leaders' challenges: A diverse Ummah, extremism, misrepresentation in the media, and applying Islam to new issues.
• 🏛️ In non-Muslim states: Imams represent Muslims, work with other faiths and guide young people online.
• 📜 Citizens' duties: Keep the law and agreements (5:1), be honest, pay taxes, serve the community.
• ⚠️ Citizens' challenges: Prejudice, practising faith at school or work, halal food, and laws that may conflict with faith — obey unless it is disobedience to Allah (Bukhari, Muslim).
(d) The Prophet ﷺ and his successors as models
• 🌟 The Prophet ﷺ: Mercy, consultation (3:159), the Constitution of Madinah, justice for all.
• 🤝 Abu Bakr: Humility: «obey me as long as I obey Allah» — kept the community united.
• ⚖️ Umar: Justice and accountability; organised the state.
• 📖 Uthman: Generosity; preserved the Qur'an in one standard Mushaf.
• 🧠 Ali: Knowledge, courage and wisdom in judgement.
• 🔑 Today's lesson: Serve, consult, be just and stay humble before Allah.
