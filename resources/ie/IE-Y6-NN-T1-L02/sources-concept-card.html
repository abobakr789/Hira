Objective: To explain the advice in the hadith and the importance of applying it in a Muslim's life.
📖 Read the text · Textbook V1 p.24
«يَا غُلَامُ، إِنِّي أُعَلِّمُكَ كَلِمَاتٍ: احْفَظِ اللَّهَ يَحْفَظْكَ، احْفَظِ اللَّهَ تَجِدْهُ تُجَاهَكَ، إِذَا سَأَلْتَ فَاسْأَلِ اللَّهَ، وَإِذَا اسْتَعَنْتَ فَاسْتَعِنْ بِاللَّهِ...»
"Young man, I will teach you some words: Be mindful of Allah and He will protect you. Be mindful of Allah and you will find Him in front of you. When you ask, ask Allah; and when you seek help, seek help from Allah..."
سنن الترمذي · قال الترمذي: حسن صحيح
احفظ الله (Ihfaz Allah): be mindful of Allah's commands
📘 Key words
احفظ الله (Ihfaz Allah)be mindful of Allah's commands
💡 Main ideas
1. He said: «Young man, I will teach you some words: be mindful of Allah and He will protect you; be mindful of Allah and you will find Him before you.»
2. «When you ask, ask Allah; when you seek help, seek help from Allah.»
3. «Know that if the whole nation gathered to benefit you, they could only benefit you with what Allah has written for you.»
4. «The pens have been lifted and the pages have dried»: everything is by Allah's decree.
✅ Lessons we take
1. Obey Allah and He protects you.
2. Ask Allah first.
🇦🇪 National identity: «Be mindful of Allah and He will protect you» builds confidence, felt by Emiratis in a safe homeland.
✉️ Lesson message: I guard Allah's commands, so He guards me ️
