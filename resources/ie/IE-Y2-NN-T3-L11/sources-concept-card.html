For all
🐎Surat Al-'Adiyat: people can be ungrateful to their Lord and love wealth too much, but Allah knows everything they do.
📖 I read the text
وَٱلۡعَٰدِيَٰتِ ضَبۡحٗا ﴿1﴾ فَٱلۡمُورِيَٰتِ قَدۡحٗا ﴿2﴾ فَٱلۡمُغِيرَٰتِ صُبۡحٗا ﴿3﴾ فَأَثَرۡنَ بِهِۦ نَقۡعٗا ﴿4﴾ فَوَسَطۡنَ بِهِۦ جَمۡعًا ﴿5﴾ إِنَّ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ لِرَبِّهِۦ لَكَنُودٞ ﴿6﴾ وَإِنَّهُۥ عَلَىٰ ذَٰلِكَ لَشَهِيدٞ ﴿7﴾ وَإِنَّهُۥ لِحُبِّ ٱلۡخَيۡرِ لَشَدِيدٌ ﴿8﴾
[Al-'Adiyat: 1–8]
“By the racers, panting” “And the producers of sparks [when] striking” “And the chargers at dawn” “Stirring up thereby [clouds of] dust” “Arriving thereby in the center collectively” “Indeed mankind, to his Lord, is ungrateful” “And indeed, he is to that a witness” “And indeed he is, in love of weal

💡 I learned
🐎Allah swore by the horses galloping, striking sparks and raising dust.
😔People can be ungrateful to their Lord, and they know it.
💰They love wealth very much.
👁️On the Day of Judgement, what is in the graves and hearts will come out, and Allah knows everything.
✅ I apply
☐I thank Allah for all His blessings.
☐I share what I have with others.
☐I remember that Allah sees everything I do.
