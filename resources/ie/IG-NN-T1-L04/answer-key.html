Silver

1: 🚫 The boycott: Quraysh agreed not to trade with or marry into Banu Hashim. The Muslims and the Prophet's ﷺ clan suffered hunger in the valley of Abu Talib for about three years. · 💔 The Year of Sorrow: Soon after the boycott ended, Khadijah and Abu Talib died, leaving him without his greatest supporters. · 🌌 Isra': Allah took him by night from al-Masjid al-Haram to al-Masjid al-Aqsa (17:1). · 🕌 Mi'raj: He was raised through the heavens; the five daily prayers were made obligatory (Bukhari). · 🗡️ The plot: Quraysh chose a young man from each clan to kill him together: «when the disbelievers plotted against you… to kill you» (8:30). · 🛏️ Ali's courage: Ali slept in the Prophet's ﷺ bed so he could leave safely. · ⛰️ Cave of Thawr: He and Abu Bakr hid in the cave: «the second of two, when they were in the cave… Do not grieve; indeed Allah is with us» (9:40). · 🐪 To Madinah: Guided by a hired guide, they took an unusual route and reached Quba, then Yathrib — later called Madinah.
2 (2 marks): Any two: he slept in his bed on the night of the Hijrah · he fought bravely to protect him · he was one of the first to accept Islam and supported the spread of Islam.
3 (3 marks): At night the Prophet ﷺ was taken from Makkah to al-Masjid al-Aqsa in Jerusalem (the Isra', 17:1). From there he was raised up through the heavens (the Mi'raj) and met earlier prophets. During the Mi'raj the five daily prayers were made obligatory for Muslims.
4 (3 marks): The people of Madinah came out to greet him and invited him to stay with them (1); he built the mosque at Quba and then the Prophet's Mosque (1); he joined the Muhajirun and Ansar as brothers, and the Ansar shared their homes and wealth (1).

Gold

1–2 (model answer): The companions protected the Prophet ﷺ: Ali risked his life on the night of the Hijrah and Abu Bakr travelled with him, so the mission could continue. Their wealth and sacrifice supported the weak, for example Abu Bakr freeing Bilal and Uthman funding the army, which kept the community strong. The Ansar gave the Muslims a home, shared their property and protected the Prophet ﷺ, so Islam had a safe base from which to grow into a community and a state.

Diamond

Indicative content: Agree: verse 39 — prophets «fear none but Allah»: persecution tested and strengthened faith (Bilal's «Ahad, Ahad»; Sumayyah's sacrifice); hardship bound the believers together and prepared them for Madinah. Other view: the opposition also caused suffering and loss — Sumayyah was killed and some Muslims had to leave for Abyssinia; verse 38 shows that strength comes from following «the established way of Allah», not from persecution itself; Allah's help and the Prophet's ﷺ leadership kept them firm. Conclusion: a justified judgement, e.g. opposition revealed and deepened their faith, but it was faith in Allah, not the opposition itself, that made them strong. Mark with the Level 1–3 descriptors (Level 3 needs the text, another view and a justified conclusion).
