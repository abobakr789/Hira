📜 Set passage · Hadith on intentions
“Actions are (judged) by motives (niyyah), so each man will have what he intended. Thus, he whose migration (hijrah) was to Allah and His Messenger, his migration is to Allah and His Messenger; but he whose migration was for some worldly thing he might gain, or for a wife he might marry, his migration is to that for which he migrated.”
Bukhari and Muslim (narrated by Umar ibn al-Khattab)
(a) The boycott and the Mi'raj
• 🚫 The boycott: Quraysh agreed not to trade with or marry into Banu Hashim. The Muslims and the Prophet's ﷺ clan suffered hunger in the valley of Abu Talib for about three years.
• 💔 The Year of Sorrow: Soon after the boycott ended, Khadijah and Abu Talib died, leaving him without his greatest supporters.
• 🌌 Isra': Allah took him by night from al-Masjid al-Haram to al-Masjid al-Aqsa (17:1).
• 🕌 Mi'raj: He was raised through the heavens; the five daily prayers were made obligatory (Bukhari).

(b) The plot and the flight
• 🗡️ The plot: Quraysh chose a young man from each clan to kill him together: «when the disbelievers plotted against you… to kill you» (8:30).
• 🛏️ Ali's courage: Ali slept in the Prophet's ﷺ bed so he could leave safely.
• ⛰️ Cave of Thawr: He and Abu Bakr hid in the cave: «the second of two, when they were in the cave… Do not grieve; indeed Allah is with us» (9:40).
• 🐪 To Madinah: Guided by a hired guide, they took an unusual route and reached Quba, then Yathrib — later called Madinah.
(c) Arrival in Madinah
• 🕌 Mosques: He built the mosque at Quba, then the Prophet's Mosque in Madinah — centres of worship, learning and community.
• 🤝 Brotherhood: He joined each emigrant with a helper as brothers; the Ansar shared homes and wealth.
• 📜 Community: Muslims could now worship freely and live as a community with its own leadership.
• 📅 The calendar: Umar later chose the Hijrah as the start of the Islamic calendar.
(d) The companions who supported him
• Abu Bakr: First adult man to believe; spent his wealth, freed Bilal, his companion in the Hijrah.
• Ali: Grew up in his house; risked his life in the Prophet's ﷺ bed; brave in battle.
• Umar: His conversion made the Muslims stronger: «We have been strong since Umar accepted Islam» (Ibn Mas'ud, Bukhari).
• Uthman: Married the Prophet's ﷺ daughter Ruqayyah, migrated to Abyssinia and spent generously, e.g. on the army of Tabuk.
• The Ansar: Welcomed and sheltered the emigrants and protected the Prophet ﷺ.
• Why it mattered: Without their sacrifice and protection the community could not have survived.
