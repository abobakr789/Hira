[Qaf: 16–19]
“And We have already created man and know what his soul whispers to him, and We are closer to him than [his] jugular vein” (16) “When the two receivers receive, seated on the right and on the left” (17) “Man does not utter any word except that with him is an observer prepared [to record]” (18) “And the intoxication of death will bring the truth; that is what you were trying to avoid” (19)
حبل الوريد (Habl al-warid): jugular veinرقيب عتيد (Raqib 'atid): an observer ready to record
1﴿Not a word does he say but there is a watcher ready﴾ means:
Every word a person says is written downSpeech is not countedAngels only record actionsRecording only happens on the Day of Judgement
2«Sakrat al-mawt» means:
the pains and hardship of deathdeep sleepa light illnessa long journey
3«Raqib 'atid» means:
an angel present who records every worda close frienda loyal neighboura city guard
