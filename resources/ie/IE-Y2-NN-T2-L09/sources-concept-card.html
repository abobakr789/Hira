Objective: To explain the lineage of Fatimah and her role in protecting the Prophet ﷺ.

📘 Key words
Az-Zahra'one of Fatimah's titles
💡 Main ideas
1. Her father was the Prophet ﷺ and her mother Khadijah. She married Ali ibn Abi Talib, and her children were Al-Hasan, Al-Husayn, Zaynab and Umm Kulthum.
2. When she came in, the Prophet ﷺ stood up, kissed her and seated her in his place.
3. She bravely removed the filth thrown on the Prophet's ﷺ back while he was prostrating.
4. He taught her to say before sleep: Subhanallah 33, Alhamdulillah 33, Allahu Akbar 34.
✅ Lessons we take
1. Love the Prophet's ﷺ family.
🇦🇪 National identity: Fatimah loved her father and served her family; we honour our parents.
