Objective: To distinguish between the types of waqf and explain its role in serving society.
📖 Read the text
لَن تَنَالُواْ ٱلۡبِرَّ حَتَّىٰ تُنفِقُواْ مِمَّا تُحِبُّونَۚ وَمَا تُنفِقُواْ مِن شَيۡءٖ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ بِهِۦ عَلِيمٞ ﴿92﴾
[Ali 'Imran: 92–92]
📘 Key words
Waqfendowment
صدقة جارية (Sadaqah jariyah)ongoing charity
💡 Main ideas
1. Waqf means holding an asset from sale and using its benefit for good; the Prophet ﷺ told Umar: «If you wish, keep its origin and give its produce in charity.»
2. Uthman bought the well of Rumah and dedicated it to the Muslims; Umar's land at Khaybar was the first waqf in Islam.
3. Types: charitable (for public causes), family (for relatives and descendants), and mixed.
4. In the UAE, the General Authority of Islamic Affairs and Endowments manages awqaf for education, health and mosques.
✅ Lessons we take
1. Waqf is charity that never stops.
2. Anyone can share, even with a little.
🇦🇪 National identity: The UAE launched major endowments such as the 1 Billion Meals Endowment.
