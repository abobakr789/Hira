Silver

1: 🕊️ Salam: Spread the greeting of peace and return it with something better (4:86). · Respect: Respect elders, show mercy to the young, and honour parents. · 🏠 Neighbours: Be generous and kind to neighbours, whatever their faith. · 🤝 Equality: No one is better by race or tribe; «the most noble of you… is the most righteous» (49:13). · 🚫 Lying: «Truthfulness leads to righteousness… lying leads to wickedness» (Bukhari). · 😠 Mocking: «Let not a people ridicule [another] people» (49:11). · 🗣️ Backbiting: Compared to eating your dead brother's flesh (49:12). · 🤐 Say good or be silent: «Whoever believes in Allah and the Last Day, let him speak good or remain silent» (Bukhari).
2 (2 marks): Any two: lying · backbiting · mocking · insulting names · spreading suspicion.
3 (3 marks): They begin by saying «Bismillah» and eat with the right hand, as the Prophet ﷺ taught. They eat from what is in front of them and avoid waste, following «eat and drink, but be not excessive» (7:31). They thank Allah after eating and do not criticise the food, following the Prophet's ﷺ example.
4 (3 marks): Lying is forbidden and leads to wickedness, while truthfulness leads to righteousness (Bukhari) (1); lying is a sign of a hypocrite (1); Muslims are told to speak good or remain silent (1).

Gold

1–2 (model answer): Good manners are commanded in the Qur'an (49:11–13) and modelled by the Prophet ﷺ, so living by them is obedience to Allah. They build trust and unity in families and communities, preventing the harm caused by lies and backbiting. They also present Islam in the best light to others and are part of what Muslims believe will be weighed on the Day of Judgement.

Diamond

Indicative content: Agree: verse 39 — prophets «fear none but Allah»: persecution tested and strengthened faith (Bilal's «Ahad, Ahad»; Sumayyah's sacrifice); hardship bound the believers together and prepared them for Madinah. Other view: the opposition also caused suffering and loss — Sumayyah was killed and some Muslims had to leave for Abyssinia; verse 38 shows that strength comes from following «the established way of Allah», not from persecution itself; Allah's help and the Prophet's ﷺ leadership kept them firm. Conclusion: a justified judgement, e.g. opposition revealed and deepened their faith, but it was faith in Allah, not the opposition itself, that made them strong. Mark with the Level 1–3 descriptors (Level 3 needs the text, another view and a justified conclusion).
