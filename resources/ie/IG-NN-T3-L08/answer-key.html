Silver

1: 💞 Significance: A sign of Allah: «tranquillity… affection and mercy» (30:21); the Prophet ﷺ married and encouraged it (Bukhari, Muslim). · 📜 The nikah: A contract with offer and acceptance, the consent of both, witnesses and usually the bride's wali. · 🎁 The mahr: A gift owed by the husband to the wife; it is hers (4:4). · ⚖️ Rights: Kindness, support and respect on both sides; the woman's consent is required (Bukhari); the walimah announces the marriage. · 🏡 Raising children in faith: Parents teach children Islam, prayer and good character. · 🤲 Honouring parents: «To his parents, good treatment» (46:15); care for them in old age. · 🛡️ Security: A safe place of love, mercy and support (30:21). · 👶 Procreation and the Ummah: «Marry the loving and fertile, for I will be proud of your great numbers» (Abu Dawud): children raised in faith strengthen the Ummah.
2 (2 marks): Any two: adhan in the ear · tahnik · naming · shaving the head · aqiqah.
3 (3 marks): The body is washed (ghusl) and wrapped in simple white cloths. The community prays the funeral prayer (Salat al-Janazah). The body is buried quickly, facing the Qiblah, and the family mourns for three days.
4 (3 marks): It is a sign of Allah, bringing tranquillity, affection and mercy (30:21) (1); it follows the Sunnah of the Prophet ﷺ (1); it is the foundation of the family and the right place to raise children (1).

Gold

1–2 (model answer): Children continue the Muslim community into the next generation, and the Prophet ﷺ encouraged marriage and children. When parents raise children in faith, teaching prayer and character, the Ummah grows in strength, not only in number. Strong, loving families (30:21) produce good citizens and future leaders who serve Islam and society.

Diamond

Indicative content: Agree: verse 39 — prophets «fear none but Allah»: persecution tested and strengthened faith (Bilal's «Ahad, Ahad»; Sumayyah's sacrifice); hardship bound the believers together and prepared them for Madinah. Other view: the opposition also caused suffering and loss — Sumayyah was killed and some Muslims had to leave for Abyssinia; verse 38 shows that strength comes from following «the established way of Allah», not from persecution itself; Allah's help and the Prophet's ﷺ leadership kept them firm. Conclusion: a justified judgement, e.g. opposition revealed and deepened their faith, but it was faith in Allah, not the opposition itself, that made them strong. Mark with the Level 1–3 descriptors (Level 3 needs the text, another view and a justified conclusion).
