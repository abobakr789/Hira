(21) And of His signs is that He created for you from yourselves mates that you may find tranquillity in them; and He placed between you affection and mercy. Indeed in that are signs for a people who give thought.
(15) And We have enjoined upon man, to his parents, good treatment. His mother carried him with hardship and gave birth to him with hardship, and his gestation and weaning [period] is thirty months. [He grows] until, when he reaches maturity and reaches [the age of] forty years, he says, "My Lord, enable me to be grateful for Your favour which You have bestowed upon me and upon my parents and to work righteousness of which You will approve and make righteous for me my offspring. Indeed, I have repented to You, and indeed, I am of the Muslims."
(a–b) Marriage: significance and teachings
• 💞 Significance: A sign of Allah: «tranquillity… affection and mercy» (30:21); the Prophet ﷺ married and encouraged it (Bukhari, Muslim).
• 📜 The nikah: A contract with offer and acceptance, the consent of both, witnesses and usually the bride's wali.
• 🎁 The mahr: A gift owed by the husband to the wife; it is hers (4:4).
• ⚖️ Rights: Kindness, support and respect on both sides; the woman's consent is required (Bukhari); the walimah announces the marriage.

Study the reported rites in words only. Do not imitate a birth or funeral procedure, put food in an infant’s mouth, or ask students to disclose a personal bereavement.

(c) The purpose of families · (e) procreation
• 🏡 Raising children in faith: Parents teach children Islam, prayer and good character.
• 🤲 Honouring parents: «To his parents, good treatment» (46:15); care for them in old age.
• 🛡️ Security: A safe place of love, mercy and support (30:21).
• 👶 Procreation and the Ummah: «Marry the loving and fertile, for I will outnumber the nations through you» (Abu Dawud): children raised in faith strengthen the Ummah.
(d) Ceremonies of birth and death
• 👂 Adhan: Whispered into the newborn's ear, so the first words heard are about Allah.
• 🍯 Tahnik: The Prophet ﷺ softened a date and placed it in a baby's mouth (Bukhari).
• 🐑 Aqiqah: On the seventh day: naming, shaving the head, sacrifice, charity.
• 🕯️ At death: The dying are prompted with the Shahadah (Muslim); the body is washed and wrapped.
• 🕌 Janazah: The funeral prayer, then quick burial facing the Qiblah.
• 🤍 Mourning: Three days; a widow's iddah is 4 months 10 days (2:234; Bukhari).

Study the reported rites in words only. Do not imitate a birth or funeral procedure, put food in an infant’s mouth, or ask students to disclose a personal bereavement.
