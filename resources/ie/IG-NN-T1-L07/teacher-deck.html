(b) Consolidation and expansion

Spec 1.7 b

⏱ 8 min

🕊️ Hudaybiyah (6 AH)

A truce with Quraysh that the Qur'an called «a clear conquest» (48:1); in peace, many more people accepted Islam.

🕋 Makkah (8 AH)

After Quraysh broke the treaty, he entered Makkah almost without fighting and cleared the Ka'bah of idols.

🐪 Delegations (9 AH)

Tribes from across Arabia came to Madinah to accept Islam.

🌍 Result

By his death most of Arabia was united under Islam.

(c) Opponents and prisoners · (d) other nations

Spec 1.7 c–d

⏱ 8 min

💚 Mercy at Makkah

He pardoned the people who had persecuted him; whoever entered the house of Abu Sufyan or stayed at home was safe (Muslim).

🍞 Prisoners

The righteous feed «the poor, the orphan and the captive» (76:8); some Badr prisoners were freed for teaching Muslim children to read.

🔓 Thumamah

He freed the captive chief Thumamah ibn Uthal, who then accepted Islam (Bukhari).

✉️ Letters

He sent letters inviting rulers to Islam, e.g. Heraclius of Byzantium (Bukhari), and the Negus of Abyssinia.

We do: a (c) Explain question (6 marks)

Point → develop → link

⏱ 8 min

(c) Explain why it was important for the Prophet ﷺ to treat his opponents well. (6) — June 2023

Point 1 + why

His mercy, such as the pardon at Makkah, showed the true character of Islam. This was important because many former enemies accepted Islam after seeing his good character.

Point 2 + why

Good treatment of prisoners followed the Qur'an (76:8). This showed that Islam has strict ethics even in war, which won respect for the Muslims.

Point 3 + why

Forgiving instead of taking revenge ended old tribal feuds. This united Arabia and brought peace, so the community could grow and spread the message.

Plan a (d) answer: June 2025, Q1(d)

12 marks · four elements

⏱ 6 min

«The most important role of the Prophet was in the establishment of the Shari'ah (deen).» Assess this statement. Refer to the hadith on obeying rulers.

✅ Agree (with the text)

❌ Another point of view

The hadith: obedience to rulers is limited by obedience to Allah — the Shari'ah set the order of Muslim society.

His most important role was receiving and conveying the Qur'an, without which there is no Shari'ah.

Through the Shari'ah he turned the message into a complete way of life (deen) for the community.

His example of mercy and character (uswah hasanah) won hearts more than law.

⚖️ Conclusion: Establishing the Shari'ah was vital, but it depends on his role as the messenger of the Qur'an, which is the most important.

Plan in a T-table first (3 minutes in the exam). Every side needs reasons; the text must appear.

Model (d) answer · Level 3

Read the tags

⏱ 6 min

Text + agree

Many Muslims agree. The hadith says Muslims must «listen to and obey» rulers «unless these orders involve one disobedience (to Allah)». This shows the order the Prophet ﷺ established: a community where Allah's law, the Shari'ah, is above every leader.

Reason

Through the Shari'ah, Islam became a complete deen, covering worship, family, trade and justice, so Muslims could live as one community in Madinah.

Other view

However, others argue that his most important role was receiving and conveying the Qur'an. Without the revelation there would be no Shari'ah to establish.

Reason

Some also point to his example of mercy, such as pardoning the people of Makkah, which brought more people to Islam than any law.

Conclusion

Overall, establishing the Shari'ah was a vital role that shaped Muslim life, but it comes from the revelation he brought. So his role as messenger is the most important, and the Shari'ah is how that message was lived.
