🎯 Explain the Prophet's ﷺ role in establishing the Shari'ah, describe the consolidation and expansion of Islam after the Hijrah, his treatment of opponents and prisoners and his relations with other communities, and assess a statement using the hadith on obeying rulers.
📜 Set passage · Hadith on obeying rulers
“It is obligatory for one to listen to and obey (the ruler's orders) unless these orders involve one disobedience (to Allah); but if an act of disobedience (to Allah) is imposed, he should not listen to or obey it.”
Bukhari and Muslim (narrated by Abdullah ibn Umar)
(a) Establishing the Shari'ah
• 📖 Revelation in Madinah: Laws on worship, family, trade and crime were revealed as the community grew.
• 🧭 Explaining: He explained and applied the revealed law and judged disputes.
• 🗣️ Shura: He consulted his companions, e.g. before battles.
• 🏛️ A legal community: The Qur'an and Sunnah became the basis of law for the Ummah.

(b) Consolidation and expansion
• 🕊️ Hudaybiyah (6 AH): A truce with Quraysh that the Qur'an called «a clear conquest» (48:1); in peace, many more people accepted Islam.
• 🕋 Makkah (8 AH): After Quraysh broke the treaty, he entered Makkah almost without fighting and cleared the Ka'bah of idols.
• 🐪 Delegations (9 AH): Tribes from across Arabia came to Madinah to accept Islam.
• 🌍 Result: By his death most of Arabia was united under Islam.
(c) Opponents and prisoners · (d) other nations
• 💚 Mercy at Makkah: He pardoned the people who had persecuted him; whoever entered the house of Abu Sufyan or stayed at home was safe (Muslim).
• 🍞 Prisoners: The righteous feed «the poor, the orphan and the captive» (76:8); some Badr prisoners were freed for teaching Muslim children to read.
• 🔓 Thumamah: He freed the captive chief Thumamah ibn Uthal, who then accepted Islam (Bukhari).
• ✉️ Letters: He sent letters inviting rulers to Islam, e.g. Heraclius of Byzantium (Bukhari), and the Negus of Abyssinia.
