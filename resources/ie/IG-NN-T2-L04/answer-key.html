Silver

1: 🧠 Knowledge: Allah knows everything — past, present and future. · ✍️ Writing: Everything is written in the Preserved Tablet. · 🌟 Will: Nothing happens unless Allah wills it: «Indeed, all things We created with predestination» (54:49). · 🌱 Creation: Allah creates everything, including people's actions, yet people choose and are responsible. · 🧭 We choose: «Whoever wills — let him believe; and whoever wills — let him disbelieve» (18:29). · ⚖️ We are judged on choices: Allah's knowledge does not force anyone; people are judged on what they chose and intended. · 🧪 Life is a test: «He created death and life to test you — which of you is best in deed» (67:2). · 💚 Mercy: Allah is merciful and just; no one is wronged on the Day of Judgement.
2 (2 marks): Any two: a test from Allah · a result of human free will · a way to grow in patience · to remind people of Allah.
3 (3 marks): Muslims believe Allah knows and has written everything, yet humans have free will to choose (18:29). Allah's knowledge does not force anyone, so people are responsible for their choices. On the Day of Judgement each person is judged fairly on what they chose and intended.
4 (3 marks): It gave them patience under persecution in Makkah, because nothing happens except what Allah decrees (9:51) (1); it gave them courage in battle, trusting Allah with the result (1); it helped them accept loss, such as at Uhud, without despair (1).

Gold

1–2 (model answer): Muslims believe Allah knows and has written everything, but His knowledge does not force people: they still choose (18:29). Because they choose, they are responsible, so the Day of Judgement is fair — each person is judged on their deeds and intentions. Allah's mercy is also part of judgement, so Muslims combine effort in good deeds with hope in His mercy.

Diamond

Indicative content: Agree: verse 39 — prophets «fear none but Allah»: persecution tested and strengthened faith (Bilal's «Ahad, Ahad»; Sumayyah's sacrifice); hardship bound the believers together and prepared them for Madinah. Other view: the opposition also caused suffering and loss — Sumayyah was killed and some Muslims had to leave for Abyssinia; verse 38 shows that strength comes from following «the established way of Allah», not from persecution itself; Allah's help and the Prophet's ﷺ leadership kept them firm. Conclusion: a justified judgement, e.g. opposition revealed and deepened their faith, but it was faith in Allah, not the opposition itself, that made them strong. Mark with the Level 1–3 descriptors (Level 3 needs the text, another view and a justified conclusion).
