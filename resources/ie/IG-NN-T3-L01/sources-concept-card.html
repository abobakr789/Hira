🎯 Explain the nature and role of the Shahadah, the importance of reciting it and how it is used in practice, and assess a statement using Qur'an 3:18–19.
📖 Set passage · Qur'an 3:18–19
شَهِدَ ٱللَّهُ أَنَّهُۥ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ وَٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةُ وَأُوْلُواْ ٱلۡعِلۡمِ قَآئِمَۢا بِٱلۡقِسۡطِۚ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ ﴿18﴾ إِنَّ ٱلدِّينَ عِندَ ٱللَّهِ ٱلۡإِسۡلَٰمُۗ وَمَا ٱخۡتَلَفَ ٱلَّذِينَ أُوتُواْ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ إِلَّا مِنۢ بَعۡدِ مَا جَآءَهُمُ ٱلۡعِلۡمُ بَغۡيَۢا بَيۡنَهُمۡۗ وَمَن يَكۡفُرۡ بَِٔايَٰتِ ٱللَّهِ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ سَرِيعُ ٱلۡحِسَابِ ﴿19﴾
(18) Allāh witnesses that there is no deity except Him, and [so do] the angels and those of knowledge - [that He is] maintaining [creation] in justice. There is no deity except Him, the Exalted in Might, the Wise.
(19) Indeed, the religion in the sight of Allāh is Islām. And those who were given the Scripture did not differ except after knowledge had come to them - out of jealous animosity between themselves. And whoever disbelieves in the verses of Allāh, then indeed, Allāh is swift in [taking] account.
(a) The nature and role of the Shahadah
• ☝️ First half: «There is no god but Allah» — Tawhid: Allah alone is worshipped; shirk is rejected (47:19).
• 📜 Second half: «Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah» — Risalah: Muslims follow his Sunnah.
• 🏛️ The first pillar: «Islam is built upon five: the testimony that there is no god but Allah and that Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah…» (Bukhari, Muslim).
• 🔑 Its role: It is the foundation of all other beliefs and actions: belief in the Qur'an, angels and the Akhirah follows from it.

(b) Why reciting it matters
• 🚪 Entering Islam: There is no other rite of passage: a person becomes Muslim by sincerely reciting it, usually before witnesses.
• 💗 Sincerity: Saying it with belief in the heart and acting on it — words alone are not enough.
• 🔁 Renewing faith: Repeating it daily reminds Muslims of their commitment and keeps Allah at the centre of life.
• 🌙 The last words: «Whoever's last words are 'There is no god but Allah' will enter Paradise» (Abu Dawud).
(c) The Shahadah in practice
• 👶 At birth: The adhan, which contains the Shahadah, is whispered into a newborn's ear.
• 📢 Five times a day: It is called out in every adhan and iqamah.
• 🧎 In every prayer: It is said in the tashahhud of each Salah.
• 🤝 Conversion: A new Muslim recites it before witnesses.
• 🕯️ At death: The dying are gently prompted to say it (Muslim).
• 🌍 Unity: Muslims everywhere share the same words, which unites the Ummah.
