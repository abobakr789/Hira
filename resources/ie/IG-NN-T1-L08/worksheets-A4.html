🎯 Describe the battles of Badr, Uhud, the Trench and Khaybar, explain why the Prophet ﷺ chose to fight and why the battles were important for the establishment of Islam, and assess a statement using Qur'an 33:9.
1Match each key term with its meaning (write the letter).
TermLetterMeaning
1) BadrA) Battle near Madinah, 3 AH (625 CE).
2) UhudB) Fortified oasis north of Madinah, 7 AH (628 CE).
3) KhandaqC) A martyr.
4) KhaybarD) First major battle, 2 AH (624 CE).
5) AhzabE) The Confederates: the allied tribes who besieged Madinah.
6) ShahidF) The Trench: the siege of Madinah, 5 AH (627 CE).
