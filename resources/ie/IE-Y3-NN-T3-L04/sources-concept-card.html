Sources & concepts: Care for the Needy
Islamic Education · Grade 3 · Non-Arabic speakers · The Faithful Are Brothers · Textbook V2 p.146
For all
Objective: To explain who people in need are and infer that helping them is a religious and national duty.
📖 Read the text · Textbook V2 p.146
كافل اليتيم (Kafil al-yatim): one who looks after an orphan
📘 Key words
كافل اليتيم (Kafil al-yatim)one who looks after an orphan
💡 Main ideas
1. People in need include the poor, orphans, the sick, the elderly and people of determination.
2. The Prophet ﷺ said: «I and the one who cares for an orphan will be in Paradise like this», showing two fingers.
3. The UAE is among the world's leaders in humanitarian work, thanks to Allah and then Sheikh Zayed.
4. I help them with money, kind words and support.
✅ Lessons we take
1. Help those in need.
🇦🇪 National identity: The UAE is among the world's largest donors of humanitarian aid.
✉️ Lesson message: I care for those in need
