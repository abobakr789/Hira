[An-Nur: 11–14]
“Indeed, those who came with falsehood are a group among you. Do not think it bad for you; rather it is good for you. For every person among them is what [punishment] he has earned from the sin, and he who took upon himself the greater portion thereof - for him is a great punishment” (11) “Why, when you heard it, did not the believing men and believing women think good of one another and say, "This is an obvious falsehood” (12) “Why did they [who slandered] not produce for it four witnesses? And when they do not produce the witnesses, then it is they, in the sight of Allah, who are the liars”
