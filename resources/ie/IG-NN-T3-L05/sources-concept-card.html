📖 Set passage · Qur'an 2:197–199
ٱلۡحَجُّ أَشۡهُرٞ مَّعۡلُومَٰتٞۚ فَمَن فَرَضَ فِيهِنَّ ٱلۡحَجَّ فَلَا رَفَثَ وَلَا فُسُوقَ وَلَا جِدَالَ فِي ٱلۡحَجِّۗ وَمَا تَفۡعَلُواْ مِنۡ خَيۡرٖ يَعۡلَمۡهُ ٱللَّهُۗ وَتَزَوَّدُواْ فَإِنَّ خَيۡرَ ٱلزَّادِ ٱلتَّقۡوَىٰۖ وَٱتَّقُونِ يَٰٓأُوْلِي ٱلۡأَلۡبَٰبِ ﴿197﴾ لَيۡسَ عَلَيۡكُمۡ جُنَاحٌ أَن تَبۡتَغُواْ فَضۡلٗا مِّن رَّبِّكُمۡۚ فَإِذَآ أَفَضۡتُم مِّنۡ عَرَفَٰتٖ فَٱذۡكُرُواْ ٱللَّهَ عِندَ ٱلۡمَشۡعَرِ ٱلۡحَرَامِۖ وَٱذۡكُرُوهُ كَمَا هَدَىٰكُمۡ وَإِن كُنتُم مِّن قَبۡلِهِۦ لَمِنَ ٱلضَّآلِّينَ ﴿198﴾ ثُمَّ أَفِيضُواْ مِنۡ حَيۡثُ أَفَاضَ ٱلنَّاسُ وَٱسۡتَغۡفِرُواْ ٱللَّهَۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ غَفُورٞ رَّحِيمٞ ﴿199﴾
(197) Hajj is [during] well-known months, so whoever has made hajj obligatory upon himself therein [by entering the state of ihram], there is [to be for him] no sexual relations and no disobedience and no disputing during hajj. And whatever good you do – Allah knows it. And take provisions, but indeed, the best provision is fear of Allah. And fear Me, O you of understanding.
(198) There is no blame upon you for seeking bounty from your Lord [during hajj]. But when you depart from 'Arafat, remember Allah at al-Mash'ar al-Haram. And remember Him, as He has guided you, for indeed, you were before that among those astray.
(199) Then depart from the place from where [all] the people depart and ask forgiveness of Allah. Indeed, Allah is Forgiving and Merciful.
(a) Nature, role, origins and significance
• 🕋 Nature and role: The fifth pillar: compulsory once in a lifetime «for whoever is able to find a way» (3:97), in Dhul-Hijjah.
• 🧱 Ibrahim and Isma'il: They raised the foundations of the Ka'bah (2:127); Ibrahim was told to call people to Hajj (22:27).
• 💧 Hajar: Her search for water between Safa and Marwah is remembered in the sa'i (2:158); Zamzam sprang up.
• ✨ Significance: «An accepted Hajj has no reward except Paradise» (Bukhari, Muslim).

(b) Key actions in order
• 1 · Ihram: Enter ihram at the miqat with intention and the talbiyah.
• 2 · Tawaf and sa'i: Seven circuits of the Ka'bah; seven walks between Safa and Marwah.
• 3 · Mina (8th): Pilgrims go to Mina and pray there.
• 4 · Arafah (9th): Standing in prayer and du'a until sunset — «Hajj is Arafah» (Tirmidhi).
• 5 · Muzdalifah: Night under the sky; collect pebbles (2:198).
• 6 · 10th and after: Stone the Jamarat, sacrifice, shave or cut hair, tawaf al-ifadah; stay in Mina; farewell tawaf.
(c–e) Importance, benefits, challenges, exemptions
• 🌟 Importance: Obeying Allah, following Ibrahim and the Prophet ﷺ, and an inner journey of repentance and forgiveness.
• ✅ Benefits: Forgiveness, equality in ihram, unity with millions of Muslims, patience and self-control (2:197).
• ⚠️ Challenges: Cost, crowds, heat, physical effort, visa quotas, being away from family and work.
• 🛑 Exemptions: Not compulsory for those without the health, money or safe route (3:97), for children or the insane; a woman without a mahram according to many scholars.
