⭐ Write three of your school rules.

Page 110 · Activity 2

In my house, we have family rules. On schooldays, we must go to bed at 7pm. My mum or dad gets us ready for bed. We brush our teeth and wear our pyjamas. Then, my dad reads us a bed-time story.

After school, we watch TV or play computer games for one hour. I share a laptop with my brother Khalid. Before school, we make our beds and put our toys away.

Page 110 · Activity 2

We must say “thank you” and “please” and “I’m sorry”. Sometimes, Khalid and I fight over the computer. We always say “sorry” after we fight. Sometimes we don’t say anything and just hug. We must respect our family members.

All communities have rules. Rules make life easy and help us solve problems. Rules help everyone to respect and treat each other fairly. Rules help us live together. Talk to your classmates and answer the questions.
