UAE heritage and traditions

A tradition is something passed down from parents to children. Heritage villages in the UAE help people learn how Emiratis lived in the past.

Traditions are passed down in families.

Activity 5: Heritage Villages. There are many heritage villages in the UAE. Each emirate has one or more heritage villages. People can go to heritage villages and learn how Emiratis lived in the past.

Page 125 · Activity 5

There’s lots to see and do at a heritage village! There are model houses and markets from the old days.

There are tents made from camel hair. You can see rugs made from palm leaves.

You can see lots of old pots and pans and tools and learn about how people used them.

You can try camel milk and taste Arabic coffee. There are all kinds of different foods.

Page 125–126 · Activity 5

There are people doing crafts and dances. You can look at traditional art and walk around a traditional market.

There are traditional sports like horse racing, camel racing and falconry. You can see horses and camels. You can even ride them!
