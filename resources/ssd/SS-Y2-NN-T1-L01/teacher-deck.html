📖 Book text 1/2

Activity 2: Salem’s Farm

There are lots of different animals on Salem’s farm. Some are big, others are small. Some are covered in wool, others in feathers. Some eat grass, others eat seeds.

One day, Salem brought a dog to watch over his farm. The other animals gathered in the farm yard. They didn’t like the new dog.

The sheep said, ‘Get away from us, strange animal! You’re not like us! We don’t want you here with us!’

The chicken said, ‘What do you do here?’ Do you lay eggs every morning?’

The cow laughed, and asked, ‘Do you give milk?’

🐑🐔🐄

Student Book Vol. 1 · page 10 · Activity 2

We Are All Different
Read the first part of the story (original text, Student Book p. 10). Ask: How are the animals different? What do the sheep, the chicken and the cow say to the dog?

📖 Book text 2/2

Activity 2: Salem’s Farm

The animals said together, ‘Go away! We don’t want you here!’

Salem went up to the animals and talked to them.

He said, ‘What is wrong with you, my friends? Is this how we welcome our guests here to Farmer Salem’s farm?’

The animals felt ashamed. They looked first at each other and then at the dog.

Then they all said, ‘You are welcome among us. Uncle Salem’s farm is for everyone. We’re sorry!’

🐕👨‍🌾

Student Book Vol. 1 · page 10 · Activity 2

We Are All Different

💡 Model answers

Beginner

Task 1: Can be different: hair, favourite food, favourite colour. Same for all children: being a child, going to school, smiling (accept reasoned answers). Task 2: circle the things above. Task 3: Open.

Developing

Task 1: 1 True · 2 False · 3 True · 4 False. Task 2: Suggested: 1 different · 2 ashamed · 3 everyone. Task 3: Answers can vary; accept reasoned answers.

Advanced

clear examples of how we are similar and different, a welcome action that is possible this week, a sentence that welcomes everyone.
