CONCEPT CARD

Helping others feel good

People can feel angry, sad, scared, shy, bored, happy, confused or surprised. We can notice how others feel. A simple kind act, like inviting a friend to play, can help someone feel good.

For every problem there’s an answer, and a simple act can go a long way.

Words to know

angryfeeling very upsetboredfeeling that nothing is interesting
scaredfeeling afraidhappyfeeling good
shyfeeling nervous with other peopleconfusednot understanding something
sadfeeling unhappysurprisedfeeling that something is new or unexpected

Think and talk

① How do people feel? ② Why do they feel this way? ③ How can I help?

Page 28 · Activity 2

One day, when Nour came home from school, she looked angry, worried and sad. Her father, Farmer Salem, asked her, ‘What’s the problem? Why do you look so sad, my dear?’

‘I was playing a game with my friends. I lost the game, and I got very angry. I asked them to start again but they said no. Then, they left me by myself and started playing without me.’

‘I think your classmates went away because you were a bad loser. What if you won and they lost? Would you be happy to start again?’

Nour looked down and said, ‘I feel angry. No one pays any attention to me. Even the farm animals turn away when I walk up to them.’

Page 28–29 · Activity 2

Salem said, ‘The farm animals turn away from you because you don’t take care of them. Everyone needs someone else’s attention! I have an idea. Let’s invite your friends to a fun day on the farm?’

Nour said, ‘Great idea! But what if they don’t want to come?’ ‘Let’s try!’ said Salem.

Nour was really surprised when her friends came. They were kind to her and to the farm animals. It was a great day! The children played hide-and-seek together. Later, Nour’s mother called them to come in for some biscuits and a lovely glass of Jallab. Nour felt very happy and said, ‘For every problem there’s an answer, and a simple act can go a long way.’

Page 29–30 · Let’s Think

‘For every problem there’s an answer, and a simple act can go a long way.’ Talk to your classmates about what it means.

How should we act if we lose a game?

What does Salem mean by ‘Everyone needs someone else’s attention’?

How did the negative feelings between the group of friends turn into positive feelings?

Page 30 · Activity 4

Think of two things that have happened to you, one that made you happy and one that made you sad. Draw a picture of yourself in each situation in the boxes below. Then talk about your feelings with your classmates.

Page 31–33 · Activity 5 and 6

Activity 5. Role play in pairs. Student A: Choose an envelope. Read the words on the pieces of paper inside. Act out the words. Student B: Do you understand what your partner is feeling? How can you help your partner? How can you show understanding?

Activity 6. Talk about your feelings with a partner. Talk about a time when you felt happy or sad. Say how you felt. Talk about what you wanted to happen.
