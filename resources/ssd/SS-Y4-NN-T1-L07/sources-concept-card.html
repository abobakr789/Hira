CONCEPT CARD

Growth and development in suitable conditions

Everyone has the right to housing and the right to education. Social care is help for people who need support. In the UAE, the Ministry of Social Affairs is developing social welfare and rehabilitation programmes, and public places and buses are being designed with people of determination in mind.

Everyone deserves suitable conditions to grow and learn.

Page 74–75 · Activity 3

Everyone has the right to adequate services. In the UAE, the Ministry of Social Affairs is developing social welfare and rehabilitation programmes to help those in need.

Read about the care it provides. Then, write about why each programme is important.

Municipality Services. The municipalities send workers to homes of people of determination so they complete their forms without having to leave their homes.

Education. The UAE provides several educational choices to people of determination. It also helps them to get enrolled in regular schools, according to each student’s improvement or level.

Free Entry to Emirates Park Zoo. People of determination are given free access to the zoo. Emirates Park Zoo aims to bring children closer to nature. It even has areas without barriers where children can touch and connect directly with the animals.

Moving around. All new public car parks, pavements, corridors, bridges, pedestrian walkways, entrances and other public places in many cities in the UAE are being designed with people of determination in mind. The new public transport buses have low floors and non-slip entrances.

Page 76–77 · Activity 4

Read about the yellow school bus. Then, work in pairs. Choose the picture you like best. Write a dialogue between the people in the picture, focusing on safety or learning. Practise your dialogue with your partner and present it to your classmates.

In many countries you can see yellow school buses. They usually have ways to protect children from getting hurt. It’s easy to see that the bus is a school bus. When they stop to let children off, they flash red and yellow lights to tell other drivers that kids are around. Another way of keeping children safe, the left side of the bus has a round red ‘Stop’ sign. This is to tell other drivers to be careful. Of course, children have to be educated in bus safety. They should always remain in their seats until the bus comes to a complete stop. Then they should get off without pushing. It’s very important to be careful after getting off the bus. Therefore, children who want to cross the road in front of the bus should move several metres in front before starting to cross.

Page 78 · Activity 5

The United Nations Committee on Economic, Social and Cultural Rights has a set of housing requirements. Work in pairs. Do research to find out more about these housing requirements. Write down what you think are the three most important requirements. Compare your answers with your classmates.
