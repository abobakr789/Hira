Student Book Vol. 1 · Original text from the Student Book.

Page 37–38 · Reading 1

What is meant by social cohesion? What are some examples of social cohesion in the UAE? Why is social cohesion important? Activity 1: Read about the main pillar of the UAE vision, then discuss the definition of social cohesion. The UAE Vision 2021 National Agenda strives to preserve a cohesive society proud of its identity and sense of belonging. Thus, it promotes an inclusive environment that integrates all segments of society while preserving the UAE’s unique culture, heritage and traditions, and reinforces social and family cohesion.

Tasks: (a) Circle the statements that express the meaning of social cohesion. (b) Define “social cohesion”.

Page 39–40 · Reading 2

Activity 2: A cohesive society gives people the tools to face the risks of life. It improves confidence of individuals in their neighbours and the government. It also encourages people to work for a brighter future for themselves and their families. Social cohesion aims to increase the participation of citizens in social work and create opportunities for promotion. It requires group participation and active co-operation from everyone in society, including the government, the business sector and civil society.

Tasks: (a) Look for the United Nations Development Programme (UNDP) and highlight its importance for the growth of the world and UAE communities. (b) Suggest three procedures that contribute to cohesion within the community to sustain economic growth within the UAE. (c) Write a speech about the importance of social cohesion and belonging to appropriate groups. Discuss a group that you are part of, express your feelings at being part of it, and deliver the speech to your classmates.

Page 41–42 · Reading 3

Activity 3: Read the article about social cohesion in the United Arab Emirates through the 2021 Vision. Strong social relations are the main guides of a society. Communities are the shared space where children can reach out away from their families. In close neighbourhoods, young people develop their Emirati identity. They also learn respect for others and develop into fully engaged citizens. The feeling of unity is what allows Emiratis to work together in harmony. Exchanges with other nationalities will further strengthen Emirati society. A spirit of respect and consideration among many cultural groups is important for the continuation of coexistence.

Social relations provide an essential safety net against social exclusion. Define the meaning of social exclusion in family and school, and analyse its negative impact on the community. Tasks: (a) Identify three ways to maintain social relations with an elderly man, a schoolmate of different nationality and a neighbour. (b) After reading the launch of the UAE Federation project, define the date of establishment and the target achieved at the level of UAE development.

Page 42–43 · Reading 4

Activity 4: How do international organisations develop the concept of social cohesion? Read about the initiative of Generations For Peace (GFP) and UNICEF in Jordan. GFP is a non-profit organisation that promotes peace and started in Jordan in 2007. It received extra funds from the United Nations Children’s Fund (UNICEF) to grow one of its programmes. The programme has had a great impact across ten communities over the past year, and UNICEF would like to include 16 communities this year. Robert Jenkins, UNICEF representative in Jordan, said these youth-led activities promote social cohesion, resilience and involvement in the community, in a society that hosts a huge number of refugees. Omar Al-Haraky, a volunteer in training, said he should be an active player in society and that we all have to ensure that our society is a safe place.

Tasks: (a) In your opinion, why is this initiative important for society? Define the targeted category and how they got help. (b) In a country like the UAE, based on multiculturalism, educational plans contribute to social integration. Name some social classes that need educational plans in order to be protected from social exclusion, and suggest programmes. (c) Conduct research on an initiative launched by an international or Emirati organisation to enhance social cohesion, then present your research.

Page 44–45 · Reading 5

Activity 5: National Service in the UAE is an example of social cohesion. Read about the seventh batch. On April 24, 2017, the military training centres welcomed the seventh batch of national service, which included male employees and fresh graduates. During national service, conscripts go through a training programme with different military and physical exercises, and attend security lectures. The book says the participants expressed joy in serving their country and called it an honour to take part in national duty.

Tasks: (a) Define the importance of military service and the role played by the youth. Analyse its importance for social cohesion, the country and all citizens. (b) Conduct research on the UAE National and Reserve Service Law. Then compare it with national service in Germany, Greece and Finland in terms of the objective of the law, targeted categories of youth and duration of service. Activity 6: Write down what you have concluded about the importance of social cohesion to ensure the interest, progress and prosperity of the nation.
