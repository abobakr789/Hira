👪 Note for parents and carers

Lesson: Early Human Migration · Year 10 · Moral, Social and Cultural Studies (Student Book Vol. 1, pages 207–216)

What we learned

This week we learned why early humans migrated, how scientists trace their journeys, and how people adapted to different environments.

How you can help at home

Ask: “Why did early people have to move?”

Look at a world map together and trace a journey.

Talk about respecting people from different places.

Home task

Ask your child to imagine a day in an early human community. Label it as a fictional reconstruction, not an oral-history source.
