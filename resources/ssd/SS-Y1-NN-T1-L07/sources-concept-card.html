Sharing my feelings

We all have feelings like happy, sad and worried. When we share our feelings and say why, others can listen and help us.

⭐ What different feelings are there on the board?

Page 56 · Activity 2

Today, my mum comes home from the hospital. My new baby brother is home too. My mum gives me a big hug.

“I love you so much Mila! You’re a special girl.”

I close my eyes and I feel happy. I feel safe and warm when I hug my mum.

My grandma is busy. She cooks and makes lots of sweets. Friends and family come to the house to meet my new baby brother.

Suddenly, I feel worried. I go to my room and sit on my bed. I think my family will love Silvio more than me!

Then, my mum comes into my room. She asks me if I want to see my baby brother.

Page 57 · Activity 2

“He’s so small, and I’m so big. What do I do?” I ask my mum.

My mum takes me to Silvio. I look at his little face. Silvio opens his eyes and starts to cry. My mum looks at me.

“You can hold him, Mila. He loves his sister.”

Mum picks up Silvio and puts him in my arms. I smile at Silvio. He stops crying and goes to sleep!

I think my brother loves me a lot. This makes me very happy.

“I love my new baby brother,” I say to my mum. “I want to be a good sister.”
