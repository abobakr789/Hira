🎭 Group 1: Sorry role-play

Group members:
Task: Act out a story where a child says sorry.

Steps

1Choose a story: someone breaks or takes something.

2Decide who says sorry.

3Practise your role-play.

4Show the class.

Roles

LeaderSpeakerWriter / DrawerTimekeeper
 
 
🚀 Challenge: show how the other child feels.

Group checklist

We took turns. We listened kindly. We used polite words. We helped each other.

📋 Group 3: Sorry steps poster

Group members:
Task: Draw three steps to say sorry: look, say, help.

Steps

1Draw three boxes.

2Step 1: look at the person. Step 2: say “I’m sorry”. Step 3: help or make it right.

3Draw a picture for each step.

4Show the poster to the class.

Roles

LeaderSpeakerWriter / DrawerTimekeeper
 
 
🚀 Challenge: add a face for each step.

Group checklist

We took turns. We listened kindly. We used polite words. We helped each other.
