CONCEPT CARD

Saying sorry

We say sorry when we do something wrong or unfair. We can say “I’m sorry” in a kind voice and show we are sorry by helping to make things right.

I can say sorry and help to make things right.

Page 31 · Activity 1

⭐ If it’s fair, hold up a blue card!

⭐ If it’s unfair, hold up a red card!

Page 32 · Activity 2

Rashid looks at the elephant. The elephant is very sick! Rashid is sad and he feels sorry. He has been unfair to the elephant. Rashid talks to the elephant.

“I’m sorry, elephant! I was unfair to you. Now I understand! Sometimes, fairness isn’t giving the same amount to everyone. It means giving everyone what they need.”

Rashid talks to his uncle.

“I’m sorry, Uncle. I wanted to be fair, so I gave the animals the same amount of food. I thought you were unfair. Now, I know I was wrong! You give the elephant, the giraffe and the rabbit what they need.”

Page 32 · Activity 2

The giraffe and the rabbit listen to Rashid. They know they were unfair. They say sorry to the elephant.

Rashid wants to help the elephant. He feeds the elephant every morning.

Soon, the elephant is better. When it’s time to go home, Rashid says goodbye to his uncle and the animals. He’s excited to go home and see his family!

Page 34 · Activity 3 and 4

Activity 4: Work with a partner. Think of an unfair story and act it out. Remember to say, ‘I’m sorry’.

Let’s talk

① Why does Rashid say sorry to the elephant? ② Should the giraffe and the rabbit say sorry? Why? ③ Why does Rashid help the elephant? How does Rashid feel at the end of the story?
