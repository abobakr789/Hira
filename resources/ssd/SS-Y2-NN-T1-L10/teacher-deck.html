📖 Book text 1/2

Activity 2: New Friends

Adam came home from school with a big smile on his face. His mother asked him why he was so happy.

He said, ‘I made new friends! Wassim helped me in the maths test! He showed me his paper so I could copy all the answers. Then Majid told me that the word night has a “g” and “h” even though you don’t hear them. Then I couldn’t find my book and Hana helped me to look for it.’

😊📝

Student Book Vol. 1 · page 72 · Activity 2

Read the first part of the story (original text, Student Book p. 72). Ask: Who helped Adam? How did each friend help?

📖 Book text 2/2

Activity 2: New Friends

Adam’s mother hugged him. Then she said, ‘I’m really happy that you made new friends, but I want you to think carefully about what Wassim, Majid and Hana did for you today. Who gave you the right type of help? It is very important to help and support others but it’s even more important to know how to help in the right way.’

Adam thought about his mother’s words. Then he said, ‘OK, I think I understand. You mean it’s important to help at the right time and in the right way. But Wassim, Majid and Hana will still be my friends, all three of them.’

Adam’s mother laughed and said, ‘Of course they will.’

👩👦

Student Book Vol. 1 · page 72 · Activity 2

Read the second part. Ask: What does Adam’s mother want him to think about? What does it mean to help in the right way and at the right time?

📖 Book text

Let’s Think

Think and answer the questions.

‘One hand can’t clap.’ What do you think this means?

Who do you think offered the right type of help to Adam?

Did anyone offer help that was not really helpful at all?

If your friend asked you for help during a test, would you help?

What would you do if your friend asked you for help before the test? Would you help? How?

Student Book Vol. 1 · page 73 · Let’s Think

Children discuss (Student Book p. 73). Main idea: it is good to help, but help must be right: helping before the test is good; showing answers during a test is not helpful. Accept reasoned answers.

📖 Book text

Activities 3 and 4: Helpful actions

Look at the pictures. Then decide.

Activity 3. How can we help others? Look at the pictures. How do you think the children are feeling? How could you help them?

Activity 4. What are helpful and unhelpful actions? Decide if each of these actions are helpful or unhelpful. 1. I did my friend’s homework for him. 2. I taught my little sister how to draw a flower. 3. I tied my friend’s shoelace. 4. I built a palace with my brother. 5. I helped our neighbour look for his lost cat. 6. I helped my brother throw away the food he didn’t want to eat.

Student Book Vol. 1 · page 74–75 · Activity 3 and 4

Activity 3: children look at the pictures (Student Book p. 74) and say how the children feel and how to help. Activity 4: decide helpful or unhelpful (p. 75). Suggested: 1 unhelpful, 2 helpful, 3 helpful (if the friend cannot do it yet), 4 helpful, 5 helpful, 6 unhelpful. Accept reasoned answers.

📖 Book text

Activities 5 and 6: Plans to help

Work in groups. Then talk with a friend.

Activity 5. Can you make a plan to help someone? Work in groups. Imagine that your school caretaker, who lives on his own, is very ill and doesn’t have anyone to take care of him. He will have to stay in bed for a long time. How could you help? Work with your group to think of a plan of action. Select a team leader. Make a list of the caretaker’s usual tasks. Decide which of his tasks you could do. Decide on the level of help you could offer him while he is ill. Develop a clear work plan. Distribute tasks and roles. Exchange ideas with other groups.

Activity 6. How can I help my family? Talk with a friend about ways to help and support your families.

Student Book Vol. 1 · page 76–77 · Activity 5 and 6

Groups make a plan to help the caretaker (Student Book p. 76), following the seven steps. Activity 6: children talk with a friend about ways to help their families (p. 77).

Let’s talk

💬 Let’s talk – answer with a full sentence.

2. How did Wassim, Majid and Hana help Adam?

Wassim showed Adam his paper to copy, Majid told Adam about the word night, and Hana helped Adam look for his book.

It is important to help and support others, but it is even more important to help in the right way and at the right time.
