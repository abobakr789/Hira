Landforms

Landforms are natural features of the earth. Mountains are the highest places, valleys are the low areas between mountains, a desert is a dry place, a plain is a large, flat area where people grow food, and an island is land surrounded by water. Jabel Hafit is the tallest mountain in Abu Dhabi.

📖 Book text

Student Book Vol. 1 · Original text from the Student Book.

Page 102–103 · Reading 1

What is your favourite landform? Why? Do you have any landforms where you live?

Warm up: Brainstorm. When you go outside, where do you like to go? The mountains? A park? Some place else? Why do you like it? Share your answers with a partner.

Page 104 · Reading 2

Landforms are natural features of the earth. They can be land or water. In this lesson, we will look at different kinds of land. In the next lesson, we will look at bodies of water.

The highest places on earth are mountains. Some mountains are so high that no trees or plants can grow on the top of them. The tallest mountain in Abu Dhabi is Jabel Hafit, a mountain in Al Ain. Valleys are the low areas between mountains. Rivers often run through valleys.

Page 105 · Reading 3

A desert is a dry place. Deserts can be hot with sand or rocks or cold and frozen. The elevation of a landform is how much it is above or below sea level.

A plain is a large, flat area of land. People grow food in plains because the land is flat. In the past, people lived in plains near places of fresh water. The people grew food and raised animals in the plains.

Page 106 · Reading 4

An island is a landform surrounded on all sides by water. The city of Abu Dhabi is an island. Another island, Sir Bani Yas is a nature reserve. A nature reserve is an area where animals are protected. On Sir Bani Yas, you can see animals like oryx, giraffes, and cheetahs.

Page 107–115 · Reading 5

Activity 2: Look at the diagrams of different landforms. Write a short description for each landform: volcano, hills, island, canyon, mountains, marshes, desert, ocean, river, jungle, waterfall, plain.

Activity 3: Vocabulary. List the six landforms from the text. Write a short description.

Activity 4: Concept Check. Answer the questions based on the text, ‘Landforms of the World’. Which landform is the highest on earth? What landform is good for growing food? What 2 things do people need to grow food? How are Abu Dhabi and Sir Bani Yas the same? How are they different? Write your answer in sentences.

Activity 5: Critical Thinking. For each landform (mountains, plain, island, jungle, desert), decide if people can find wood, food, and fresh water. Write “Yes”, “No”, or “Maybe”. Compare and discuss your answers with other students. Take notes.

Activity 6: Name your two favourite landforms. Then, draw the landform and describe why it is important for people.
