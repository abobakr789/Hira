CONCEPT CARD

Natural resources in the UAE

The nature of an area affects how people live. Things people get from nature and use to live are natural resources. People on the coast worked in fishing, pearl diving and sea trading. People in an oasis farmed, grew dates and kept camels, goats and sheep. The UAE is surrounded by the Arabian Gulf and the Sea of Oman. The dugong is an endangered marine animal.

Nature gives us natural resources. We take care of it.

The nature of an area affects how people live. It affects the jobs people have, the kind of food they eat and their homes. People use things that are found in nature. These are called natural resources.

Page 95 · Activity 5

The UAE is surrounded by two bodies of water: the Arabian Gulf and the Sea of Oman. More than 500 kinds of fish live in the seas near the UAE. Fish have always been an important natural resource for people in the UAE. There are very small fish like seahorses. There are big mammals like dolphins. Fish and other animals that live in the sea are called marine animals.

Page 96–97 · Activity 5 and 6

Dugongs are a kind of marine animal that live in the warm waters near the UAE. Dugongs are sometimes called sea cows. They are very big, and they eat seagrass. Baby dugongs stay with their mothers for nearly two years.

The dugong is an endangered animal. This means that there are few of them left in the world. People are using the seas where dugongs used to live. There is not enough sea grass left in the seas to feed them.
