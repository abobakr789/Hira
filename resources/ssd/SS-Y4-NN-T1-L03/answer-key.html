🔑 Teacher answer key

For teachers only. Accept any sensible answer for open questions.

Worksheet Level 1

Task 1: ways to help Tarik: bring a plank, carry the chair, say a kind welcome, shake his hand. Task 2: circle those four. Task 3: Open.

Worksheet Level 2

Task 1: 1 False · 2 True · 3 True · 4 True. Task 2: Suggested: 1 wheelchair · 2 plank · 3 grateful. Task 3: Answers can vary; accept reasoned answers.

Worksheet Level 3

All tasks are open. Look for: clear sentences about sympathy, empathy and People of Determination; realistic school ideas (ramps, special classes, sign language, a helpful welcome); a kind welcome message.

Let’s talk

1. Worried and scared at first; later surprised, grateful and relieved. 2. He brought a wooden plank so Tarik could get up the steps. 3. People of Determination.

True or false (deck)

1 False · 2 True · 3 True · 4 True · 5 False.

Quick quiz (deck)

1 a (Understanding and sharing the feelings of another person) · 2 a (A wooden plank) · 3 a (People of Determination) · 4 a (Equal opportunities for education) · 5 a (Happy, grateful and relieved)

Matching and words in action (deck)

I feel sorry for a friend with a broken leg. → sympathy · I understand how scared you feel because I felt the same. → empathy · A person works hard and never gives up. → determination · Ahmad brought a plank to help Tarik. → empathy. More than one answer can fit if the child explains why.

Exit ticket

Open. Check each child completes all three sentences with a clear idea.
