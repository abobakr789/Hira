How is Fair Distribution Accomplished?

Unit 1: Equality and Justice as Fairness · Lesson 2

Student Book Vol. 1 · pages 18–31

Welcome the class. Today we explore how fairness depends on point of view and opportunity, using the Chinese story “Chunhua and the Emperor” and an article about the foundation of the UAE. Ask the lesson questions from the Student Book (p. 20) and collect ideas without correcting.

📖 Book text 2/6

Chunhua and the Emperor (1)

Read the story.

Activity 2: Do you think fairness depends on your point of view? Read the Chinese story ‘Chunhua and the Emperor’. A long time ago in China, there was an emperor who loved flowers […]. The emperor was getting very old and he had no children, but he had to choose a successor. […] The emperor ordered that his imperial announcement was to be shown in every city in China, so that every Chinese man and woman could read it. Imperial Announcement: To every man and woman wants to sit on the throne, come take a seed and work to make it grow. Come next year with a plant to show, and he or she with the most beautiful flower will inherit my throne.

Student Book Vol. 1 · page 22 · Reading 2

Student Book p. 22 (selected passages). The story is taken, as the book says, from Valerie Dovey’s book; treat it as a story, not as history.

📖 Book text 3/6

Chunhua and the Emperor (2)

Read and discuss.

[…] a poor young woman called Chunhua entered the palace. […] She watered it and sang to it […]. Days turned to months, but nothing grew in Chunhua’s pot, and she felt extremely sad. […] When spring came, everyone rushed to the castle with their pots. The pots all had beautiful flowers […]. All the men and women with beautiful flowers laughed when they saw Chunhua holding her empty pot. […] Suddenly, a flowerless pot caught his eye. […] The old emperor listened quietly and smiled. Then, he turned to everyone and said, ‘I gave all of you pebbles to grow! And yet everyone here brings me such wondrous plants, except for this honest young lady. She is the only winner!’ The emperor placed a small crown on Chunhua’s head […].

Questions and Activity 3

📖 Book text 5/6

The foundation of the UAE (1)

Read the article.

Activity 4: Fairness is one of the key pillars on which the UAE was founded. Read the article about the foundation of the UAE. Then discuss the points that follow. In 1960, an economic movement started in the country fuelled by the good news of oil discovery in Abu Dhabi. The movement was started by establishing the ‘Trucial States Council’. […] Sheikh Zayed met with Sheikh Rashid at Seih Al-Sudaira, located between Abu Dhabi and Dubai, in February 1968. […] thanks to good communication, and the commitment of the seven rulers of the UAE, work began on the modern UAE.

Student Book Vol. 1 · page 28 · Reading 5

Student Book p. 28 (selected passages). Teacher check: the book text dates the Trucial States Council to 1960; sources usually date it earlier. The book is quoted as written and the point is flagged, not corrected.

📖 Book text 6/6

The foundation of the UAE (2)

Read and discuss.

[…] the late Sheikh Zayed bin Sultan Al Nahyan used all the resources available to him to develop the country and raise the standard of living. […] Under Sheikh Zayed’s presidency, the UAE was industrialised and thousands of people moved from houses made of vines and mud into clean, healthy homes. Fresh water and electricity were provided to every house, new roads were built and the education system was modernised.

Through his values, Sheikh Zayed led the people […]. The values of fairness, generosity and spreading world peace became the backbone of the country. Fairness and justice were essential parts of his life and work. Sheikh Zayed was fair with his family, his community and with all people. His way of life turned the country into a place for anyone seeking justice and security.

Discuss: (a) Discuss the importance of justice and equality in the UAE. Write down your ideas. (b) Write down an example of fair distribution in the UAE. Activity 5: Draw a picture showing an unfair situation in the world. Then, show your drawing to your classmates.
