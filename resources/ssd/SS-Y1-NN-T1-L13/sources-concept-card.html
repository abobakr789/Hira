CONCEPT CARD

My school community

A community is a group of people who live close together or do the same things. A school is a community. We can solve problems together by sharing and talking.

My school is a community and I am part of it.

Page 103–104 · Activities 1 and 2

Activity 1: A community is a group of people who live close together or do the same things. A school is a community, and you are part of the school community. Can you think of other communities?

Activity 2: Write about your school community. Draw your classroom. Where is the teacher’s desk? Where is the whiteboard? Who do you sit with?
