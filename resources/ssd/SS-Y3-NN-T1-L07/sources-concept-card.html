📖 Book text

Student Book Vol. 1 · Original text from the Student Book.

Page 66–67 · Reading 1

Who are the important people in your life? Why? Which places are important to you? Why? What things are important to you? Why?

Activity 1: What Matters to Me? (a) Think about the people, things and places that are important to you. (b) Write your ideas on three pieces of paper. (Use one for important people in your life, one for your important things and one for your important places.) (c) Stick them on the board.

Page 68–69 · Reading 2

As the curtain went up, my brother Abdullah appeared on stage with his friends. […] Dressed in the Emirati flag, Abdullah went up to a girl. She was wearing a traditional Lebanese dress. […] Abdullah asked, ‘Where are you going to take us, Raya?’ She replied, ‘Abdullah, I am very happy to unite my country’s flag with the flag of the country I live in now – the UAE. Our countries have shared ties of brotherhood and friendship. They respect one another and work together. I’m going to take you, Julia, Sam and Hassan to my world.’

‘We’re going to leave the United Arab Emirates now and travel to Lebanon. My home country is also in Asia but is on the Mediterranean Sea. It’s one of the smallest Arab countries. It has people with many different cultures.’ Raya pointed to the map. ‘This is Byblos, where the Phoenicians invented their alphabet. Here you can see our coastline. And here are our mountains, which are capped with snow in winter. Over here is the citadel of Baalbek, and this is the Jeita Grotto. Down in the south is the ancient city of Tyre, where the dye “Tyrian purple” was first made.’

History check: UNESCO links Byblos to the history and spread of the Phoenician alphabet. This does not establish a single invention site for every stage of alphabetic writing.

Page 70 · Reading 3

Questions: (1) What do you think Raya meant when she referred to ‘shared ties of brotherhood and friendship’? (2) Do you know of any Lebanese regions that Raya didn’t mention? (3) Do you know what they are known for? (4) What shows that there are many different people with different cultures in Lebanon? (5) What have the worlds of Abdullah and Raya got in common?

Let’s Think Together. What does the expression ‘My Beloved World’ mean to you?

Page 71–73 · Reading 4

Activity 3: My World in Boxes. (a) You are going to draw four pictures. In your drawings, try to show what is important to you. Your home or your family · Your school or your friends · Your country · Your city or community. (b) Work in pairs. Show your drawings to your partner. Explain how the pictures show what is important to you.

Activity 4: Customs and Traditions around the World. (a) Choose a country and research a custom, tradition or event celebrated there. (b) Collect pictures and information about your research topic. (c) Present your work to the class. The pictures show Brazil, Kuwait and the United Arab Emirates.

Page 74–75 · Reading 5

Activity 5: Tell the class about a traditional craft practised by your family or in your country. Describe its purpose and benefits.

Let’s talk

① Which country did Raya take us to? ② What do the UAE and Lebanon share, according to Raya? ③ What do we draw in My World in Boxes?
