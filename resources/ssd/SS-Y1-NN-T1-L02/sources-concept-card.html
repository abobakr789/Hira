CONCEPT CARD

Showing love

We show love to our friends and family with kind words and kind actions. Love makes us happy and makes others happy too.

I can show love when I share, help, hug and say kind words.

Page 18 · Activity 2

A group of friends sit together in the school dining hall. It’s time for lunch!

Yara looks at Rashid. He’s sad. “Why are you sad, Rashid?” asks Yara.

“I don’t have my lunch bag,” says Rashid.

“Why didn’t you tell me?” asks Yara. “You can have some of my cheese sandwich,”

“Thanks, Yara. But it’s your sandwich.”

“I have lots of food. Here my friend. Take it.”

Ahmad looks at his friends and says, “Can we all share our food?”

“Great idea!” says John.

Page 19 · Activity 2

“I have lots of food to share,” says Khaled.

They open their lunch bags and put their food on the table. Now, there’s a lot of food! There are apples, dates, juice, sandwiches and water on the table.

“How kind of you, Yara! How nice of you, Mila! How caring of you, John! How kind of you, Khaled! It’s so good to have friends! And I’m so happy you are my friends!” says Rashid.

Rashid feels happy.

Let’s talk

① Why did Ahmad want to share the food? ② Do you like to eat on your own? ③ Do you share food with your friends?
