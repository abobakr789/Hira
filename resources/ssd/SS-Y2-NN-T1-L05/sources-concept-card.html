CONCEPT CARD

Put yourself in my shoes

Understanding means knowing how someone feels. Sympathy means showing that you care when someone is upset or new. A smile and kind words can help. We can imagine how we would feel and treat others as we like to be treated.

I understand how others feel and I show sympathy.

Page 36 · Activity 2

A boy stood beside the farm fence. He was pointing to the animals with a smile on his face and speaking in a different language.

‘Hi there! Who are you?’ Nour asked the boy. He stopped smiling and started to look worried.

‘Would you like to play with me? Come into the farm,’ she added. Scared and upset, the boy ran away.

Nour asked her neighbours, ‘Do you know who the new boy is?’

‘He’s our new neighbour Bruno. He came with his family from Brazil. He is always by himself and doesn’t talk or play with anyone. We often notice him standing by the farm fence. We don’t know why he just stands there.’

Page 36 · Activity 2

Nour thought it was strange and told her father, Salem, all about it.

‘Yes, he is Brazilian and doesn’t speak our language. That is why he’s always alone. Why don’t you invite him over to play?’ Salem said.

‘I did, but he didn’t say anything and just ran away,’ Nour said.

‘Try inviting him again. Next time you see him, offer him a piece of halwa. Bruno’s from a country far away and doesn’t know anyone here. He must be feeling homesick.’

‘How do you know this, Dad?’ Nour started thinking…

Page 37 · Let’s Think

How does Noor’s Dad know how Bruno feels?

Why did Bruno run away when Noor spoke to him?

Imagine being in a new country where you don’t know anyone. How would you feel? How would you like others to treat you?

Page 38 · Activity 3

What would you say to the people in the photos?

Page 39 · Activity 4 and 5

Activity 4. Tell your partner about a time when you felt upset. Your partner should show sympathy. Then change roles. Talk about your feelings in both roles.

You should both express your feelings: when you show sympathy to your partner and when your partner shows sympathy to you.

Activity 5. Make a sympathy card. Think of a situation where someone has a problem and is in need of sympathy. How would you show your sympathy to them? On coloured card, draw the situation. Show the card to the class. Explain the importance of expressing your feelings to people who need sympathy.
