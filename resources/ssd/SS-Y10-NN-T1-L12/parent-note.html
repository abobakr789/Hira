👪 Note for parents and carers

Lesson: Helping Others to Fight and Overcome Addiction · Year 10 · Moral, Social and Cultural Studies (Student Book Vol. 1, pages 182–194)

What we learned

This week we learned how people can be helped to overcome addiction: stigma, counselling, therapy, detox and rehabilitation, the role of families, and how the UAE helps.

How you can help at home

Ask: “Who can you ask for help if you need it?”

Keep talking openly and kindly about difficult topics.

Show understanding, not anger or judgement.

Home task

Talk with your child about why asking for help is a strength. It is a family conversation; nothing is marked.
