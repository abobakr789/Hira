CONCEPT CARD

Living in groups

People and animals live in groups. Animals live in groups to find and share food and to stay safe. People live in families, where they care for each other and learn from each other.

Living in a group helps us stay safe and learn.

Page 86 · Activity 2

People and animals live in groups. Animals live in groups with names like ‘flock’, ‘swarm’, ‘pride’ or ‘school’. Each animal in a group or person in a family is a member of that group or family.

Lots of different animals live in groups. They live in groups to find and share food. It’s important for younger animals to be part of a group. The older animals can teach the young animals. They can help take care of the young animals too.

It’s a lot safer for animals to live in groups.

Page 86 · Activity 2

People live in families. Family members live together and take care of each other. They can share work and help each other. Children can learn a lot from their parents and their grandparents. Families have fun together too!
