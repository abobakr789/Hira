CONCEPT CARD

How healthy is your lifestyle?

Healthy habits include good nutrition, regular exercise and enough sleep. The article says these are among the most important factors for health and should be learned early in life. The Mediterranean diet emphasises olive oil, fish, whole grains, fruit and vegetables, and legumes.

Build healthy habits early.

Words to know

nutritionthe food a body needs to grow and stay healthysleep patternhow much and when a person sleeps
dietthe food and drink a person usually eatsobesityhaving too much body fat, which can cause health problems
carbohydratea nutrient that gives the body energychronic diseasea health problem that lasts a long time
exercisephysical activity that keeps the body fitportionthe amount of food served to one person

📖 Book text

Student Book Vol. 1 · Original text from the Student Book.

Page 58–61 · Reading 1

Unit questions: What can I do to stay healthy and protect the health of others? What does it mean to be healthy? How can you maintain a healthy body? What factors contribute to poor health? What are some big health issues in the world today? What can we do to ensure good health for all?

Lesson questions: What are healthy habits? Why are healthy habits important for people? What might happen to people who do not have healthy habits?

Activity 1: Read the quote. What do you think it means? “Take care of your body. It is the only place you have to live.” Jim Rohn.

Page 62 · Reading 2

Activity 2: Read the article, Learning the Health Habit, and answer the questions. With child obesity and other health problems like diabetes on the rise in developed countries, child nutrition and health have become frequent topics of discussion. […] The main sources of obesity are the food choices we make, lack of physical activity, and family habits that are not in line with a healthy lifestyle. Doctors fear that the increased frequency of obesity could lead to chronic health problems in adulthood. Experts suggest that good nutrition, physical exercise, and sleeping patterns are among the most important factors that contribute to our health. They also suggest that it is essential that good habits are learned early on in life.

Page 62–63 · Reading 3

[…] It is important to understand the nutritional value of food and how to prepare and cook quick and easy healthy meals. Exercise should be incorporated into our daily routine, and we should make sure to get enough sleep to ensure we have the healthy minds and bodies we need to achieve our full potential in life and avoid health problems, like obesity or diabetes, in later life.

Questions: (a) In what ways do health issues affect the country as well as the individual? (b) Why do you think it’s easier to learn good habits early in life? (c) What family habits can contribute to health problems like the ones mentioned in the text?

Page 64–65 · Reading 4

Activity 3: Read the text below and answer the questions that follow. The Mediterranean diet is known for its health benefits and claims to deter chronic diseases, especially those which are related to the heart. It places great emphasis on olive oil, fish, whole grains, fruit and vegetables, and legumes as its main foods. […] As a result, this kind of diet is low in saturated fat and cholesterol, both of which can be dangerous for heart health. In this kind of diet, protein is obtained from fish and legumes like lentils and beans […]. It is also believed that following a Mediterranean diet can benefit the health of your brain. […]

Questions: (a) Give examples of each of the following food groups: fruits, vegetables, proteins, grains, dairy. (b) Draw the correct portion for each food group on the plate in the image.

Page 66 · Reading 5

Activity 4: In small groups, read the following statements and decide whether they are True or False. (a) Frying is the healthiest method for cooking food. (b) Eating homemade food contributes to a healthy body. (c) The average 6–13 year old should sleep 9–11 hours per day. (d) Using electronic devices helps us sleep well at night. (e) Regular exercise can harm bones and muscle.

Page 67 · Reading 6

Activity 5: Look at the pictures below and discuss with your partner which are healthy and which are unhealthy. Ask your partner what food they like to eat, then evaluate their lunch box and advise if it needs to be healthier. The picture groups are: Fruit and Vegetables, Treats, Grains, Dairy, Soft drinks, Proteins.
