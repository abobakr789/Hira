CONCEPT CARD

Different kinds of maps

Different maps show different information. The legend shows what the colours and symbols mean. Political maps show borders of countries and can show the capital city. A population density map shows how many people live in 1 square kilometre.

The legend helps us read a map.

Words to know

political mapa map that shows the borders of countriesborderthe line around each country
population densityhow many people live in 1 square kilometrecapital citythe main city of a country
legendshows what the colours and symbols on the map meanSouth Americaa continent shown on a political map in the book

There are different kinds of maps which show different information. Map titles and map legends are important. The legend shows what the different colours and symbols on the map mean. The map has a legend in the lower left corner. It describes what each colour means.

Challenge Question: What do the colours show on the map?

Page 191–194 · Reading 3

Political maps show borders of countries. A border is the line around each country. Political maps often use colours to help identify each country. Political maps can also show things like the capital city of a country.

Activity 4: Look at the political map of South America. Practice saying the names of the countries and capital cities with a partner. Challenge Question: What problems might a country have if it is not next to an ocean?

Activity 6: Population Density Map. This world population density map shows how many people live in 1 square kilometre. One square kilometre is about the size of 10 football fields put together side-by-side. What other information do you see on the population density map? Not every symbol on the map is in the legend. However, we can look at other symbols to know where things are.

Activity 7: Using the legend in the population density map, answer the questions. Which continent has the most areas where 200 to 8100 people live in each square kilometre? How many people live in each square kilometre in the yellow areas? Why does Antarctica have the lightest colour? What does it mean as the colour of areas become redder?

Activity 8: The two maps of Pakistan show different information. Map A is a political map. What information does map B give? In the political map, what do the colours mean? In the rainfall map, what do the colours mean? How much rain does the city of Zhob receive each year? What do the darker colours mean in the rainfall map?

Activity 9: Use the city map to answer the question. Where is the restaurant? Then, work with a partner and make your own questions about the map.
