CONCEPT CARD

Rules and freedom

Rules are things we must or must not do. They keep us safe and fair. Our behaviour affects others. Freedom means we can choose, but we must respect the rights of others. Good behaviour helps everyone learn.

I follow the rules and respect the rights of others.

Pick a card.

Page 52 · Activity 2

A new student started at our school today. His name is Adam. Adam wasn’t very organised. When the class began he was still looking for his books and pencils. When he finally started paying attention to the class, he kept interrupting the teacher and speaking when it wasn’t his turn. When the boys were playing football, Adam pushed them out of the way. He wanted to win, but he didn’t want to follow the rules. Then, when it was time to stand in line, Adam pushed to the front.

The teacher said, ‘I’m sorry, but you can’t push in front like that. We’ve got rules here and we all have to respect them.’

Page 52–53 · Activity 2

Adam was surprised. ‘Why are you all trying to stop me? I can do what I want.’

The teacher said, ‘Yes, you do have the right to have your say, but the rights of others have to be respected as well. And that means following the rules.’

Adam said, ‘But why do I always have to respect the rules? What about my freedom?’

Then it was time for reading class. The teacher told everyone to sit on the floor in a circle. She was going to tell a story. Adam rushed to the front, next to the teacher. He said, ‘Great! I really love listening to stories.’

The teacher looked at him and said, ‘Hmm… now I know which story to tell.’

Page 53 · Let’s Think

What does Adam mean when he says, ‘Why do I always have to respect the rules? What about my freedom?’

If Adam was in your class, what advice would you give him?

How would you describe Adam’s behaviour?

What do you think the subject of the teacher’s story is going to be?

Page 54 · Activity 3

When do we follow rules and when do we do what we want?

1. wearing what I want

2. speaking without permission

3. interrupting others

4. choosing my own friends

5. annoying people I don’t want to play with

6. talking loudly when others are working

Write a sentence about the difference between personal freedom and respecting the rules.

Page 55 · Activity 4 and 5

Activity 4. Draw a red circle around behaviours that help learning. Draw a blue circle around the behaviours that stop learning. having rules · being ready · listening · showing respect · cooperating · making noise · thinking · racing in the corridor

Activity 5. Talk in groups about classroom behaviour. a. Think of a classroom behaviour that could be improved. b. Think how it could be improved. c. Persuade other groups to improve the behaviour.
