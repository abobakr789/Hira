CONCEPT CARD

Life in coastal cities

Merchants are people who buy and sell products. In the past, they sailed in dhows to other countries. They sold dates, dried fish and pearls, and bought rice, spices and cloth. Selling and buying is called trade. The small coastal towns of the past grew into big cities with large modern ports. Ships bring goods from all over the world to the UAE.

Trading has always been important in the UAE.

Page 110 · Activity 3

Trading has always been important to the people of the UAE. In the past, merchants sailed to other countries to buy and sell products. They would use dhows to travel on the sea.

The merchants sold things from the UAE like dates, dried fish and pearls. They used the money to buy things from other countries. They bought rice, spices and cloth. When the merchants came back, they sold the new things to people in the coastal towns. A coastal town is a town next to the sea. Selling things you have and buying things you need or want is called trading.

Page 110–111 · Activity 3

The small coastal towns of the past grew into the big cities of today. The big cities have large modern ports that can take in a large number of ships. Ships bring goods from all over the world to the UAE. Ships take goods from the UAE to cities of the world.

The Dhow. A dhow is a wooden boat used by sailors to travel on the sea. Dhows were used for fishing and for trading. Merchants used dhows to carry goods such as fruit, dates, fish, rice and spices. In the past dhows had no motors. How do you think they moved?
