My place in the world

We live in a school, a city, an emirate, a country, a continent and on one planet. The UAE is on the continent of Asia, in the Arabian Gulf region. Its capital is Abu Dhabi. The UAE is characterised by cultural diversity.

We all share one world.

📖 Book text

Student Book Vol. 1 · Original text from the Student Book.

Page 56–57 · Reading 1

What is your favourite Emirati food? What is your favourite UAE celebration or holiday? What is your favourite place in the UAE?

Vocabulary: location, planet, country, planet Earth, continent, map, world.

Page 58 · Reading 2

Activity 1: Questionnaire. Answer the questions. (a) What is the name of your school? (b) Where do you live? (c) What is the name of your street? (d) What is the name of the emirate you live in? (e) What is the name of the country you live in? (f) What is the name of your home country? (g) What is the name of the city or town you are from?

Work in pairs. Compare your information with your classmate’s information. Write what is the same and what is different.

Page 58–59 · Reading 3

I went with my family to my brother Abdullah’s school. We were going to see a play at the school to mark the World Day for Cultural Diversity. […] Then we all stood up for the national anthem of the United Arab Emirates. Abdullah appeared on stage and started to welcome the actors. ‘I am happy to welcome my friends Sam from India, Raya from Lebanon, Hassan from Egypt and Julia from the United States of America.’

‘Welcome to the Land of Happiness exhibition. […] Let me introduce you to the heritage of the United Arab Emirates.’ Abdullah held the brightly coloured flag of the United Arab Emirates. He pointed to a large map of the world beside him and said, ‘This is where I am, on the continent of Asia, in the Arabian Gulf region.’ […] ‘Here you can see our capital, Abu Dhabi, with its clear waters, colourful fish and pearls. These birds are falcons and over here we have the historic Qasr Al Hosn Palace. These beautiful palm trees and these magnificent horses running through the desert symbolise our country.’

Page 60–62 · Reading 4

Questions: (1) Why do you think all celebrations start with the national anthem? (2) What do you know about the countries mentioned in the text? (3) Do you know of any Emirati features and symbols that are not mentioned in the text? (4) Do you know any features and symbols of other countries?

Let’s think together about the statement, ‘The UAE is characterised by cultural diversity.’ What do you think this means? Share your thoughts with the class.

Activity 3: Map of the world. Find the continent of Asia and mark it on the map. Find the Gulf region and mark it on the map. Find the United Arab Emirates and colour its borders red.

Page 63–65 · Reading 5

Activity 4: A Country I Know. Work in pairs. Name a country and find it on the world map. Then write a short description of where the country is located and some information about it. Example: Egypt is a big country in North Africa. It has a very rich history. It also has many old buildings, for example the pyramids. The language of Egypt is Arabic, though many people speak English as well.

Activity 5: My Country. Imagine that a friend is coming to visit you from abroad. He/she wants to know everything about your country. Complete the table below with information that will be of interest to him/her. Country name · Famous local dishes · National language · Places and landmarks · Celebrations and holidays · Traditions and customs.

Let’s talk

① Which continent is the UAE in? ② What is the capital of the UAE, according to the story? ③ What does cultural diversity mean?
