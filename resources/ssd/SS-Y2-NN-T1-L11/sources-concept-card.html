Page 84 · Activity 3

A century ago, the area now called the United Arab Emirates, or UAE, was called the Trucial States. At that time, most people made a living by farming, fishing, and trading. Many men were also pearl divers. Money from selling pearls helped the Trucial States grow. In 1912, the first national school named Al Ahmadiya School opened in the emirate of Dubai.

Page 86 · Activity 3

In 1893, a man named Kokichi Mikimoto came up with a way to make pearls. This meant that pearls could be made cheaply in pearl farms. By 1930, pearl divers and traders could not sell expensive natural pearls anymore. Life became difficult in the Trucial States.

In 1958, oil was discovered in the UAE. Abu Dhabi first exported oil in 1962. Dubai first exported oil in 1969. The leaders used the money from selling oil to build their emirates.
