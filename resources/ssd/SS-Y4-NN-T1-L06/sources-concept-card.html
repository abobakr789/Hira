CONCEPT CARD

Development of both mind and body

Personal hygiene means keeping our body clean. A healthy lifestyle includes a healthy diet and regular exercise. Exercise produces endorphins that make us happier and helps our heart, lungs, muscles and bones. Looking after our body also helps our mental health.

A healthy body and a healthy mind help us achieve success.

Page 62–63 · Activity 2

Read the story of Khalid. Then answer the questions that follow.

Khalid is an 11-year-old boy. He is very popular with his classmates. One day, he noticed that his classmates were not talking to him. They whispered about him behind his back and complained when they had to sit next to him. Confused and upset, Khalid didn’t know what was wrong. He started to fall behind in class.

At home, he didn’t pay attention and he didn’t talk with his family. He also became less excited about going to school. He began to feel sick and get stomachache, especially in the morning just before the school bus arrived. Khalid’s parents became worried about his mental and physical well-being. They contacted the school’s counsellor to discuss their concerns. The counsellor asked Khalid to come and see him. He asked Khalid to tell him the real reason he was feeling so down. He also talked to two of Khalid’s classmates and was surprised to find out that the entire class was put off by Khalid smelling of sweat after PE.

Page 66 · Activity 4

Read about the importance of exercise. Then, write about your favourite sport. Think about how playing your favourite sport benefits you physically and mentally.

1. Exercise produces hormones, called endorphins, making us happier and reducing stress.

2. It helps us make good use of our spare time.

3. It helps to develop fitness, strength and healthy muscles and bones.

4. It helps to burn calories and turns nutrients into useful energy. It also reduces fat, especially around the stomach, thighs, and hips. This helps us keep a good weight and body shape.

5. It gives you a healthy heart. Exercise makes the heart work harder. The heart then beats faster and pumps blood well. This in turn helps to pass oxygen and energy to the brain and muscles.

6. It improves our lungs. They can then provide more oxygen to the body and send it to the organs, especially the brain and muscles.
