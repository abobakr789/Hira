CONCEPT CARD

Modern-day UAE

The UAE is a modern, developed country. The leaders used oil money to build roads, hospitals and schools. People come from more than 200 nationalities and live in peace with tolerance. Places can be natural (mountains, lakes) or man-made (buildings). The UAE helps others through charity, such as the Emirates Red Crescent.

Peace and tolerance make the UAE special.

Page 145–146 · Modern-Day UAE

Nowadays, the UAE is a modern, developed country. After oil was discovered in the UAE, the wise leaders used the money from selling oil to help the people. They built roads, hospitals, schools, and other important places. What makes the UAE special?

People. The people that live in the UAE come from more than 200 countries. Peace and tolerance among all people makes the UAE a very special place. In 2021, the UAE made history as the first Arab country to reach Mars. The Emirates Mars mission, named the Hope probe, reached Mars on February 9th.

Accuracy note: The UAE Government reports more than 200 nationalities. This is not a count of countries.

Page 147–148 · Modern-Day UAE

Cities. The UAE has modern and developed cities. There are tall buildings, parks, hotels, museums, amusement parks, and other places to visit.

Achievements. The UAE seeks to be a leader in the fields of science and space. The astronaut Hazza Al Mansoori was the first Emirati to reach space on 25 September, 2019. Khalifa-Sat was the first satellite made by Emiratis sent into space.

Generosity. The UAE government offers help to people in other countries. Charity organizations such as the Emirates Red Crescent also help people in other countries to build hospitals and schools.
