CONCEPT CARD

Fair and unfair

Fair means right for everyone. Unfair means not right for everyone. When something is unfair, we can feel sad. We can talk about our feelings and try to be fair.

I can be fair when I share, take turns and listen.

Page 26 · Activity 2

In the school holidays, Rashid goes to the zoo. Rashid’s uncle works at the zoo. His uncle takes care of the animals. Rashid helps his uncle.

Every morning, his uncle feeds the animals. He gives a big bowl of food to the elephant. He gives a smaller bowl of food to the giraffe. He gives the smallest bowl of food to the rabbit.

Rashid talks to the animals. The giraffe and the rabbit are sad. They think Rashid’s uncle is unfair. Why does the elephant get a big bowl of food?

Rashid wants to help the animals. He asks his uncle if he can feed the animals. His uncle is happy. Rashid feeds the animals for one week.

Page 26 · Activity 2

Rashid wants to be fair to the giraffe, the rabbit and the elephant. He shares the food into three bowls. Now, the giraffe and rabbit are happy. They have the same amount of food as the elephant! It’s fair!

After a week, the animals feel bad. The rabbit is fat and sleepy. He can’t run or jump. The giraffe is hungry and sad. The elephant can’t stand up because he’s so hungry.

Rashid tells his uncle the animals need help! Rashid’s uncle calls the vet. Now, Rashid understands how to be fair.
