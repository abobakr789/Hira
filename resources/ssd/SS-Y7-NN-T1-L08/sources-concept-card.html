CONCEPT CARD

First aid and the Red Crescent

First aid is first help for a hurt or ill person. The book describes steps for choking and for an unresponsive person, but says abdominal thrusts can harm young children and a professional is preferred. The Emirates Red Crescent, established on 31 January 1983, is a volunteer humanitarian organisation, and Red Crescent Day is 8th May, says the book. The official UAE portal lists 998 for an ambulance.

Prepare and help.

Page 80–81 · Reading 1

What are some basic first aid skills? What is the Emirates Red Crescent? Activity 1: Complete the following activity. There are many different incidents that require first aid. Would you know how to perform first aid if one of your friends was injured? (a) First aid is: … (b) Why is it important to know first aid?

Page 82–84 · Reading 2

Activity 2: The Emirates Red Crescent is a volunteer humanitarian organisation. It was established on 31 January 1983 and was internationally attested as a member of the International Federation of Red Cross and Red Crescent Societies in 1986. […] In times of peace: organising awareness programmes, providing first aid, protecting and controlling epidemics, paying attention to social issues, providing various humanitarian assistance for vulnerable, needy people and victims of accidents and disasters. In times of war: transferring and treating the wounded, providing first aid and relief to victims, protecting civilians, and searching for missing persons and reuniting separated families. […] Our values: transparency, credibility, humanitarian partnership with civil society institutions, and encouraging voluntary work as a value in itself.

Questions: (a) Give some real-life examples of Red Crescent work. Say how the Red Crescent’s intervention alleviated people’s suffering. (b) Design a poster for the Emirates Red Crescent outlining the work that it does. Key fact: Red Crescent Day is 8th May.

Page 85–87 · Reading 3

Activity 3: Choking can be scary. It can happen in a second, so it is good to be prepared. Here are the steps that are done if a person is choking. 1. Hit the back: give 5 blows in the back, just between their shoulder blades. 2. Ask a question: if the person cannot respond or is having trouble breathing, then it is severe choking. 3. Give abdominal thrusts: do five sharp inward and upward thrusts. Note: this procedure can cause harm to young children and should preferably be performed by a professional. Repeat steps 2 and 3 until the person stops choking.

Questions: (a) Make a list of items that may cause choking. (b) How would you know that someone was choking? (c) How do you think it might feel if you were choking? (d) Why could abdominal thrusts be harmful to babies under 1 year of age?

Page 87–89 · Reading 4

Activity 4: Now that we have looked at what to do when someone is choking, we’ll look at how to put them into the recovery position. The steps are based on posters from St. John Ambulance, a first aid organisation in the UK. What to do if someone is unresponsive: 1. Open their airway. 2. Tilt head. 3. Check for normal breathing: look, listen and feel. 4. If they are breathing, put them in the recovery position, then call for an ambulance. If they are not breathing, call for emergency help and start CPR.

Questions: (a) Discuss with your classmates at least three situations where you might use this. (b) In groups of three, practise putting each other in the recovery position.

Page 89–91 · Reading 5

Activity 5: Now that you have learned some first aid skills, read each story below and decide how you would react to them. Scenario 1: you are playing football and one of the players collapses on the pitch. He appears to be unconscious but is still breathing. Scenario 2: you are out shopping with your parents in a busy street. An old woman falls on the stairs and points to her leg. Scenario 3: you see a man walking unsteadily; he is clutching his chest, then collapses. Scenario 4: a friend chokes on his food while telling a story. “Help me,” he gasps.

Activity 6: Think of a scenario that hasn’t been mentioned in this lesson. Write the scenario and what first aid you should give in that situation.
