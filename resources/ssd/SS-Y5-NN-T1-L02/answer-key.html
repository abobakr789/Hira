Worksheet Level 1

Task 1: a hard situation where it is hard to decide what is right. Task 2: circle moral, sympathy, generosity, dilemma. Task 3: Open.

Worksheet Level 2

Task 1: 1 True · 2 True · 3 True · 4 True. Task 2: Suggested: 1 sad or helpless · 2 landlord · 3 neighbours. Task 3: Answers can vary; accept reasoned answers.

Worksheet Level 3

All tasks are open. Look for: clear sentences about a moral choice; a sensible decision with a reason; a clear poster with one sentence.
