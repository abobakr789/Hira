Our new team

A good team has a plan and a leader. Everyone must join in and keep to the plan. Team skills are helping, listening, sharing, being tolerant and making an effort. Teamwork brings success.

I keep to the plan and use my team skills.

Page 64 · Activity 2

Adam’s mother kept talking to him about the importance of teamwork. However, Adam wasn’t paying attention. He was thinking about his new school and how he could fit in. The next day at school, the students were getting ready to play a football match. Adam’s coach split the team into attacking players and defenders. He said that it was important for everyone to keep to the plan. Attacking players should attack. Defending players should defend.

The match started. Adam’s team was keeping to the plan. Adam was playing in defence. Suddenly, he decided to change the plan. He wanted to score a goal by himself. He ran towards the goals, but the other team ran towards Adam and took the ball. They scored a goal.

Page 64–65 · Activity 2

Adam’s teammates were angry and shouted at him, ‘What do you think you are doing?’

The coach said, ‘Why didn’t you stick to the plan? They wouldn’t have scored if you had stayed in defence.’

Adam felt bad about what he had done. He said, ‘I thought I was doing what was best for the team. I’m really sorry.’

He wondered what he could do to put things right.

Page 65 · Activity 2

Adam came to a decision. He would stay in defence for the rest of the match.

At the end of the match, when the referee blew his whistle, Adam’s team had won.

Adam, his teammates and the coach were all happy.

Let’s Think: Why is it important to work as a team?

Questions: How do you think Adam feels about his teammates at the beginning of the match? Why do you think Adam’s team won the match? When do you think teamwork is important?
