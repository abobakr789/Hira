Page 87–88 · Activity 1

What are some problems that the environment is facing these days? How do you think the situation will improve or get worse when you are older?

Key Vocabulary: noise pollution, environmental pollution, environmental responsibility.

Activity 1. Social responsibility is shown through participating in the protection of the environment. Why does the environment need to be protected? What does it need to be protected against?

Page 89 · Activity 2

Read the text about marine pollution. Then answer the questions that follow.

Mohammed and Mark were exercising on the beach. While they were running, they saw algae and dead fish in the water. As they were talking about this, a man came along and took some photographs. Mark was interested and said, ‘Why are you taking pictures? Are you a journalist?’

The man said that he wasn’t a journalist, but a teacher of moral education. He said his name was Ziad and that he was taking the pictures to show his class the next day. Ziad explained that he was teaching his class about the environment. He wanted his students to be aware of environmental issues and be prepared to take responsibility for protecting the natural world.

Page 90–91 · Activities 2 and 3

1. Look at the photograph Ziad took. Why do you think the fish died? 2. What everyday human activities cause environmental pollution? 3. Work in groups. How can we reduce environmental pollution caused by human activity?

Activity 3. Look at the two images. Which one do you think would be more effective in fighting pollution. Why? What is the role of the media in educating people about the threat of pollution to the environment? Discuss and write down some of your ideas.

Page 92 · Activity 4

Read the text about the late Sheikh Zayed bin Sultan Al Nahyan (May God have mercy upon him)’s interest in sustainable environmental development and how it affects life in the UAE today. Then do the activity that follows.

From 1966, the late Sheikh Zayed bin Sultan Al Nahyan (May God have mercy upon him), was deeply concerned about environmental issues. For example, in 1977, he banned fishing in Abu Dhabi. However, perhaps his greatest achievement was the greening of the desert. Fact check: the ban concerned fishing with explosives, not all fishing. Sources differ on 1976 or 1977. Oryx recovery involved regional and international teams, alongside Sheikh Zayed's important work.

The late leader is respected for his many important environmental programmes, especially those aimed at stopping desertification. Under his rule, ground water was protected, bridges were built, and plants were grown using environmentally-friendly soil fertilisers manufactured in a purpose-built facility.

One of the key achievements of Sheikh Zayed was the roadside planting scheme along the Abu Dhabi-Al Ain highway. As a result of this scheme, large parts of desert became lush and green. This greening policy continues today, with more trees, more gardens and more flowers being planted every year.

He also established several nature reserves, the most important being the Sir Bani Yas Island Nature Reserve. In terms of size and diversity, this is one of the biggest man-made nature reserves in the Arabian Peninsula. It is an important sanctuary for rare species of endangered animals, especially birds. Sheikh Zayed was personally responsible for saving the Arabian Oryx from extinction.

Page 93–94 · Activities 4 and 5

“We have worked, since the establishment of our state, to protect the environment and wildlife and have established all the systems, legislations, programs and projects that have made the UAE a pioneer and a world-class model in environmental care, protection and preservation.” Sheikh Zayed bin Sultan Al Nahyan

Work in groups. In what ways do the achievements of Sheikh Zayed impact the lives of citizens and residents of the UAE today?

Activity 5. Make a poster about your country’s natural beauty. Get information and pictures from newspapers, magazines, or websites. Then use your poster to talk about your country to your class.

Page 94–96 · Activity 6

Developing an environmental project. Work in groups. You are going to work on a project on a global environmental issue. Choose an issue that interests you. For example, you could choose something like desertification, global warming, disruption of ecosystems, lack of fresh water, depletion of natural resources, or diseases caused by pollution. Consider all aspects of the issue. Present the problem in the form of a question that needs a solution. Then present your solution. Follow these steps: Research check: test your hypothesis against the evidence. It is valid to revise or reject it.

1. Preparation. Choose your topic. What specific questions are you going to ask? 2. Hypothesis. Develop your hypothesis. Think about how your issue affects the environment. 3. Information gathering. Find information to support your hypothesis. Use websites recommended by your teacher to find the information you need. Be prepared to compare different solutions. If possible, interview environmental experts. Use the information you find to determine the overall environmental impact of the issue you have chosen. Don’t forget to write down your references.

4. Evaluate the information you collected. Select what is most important. Present your information using graphs, charts, pictures, and maps. Ensure that your information addresses the issue and that your hypothesis is correct. Make sure that your presentation includes a conclusion with recommendations. Suggest relevant, practical and scientific solutions to the problem. Think of ways these can be adopted in the UAE and other countries. Are there any recent technological developments that can be part of the solution? 5. Conclusion. Take a step back. Assess the work you have done and conclusions reached. Was your hypothesis correct? Consider the environmental impact of the factor you considered. 6. Written report. Using the presentation you created, write up your research in the form of an academic report. State the stages you went through, your research procedures, and your conclusions. 7. Presentation. With your team, present your report to the class or school. Show your procedures and conclusions clearly using slides and graphics. Make sure you practise your presentation beforehand.
