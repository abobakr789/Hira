Student Book Vol. 1 · page 201

Palaeontology studies ancient life through fossils. Some fossils are millions of years old. Radiocarbon dating is different from dating all fossils: it measures carbon-14 in suitable once-living material and usually reaches back about 50,000 years. Older finds need other dating methods and geological evidence. A date is an estimate with uncertainty, not an exact birthday.

Archaeologists travel to areas where ancient civilisations are known to have existed
