CONCEPT CARD

Exercise and healthy habits

There are three types of exercise: aerobic, anaerobic and flexibility and stretching. Sports help the immune system and also help release stress. Basic hygiene, such as washing hands and brushing teeth at least twice a day, protects health. A Health Diary records diet, activity and hygiene.

Move, keep clean, record your habits.

Page 70–71 · Reading 2

Activity 2: Read the information about different types of exercise and the benefits associated with each. Aerobic (also known as Cardiovascular or Cardio): Aerobic means relating to free oxygen. […] This type of exercise helps maintain a healthy weight as well as regulating digestive health and strengthening the immune system.

Anaerobic (also known as strength or resistance training): This type of exercise tones your muscles making them stronger and firmer. It requires short intense actions that are not reliant on a huge use of oxygen. It also improves the health of your bones, as well as your balance and coordination.

Flexibility and Stretching: This type of exercise stretches and lengthens your muscles to increase your range of movement. […] Stretching should only be started when muscles are warm and the body temperature is raised. This type of exercise also creates good posture, helps to prevent injury during more intensive exercise […].

Page 71 · Reading 3

Look at the sports and activities below. To which category of exercise do they belong? Can you add any more to the list? Swimming, Sprinting, Zumba, Ballet, Martial Arts, Football, Gymnastics, Walking, Push Ups. The categories are: Aerobic, Anaerobic and Flexibility and Stretching.

Page 72 · Reading 4

Activity 3: Read about the health benefits of playing sports. Sports activities have many benefits for physical and mental health. Studies showed that regular moderate sports have a positive effect on the immune system. When one exercises, the heart pumps more blood, consequently, improving blood circulation. The lungs’ intake of oxygen is increased and the muscles become stronger. […] Sports also have a positive mental effect on people. Playing sports whether alone or in a team is a good way of releasing stress, it is motivating and rewarding. It also improves the memory and boosts communication.

Questions: (a) Explain how sports can be motivating, rewarding and improve memory and communication. (b) Name other benefits of sports.

Page 73–75 · Reading 5

Activity 4: Read about other habits and answer the following questions. By playing sports and eating a well-balanced diet we are leading a healthy life style, but what about other healthy habits? Basic rules of hygiene are necessary in order to stay healthy. If they are not followed, then it is easy for infections and diseases to threaten our health and the health of everyone we come into contact with. These basic rules include washing hands frequently. […] Brushing teeth at least twice a day is another important habit that preserves the health of your teeth. Fill in the mind map with your important healthy habits.

Activity 5: Keep a Health Diary for the next week. In it, record your daily diet, your physical activity, and the rules of hygiene you follow, and how often.
