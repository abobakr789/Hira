Page 157 · Project

You will make a PowerPoint Presentation about traditional Emirati sports and games.

Step 1: Read about traditional Emirati sports and games. Step 2: Find out information about one of the traditional games. Step 3: Make PowerPoint slides about a traditional Emirati game. Step 4: Add pictures to your PowerPoint presentation. Step 5: Show your PowerPoint to your class.

Page 158–159 · Activity 1

People in the UAE have fun and interesting traditional sports and games. Traditional sports and games were played in the past. People still play these sports and games today. People made sports and games with what they had around them in nature.

Falconry: One traditional sport is falconry. Falconry is training falcons to hunt. Falcons have been used for hunting in the UAE for almost 2,000 years. Now, falconry is a sport. You need skill and patience to do it.

Activity 1. Write 3 things about falconry.

Page 160 · Activity 2

Camel Racing: In the past people used camels to travel long distances in the desert. Now camels are used for racing as a sport. People used to ride the camels in the past. Now robotic jockeys ride the camels during their races. The robotic jockeys are controlled by the trainers who drive along the track in their cars.

Activity 2. Write 3 things about camel racing.

Page 161 · Activity 3

Horse racing: Like camel racing, horse racing is very important in Arab culture and history. One of the most famous horse races, the Dubai World Cup, is held in the UAE.

Horse races are not always for speed. In endurance racing, horses go for long distances. The World Endurance Championship is held in the UAE.

Activity 3. Write 3 things about horse racing.

Page 162 · Activity 4

Traditional games: Some traditional games are Al Teela, Habil Al Zaibal, Al Dusays, Al Zubout, Al Karabi and Al Miryhana. They are a part of the history and culture of the UAE.

Activity 4. Search for information about UAE traditional games. Choose one game and write how the game is played.
