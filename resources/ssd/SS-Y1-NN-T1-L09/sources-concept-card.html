Page 68 · Activity 2

I love Fridays! On Fridays, my grandma and grandpa come to our house. My grandma helps my mum cook. Grandma is a good cook!

I love my grandma. She’s kind and fun. She loves me and she wants me to be happy. She always makes my favourite food.

My grandma says that I’m a special girl. She makes me very happy.

Today, something is different. We sit at the table. I look at the food. My grandma hasn’t made my favourite food! She made chicken curry! I don’t like spicy food!

Page 69 · Activity 2

My grandma asks me to eat the chicken curry. She says she’s very busy. She didn’t have time to cook my favourite food.

I love my grandma. I don’t like to say no to her. I’ll try to eat the chicken curry!

Page 70 · Activity 4

Waddah wants to play with his friends. He asks his friends to come to his house. He thinks about what food his friends like. He thinks they like popcorn, pizza, biriyani and orange juice.

Waddah thinks about what his friends like to do. He thinks they like watching films.

Pretend to be Waddah. What food do you give your friends? What do you do with your friends? Why?
