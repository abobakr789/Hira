CONCEPT CARD

Tolerance

Tolerance means respecting people who are different from us. Discrimination means treating people unfairly. Role models like Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. and Mahatma Gandhi taught peace and forgiveness. The UAE promotes tolerance with a Minister of Tolerance and laws against hatred and discrimination.

Through tolerance, peace can grow all over the world.

Words to know

Page 39 · Activity 2

Martin Luther King is a hero of freedom and tolerance. Read the following about him. Then do the tasks that follow.

Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. was a political activist and spiritual leader. He was born in Atlanta, Georgia, in the United States of America, in 1929. He is one of the most important people who fought for all people to be treated the same. Many people liked Dr. King for the peaceful way he fought for racial equality and for African Americans’ civil and political rights.

The Atlanta that Dr. King grew up in was filled with racism. He became a politician after he started college. He himself suffered unfair treatment throughout his life due to the colour of his skin. One time, his house was bombed, and he, his wife and son almost died. Because of this, he started coming up with ideas to teach people about ‘peace’. He taught people to be forgiving and to avoid violence. He is especially famous for his ‘I Have a Dream’ speech, which he gave at a political meeting in 1963. During this speech he talked about his dream of his children one day being able to live in a society that didn’t judge them based on the colour of their skin, but on their behaviour.

Page 40–41 · Activity 2

As a result of his efforts in fighting for human rights, the American President of the time, Lyndon Johnson, signed the Civil Rights Act in 1964. This act was to stop the bad treatment of people because of the colour of their skin in the workplace and public places and gave them other human rights too. President Johnson also signed the Voting Rights Act in 1965.

In 1964, Dr. King was awarded the Nobel Peace Prize. He was the first African American to win the award and is the youngest ever winner. Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. was killed in 1968.

Martin Luther King Jr. Day is now celebrated every year on the third Monday of January and is a holiday in the United States. Dr. King’s is celebrated in many ways on this day, including people taking part in volunteer work in their communities.

The Martin Luther King Jr. Research and Education Institute is based at Stanford University, California. It’s home to all of Dr. King’s speeches. Stanford University regularly brings together social activists from all over the world to work on supporting human rights.

Question. Why is Martin Luther King considered a hero of freedom and tolerance? Write sentences from the discussion to answer the question.

Page 44–45 · Activity 5

Read the following two texts on the theme of tolerance. The first one is on the UAE’s Year of Tolerance and Happiness in 2016. The second one is on the leader of the Indian independence movement, Mahatma Gandhi. Then design a wall chart with pictures, words and sentences on the subject of tolerance. Quote the words of important role models of tolerance from the UAE, India and the wider world.

The UAE is known for the values of tolerance, peace, security and cultural diversity. There are more than 200 nationalities in the UAE. They are all enjoying a fair and good quality of life. Laws in the UAE promise justice, respect and equality for everyone. Laws in the UAE make hatred, intolerance and discrimination a crime.

The UAE has played an important part in international agreements calling for a stop to violence, extremism and discrimination. In addition, it has become a global meeting place, for eastern and western cultures. The UAE has been an example of peace, harmony, and respect for different beliefs. The many churches and temples in the country enable non-muslims to practise their religion.

The country has also led the way in pushing for global security and peace. The UAE aims to improve living standards for everyone.

The Mohammed bin Rashid Al Maktoum Award for World Peace is one of the most important awards in the field of international bridge-building and peace-making. The award comes from the teachings of Islam. They teach about tolerance and moderation. Some of the key achievements of the UAE government in promoting tolerance across the UAE include: • Having a Minister of Tolerance • Having a National Programme of Tolerance • Having an Anti-Discrimination and Hatred Law • Opening the Hedayah Centre • Opening the Sawab Centre.

Page 46–47 · Activity 5

The words of Mahatma Gandhi, Indian independence leader.

Gandhi was asked, ‘Why don’t you take revenge on your enemies?’ He said, ‘Because I can’t spend my life running after a dog to bite him in the same way that he bites me.’ He also said: ‘I do not like the word tolerance but I can’t find a better word.’

‘Anger and intolerance are the twin enemies of correct understanding.’ ‘Hatred ever kills, love never dies.’ ‘Having thrown aside the sword, there is nothing except the cup of love which I can offer to those who against me.’ ‘The weak can never forgive. Forgiveness is the characteristic of the strong.’

‘I look only at the good qualities of men. Not being faultless myself, I won’t look into the faults of others.’ ‘I believe that non-violence is better than violence and forgiveness is more manly than punishment.’ ‘We can only win against our enemies by love, never by hate.’ ‘Hate is the smallest form of violence. It affects the hater without affecting the hated at all.’ ‘We must respect other religions even as we respect our own.’
