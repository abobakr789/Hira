CONCEPT CARD

Prejudgments

Stereotyping means believing that all people in a group are the same. Discrimination means treating people unfairly. A prejudgment is an opinion about someone before we know them. We should get to know people and treat everyone with respect and compassion.

Get to know people before you judge them.

Page 49–50 · Activity 1

Write on a piece of paper something that girls are known for. Write on another piece of paper something that boys are known for. You can include hobbies, favourite colours, favourite places, feelings etc. Stick your piece of paper to the board with all the other pieces of paper from your classmates. Discuss what has been written with your classmates.

• Do you agree with everything that has been written? • Is it fair to think that all girls and all boys behave in the same way or feel the same way? • What is this kind of generalising called?

Write sentences from the discussion to answer the questions.

Page 51 · Activity 2

Read this story about something that happened on a plane. Then answer the questions that follow.

On board a plane, a woman in economy class, where the cheapest seats are, saw that the man next to her had white burn scars on his hands and chin. She angrily called the flight attendant and said, ‘I’m seated next to a deformed man. I’m so disgusted. I can’t sit next to him throughout the flight! Please find me another seat.’

The attendant tried to calm her down, saying quietly, ‘Don’t worry, Madam. These are just burn marks and they won’t make you ill’.

However, the woman kept asking to be moved. She didn’t want to sit next to the man, who she thought was ugly.

The attendant then said, ‘All the seats in economy class are taken, but I’ll do my best to find a solution.’

The attendant left for a while, then she came back and said, ‘I told the captain about the problem and he told me that there’s an available seat in first class.’ The woman seemed pleased and was about to stand up when the attendant asked her to remain in her seat. She went on, ‘Madam, although we do not usually allow an economy-class passenger to sit in first class, the captain decided to change the rules in this case as he thought it was wrong for someone to be in a bad situation like this.’ Then, the attendant turned to the man and said, ‘Please bring your belongings and follow me, sir. There’s a seat waiting for you in first class.’

Page 52 · Activity 2

1. Why did the woman react negatively to the passenger next to her?

2. What do you think of her behaviour?

3. How would you have felt if you had been in this man’s shoes?

4. The woman acted in a discriminatory way towards the man. How would you have acted if you had been in her position?

Page 53–55 · Activity 3

Look at this picture of the former Prime Minister of the Netherlands. Then, answer the questions. What are common stereotypes about politicians?

Read about the life of Dutch Prime Minister Mark Rutte. Then, think again about the life of a prime minister.

The Netherlands’ former Prime Minister Mark Rutte was born in The Hague and raised in a middle-class family. His father was a merchant and his mother was a secretary. They taught him to be modest, humble, hard-working. He was also taught to be respectful and supportive of others. His dream was to be a pianist. He learned to play the piano at a music school. However, he later chose to study history at Leiden University. Before becoming a politician, Rutte worked in business. He worked as the general manager of a company. Rutte got the world’s attention by cycling to the government offices, with no grand parade or bodyguards.

Does his life match the stereotypes that you thought of before you read the text?

Page 56 · Activities 4 and 5

Activity 4. Read the following judgements and stereotypes about poor people. Discuss each one with your classmates.

• Poor people are lazy and hate to work. • If poor people showed more effort they would have better lives. • Poor people choose to rely on help from other people.

Activity 5. Prepare a role-play script to present in class. The aim of the role-play is to show compassion to a family who is living in poverty. Include the following in the role-play script: • A description of this family’s situation. • Scenes of discrimination from other people towards them. • An invitation to show compassion. • A suggestion of a solution for this family’s situation.

Let’s talk

① What is stereotyping? ② Why was the woman on the plane wrong? ③ What did Mark Rutte’s life show?
