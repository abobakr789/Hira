CONCEPT CARD

Treat others kindly

We all have feelings and emotions. We like to be treated in a kind and friendly way, not in a mean way. Always treat other people the same way you would like to be treated. Our behaviour shows our kindness.

I treat others the way I like to be treated.

Pick a card.

Page 22 · Activity 2

One day, Farmer Salem took his daughter Nour to visit his brother. Salem and his brother drank coffee in the living room. Nour and her cousin Rashid went into the garden to play.

Nour saw two bicycles, a big blue one and a little red one, in the corner of the garden. After she asked Rashid for permission, Nour rode the big blue bicycle, and Rashid rode the little red one. They cycled around the garden.

Then Majid, Rashid’s younger brother, came and said, ‘Get off! Get off my bicycle, now!’ Majid grabbed hold of his brother’s clothes and said, ‘Who gave you permission to ride my bicycle?’

Page 22–23 · Activity 2

Rashid got off the bike and tried to push his brother. Majid was crying and shouting. At that moment, their mother came out with some glasses of juice on a tray. She put the tray down on the table.

She said to her two children, ‘Let’s think! How do we all like to be treated by other people?’ She looked at Majid and said, ‘You, Majid. Don’t you like to play with your brother’s ball sometimes? What if he wouldn’t let you have it?’

Then she turned to Rashid, saying, ‘What about you, Rashid? Do you like it when your brother pushes you and you fall over?’

The two boys felt ashamed. Then they smiled at each other.

‘Let’s be kind,’ Rashid said.

Their mother laughed and said, ‘Now I’m happy! It’s so nice when we are all living together peacefully and happily.’

Page 23 · Let’s Think

‘Always treat other people the same way you would like to be treated.’ What does this mean?

Nour asked for permission before riding Rashid’s bicycle. Was this the right thing to do?

How did Majid feel when he saw his brother riding his bicycle? Would you act in the same way?

1. My classmate left his pen at home.

2. My classmate fell over in the playground.

3. On the first day of school, my classmate missed his mother.

4. My sister broke her toy.

For each one, write: the situation – feelings – what I would do.

Activity 4. How well do I get along with my friends? Tell your classmates what you would like to hear them say. I like it when you tell me ... I don’t like it when you tell me ...
