📖 Book text

Student Book Vol. 1 · Original text from the Student Book.

Page 47–49 · Reading 1

What are moral values? What is voluntary work? What are some examples of voluntary work? Activity 1: How do you apply moral rules to control behaviour? Look at the pictures and discuss the moral values they represent.

Activity 2: On January 17, 2016, a Filipino taxi driver took an Australian businessman to Baguio city in the Philippines. When he was getting out of the car, the businessman was in a hurry and left his bag behind. […] We arrived at the police station to report the missing luggage, but found that the taxi driver had got there before us. […] The Philippines government gave this honest taxi driver a certificate of appreciation and praise during a special ceremony. […] an Australian company promised to offer him a job as a reward for his honesty.

Page 49–50 · Reading 2

Questions: (a) What do you think would have happened if the driver had not brought the bag to the police station? What would you have done if you were the driver? Why? (b) Conduct a questionnaire in class. Ask your classmates to name the three most important moral rules that should be applied in class, in the playground, on the school bus, at the school library, in the laboratory.

Activity 3: The UAE regards human rights as a top priority. The UAE is committed to promoting and protecting human rights at home and throughout the world. The pillars of the State’s foreign policy are based upon the principles of justice, equality and the observance of these rights. […] Tasks: (a) Suggest a list of five rights and a list of five duties that you should respect to preserve human rights in your community and throughout the world. (b) Work in groups and prepare a study on how people apply moral rules by observing their actions in society, institutions, schools and other places. Present the results in class.

Page 51–52 · Reading 3

Activity 4: Voluntary work is an essential activity that reflects the moral values in our community and the world. During their leisure time, one third of Germans participate in voluntary work and activities for charitable purposes. Such activities help people develop moral values, such as integrity and honesty, and social values, such as tolerance and helping others. […] In the United Arab Emirates, HH Sheikh Mohamad Bin Rashid Al Maktoum launched volunteers.ae, the National Volunteer Platform in Dubai. This platform aims to encourage more people to do volunteer work in the UAE by facilitating the process of volunteering. […] Individuals can register and apply for volunteer roles on the website, and can use the platform to search for opportunities according to their interest, skills and experience.

Page 53–54 · Reading 4

Tasks: (a) Voluntary work is a moral duty towards society. Explain the concepts of both voluntary work and moral duty. (b) Did you know that anyone over the age of six years can volunteer in the Dubai Volunteering Program? Design an activity or task that will benefit you and the school so that you will be an example for other students to follow.

Activity 5: The application of moral values is reflected in the protection of the environment. Environmental protection is the main objective of the UAE’s development policies, which aim to increase green spaces and develop water resources. The UAE has developed a set of laws to ensure environmental sustainability. […] The environment in Finland is in a positive and healthy state. […] Tasks: (a) What is the connection between moral values and environmental preservation? Give examples. (b) Conduct a team exercise with your classmates that benefits the school environment and submit a report.

Page 56–57 · Reading 5

Activity 6: Discuss what you see in the images (students in the United States cleaning their town; a young man helping an elderly lady carry shopping). (a) What are the moral rules reflected in the images? (b) Why is it important to practise ethics in daily life? What impact does it have on individuals and societies?

Activity 7: Read the quote. “Without morality and good behaviour, and without learning, nations cannot build their generations nor do their duty.” Sheikh Zayed bin Sultan Al Nahyan (may God have mercy upon him). Tasks: (a) Identify the values that you want to enhance in your school. (b) Develop a project to raise awareness and instill global moral values among children at the primary stage, for example a theatrical performance or an open day for values. (c) Launch a media campaign to support your project and invite parents to help.

Let’s talk

① What did the taxi driver do with the bag, in the book? ② What is volunteers.ae, in the book? ③ What is the main objective of UAE development policies on the environment, in the book?
