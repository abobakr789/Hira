📖 Book text

Student Book Vol. 1 · Original text from the Student Book.

Page 84–85 · Reading 1

What things are good or bad for the environment in your country? What are people in your country doing to save the environment?

Activity 1: Try this. Work in groups. Collect the following: a plastic cup, water, a spoonful of flour, a little soil, a spoonful of oil, and some pencil shavings. Mix everything together in the plastic cup. Think about how the water can be made clean again.

Page 86 · Reading 2

In the theatre, the show is ready to start. The curtain rises and the people can hear the sea. On a large screen, they can see a lady swimming deep in the water. She is wearing a diving suit. Many kinds of fish are swimming around her. […] The girl answers from behind the screen, ‘I am Julia. I’m from Hawaii, in America. Hawaii is a group of over a hundred islands and coral reefs in the Pacific Ocean. I’m interested in coral reefs. I came to the United Arab Emirates to learn more about them. I wanted to see your beautiful sea life.’

Page 87 · Reading 3

‘Come and tell us what you found out about the coral reefs’, said Abdullah. Julia walks onto the stage. […] She says, ‘The coral reefs in many countries are dying because of pollution. I hope that other countries will copy the UAE and try to protect their sea life the way you do.’ ‘Here in the UAE, we are all friends of the environment’, said Abdullah. ‘We know that we must look after it. You are always welcome to visit our coral reefs and their beautiful fish.’

Questions: (1) Where are the Hawaiian islands? What is important about them? (2) What are the causes of sea pollution? (3) Who do you think causes sea pollution? (4) Who does it hurt?

Let’s Think: Let’s think about the statement, ‘We are all friends of the environment.’ What can we do to help protect the natural environment?

Page 88–90 · Reading 4

Activity 3: Work in groups. What problems for the environment can you see in the pictures? How can we solve these problems? The page has three picture boxes with the headings Problem and Solution.

Page 91 · Reading 5

Activity 4: Work in groups. You are going to work on a project called ‘I Must Look After My Environment’. Follow the steps below. Make a list of things that are a danger to the natural environment. This could be in the sea, in the air, or on land. Make a poster with two columns. At the top of one column write ‘A Healthy Environment’. At the top of the second column write ‘An Unhealthy Environment.’ Collect photographs and pictures showing dangers to the environment and healthy environments. Stick these on the poster in the correct column. Hang your poster on the wall outside your classroom. Invite other students and teachers to write comments on your poster.

Let’s talk

① Where is Julia from? ② Why are coral reefs dying in many countries, according to Julia? ③ What does Abdullah say we all are?
