💬 Talk

⏱ 5 minutes

Let’s talk

💬 Let’s talk – answer with a full sentence.

1. Why is Mila sad?

Her mum and dad are at the hospital, and she has a new baby brother. She is worried that mum will love the baby more.

2. How does Mila’s grandma make Mila feel happy?

Grandma gives her a hug and takes her out. Mila loves going out with her.

3. What other feelings does Mila have in the story?

She feels worried. She also has many questions and may feel left out.
My Family
Ask each question and let children answer in pairs, then as a whole class. Show the model answer only after the children have answered.

🧑‍🏫 I do · We do · You do

⏱ 5 minutes

Let me show you

🧑‍🏫 I do – the teacher shows

“I love my family. My dad plays with me.”

👫 We do – we try together

“I love my ___. My ___ helps me.”

🧒 You do – I try alone

“I love my ___. My ___ ___.”

Sentence frame

I love my ______. My ______ ______ me.

My Family
Model the sentence with your own example first (I do). Build a second sentence with the class (We do). Then pairs say their own (You do).
