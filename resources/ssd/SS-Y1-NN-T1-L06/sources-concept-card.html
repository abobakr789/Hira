⭐ How do you feel about your family?

Page 49 · Activity 2

Today is a special day. My mum and dad go to the hospital. Mum has a new baby.

Grandma and grandpa look after me. I feel sad.

My grandparents try to make me feel happy, but I want to stay in my room.

I don’t know why I feel so sad. I have a lot of questions. Will I have a little brother or sister? Will mum love the baby more than me? Will the baby be more important than me? Why is everyone so excited about the new baby?

Page 49 · Activity 2

My grandparents open the door and come into my room. They’re excited.

“Mila! Good news! You have a new baby brother. His name is Silvio.”

I feel sad. My grandma gives me a hug.

“Come on, Mila. Let’s go out.”

My grandma makes me happy. I love going out with her.

Page 51 · Activity 3

Look at the pictures in the Student Book (p. 51) and say how Mila feels.

Page 52 · Activity 4

1. My mum takes care of my little brother. She asks me to do my homework.

2. My friend is sick. He goes to hospital.

3. My mum and dad see me win a competition.

4. I love my cousins and play with them. Then, they move far away.

5. My little sister plays with my favourite toy. She breaks my toy.

Page 52–53 · Activities 5 and 6

Activity 5: Who is in your family? What do they do for your family? Match family members to what they do. (my mum, my dad, my grandma, my grandpa)

1. help me with my homework 2. take care of me 3. cook food 4. go to the supermarket 5. read me a story 6. clean the house

Activity 6: What do you do for your family? Write two things that you do for your family.

Let’s talk
