CONCEPT CARD

Respect is a duty

Respect means to treat someone or something with kindness and care. It is our duty to show respect. We listen, greet, take turns and say sorry. We respect people, animals and things.

I show respect when I listen, greet and care.

Page 16 · Activity 2

One day, chaos spread throughout the farm, leaving hay and wood scattered everywhere.

‘What’s going on? Why all this mess?’, said Farmer Salem. ‘I’m not responsible!’

‘Cock a doodle doo!’, answered the rooster.

The chicken interrupted him, saying, ‘I laid a large egg.’

The cow shouted, ‘I want water! Where’s my water?’

The sheep shouted, ‘I want grass. I’m hungry!’

Page 16 · Activity 2

At that moment, Salem’s nephew, Rashid, came and greeted everyone. ‘Good morning, my friends,’ he said.

‘Good morning and welcome!’ said Salem. ‘Come over here, Rashid!’

The animals continued to cause chaos, accusing one another of different things, and demanding food and water.

Salem turned to them and said, ‘Enough! I can’t understand anything anymore. How can we communicate if everyone talks at the same time? Haven’t you noticed Rashid is here? Why haven’t you greeted him?’

The animals looked at one another and realised their mistake. They apologised to Salem and then to one another. Then each of them greeted Rashid.
