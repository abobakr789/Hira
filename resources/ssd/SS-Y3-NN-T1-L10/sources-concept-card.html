📖 Book text

Student Book Vol. 1 · Original text from the Student Book.

Page 92–93 · Reading 1

Do you think water and trees are important? Why? What can we do to save water and trees?

Activity 1: Agreeing and Disagreeing. Ibrahim bought an old, traditional house in Al Ain. He wanted to knock it down and build a new one. His friend Yasser said he should keep the old house and restore it. Who do you agree with? Ibrahim or Yasser? Find someone in the class who disagrees with you and talk to them about it.

Page 94 · Reading 2

The curtain rose again. The large screen showed a boy sitting in the middle of the desert. He looked very sad. […] The boy replied, ‘I am Hassan. I am from Egypt. Egypt is one of the oldest places on Earth. It is in the north of Africa.’ Raya said, ‘Why are you so sad?’ Hassan said, ‘The desert called me. It said, ‘My friend, why have you left me all alone? The birds and all the other living creatures are gone. My sand is almost gone. There is no water. There are no trees to protect me.’

Page 94–95 · Reading 3

Abdullah held out his hand to Hassan and said, ‘Come with us. We will all work together to help the desert. […]’ Now the screen showed a desert in the UAE, with flowers and trees. Everyone smiled. Abdullah said, ‘Don’t worry Hassan. We know how to protect the desert. We will water it.’ Raya said, ‘I will plant trees.’ Julia said, ‘I will build houses near the desert.’ Sam said, ‘I will give it electricity.’ […] The friends stood together on the stage. They said, ‘If we work together, we can make our world a better place.’ […] The winner is “Human World”.

Reading carefully: These are promises in a play, not a conservation plan. Deserts are living habitats. Protect native plants and animals and save water; buildings, electricity and extra watering are not automatically environmental protection.

Let’s Think: Let’s think about the statement, ‘If we work together, we can make our world a better place.’ What does this mean?

Page 96–97 · Reading 4

Activity 3: Looking after our resources. The late Sheikh Zayed bin Sultan Al Nahyan (may God have mercy upon him) said, ‘Our environment and resources are not our property; they have been entrusted to us. We all have responsibility for looking after them and passing them on safely to future generations.’ Talk about this in groups. Look at the photographs. How can we protect these traditions?

Activity 4: Protecting the environment. Put a tick beside good behaviour and a cross beside bad behaviour. (a) I keep the tap on when I brush my teeth. (b) I switch off the light when I leave a room. (c) I sort my rubbish into paper, metal, plastic and glass. (d) I plant trees near my house.

Page 98 · Reading 5

Activity 5: Go to a park with your friends. Plant a tree to show how much you care about protecting the environment.
