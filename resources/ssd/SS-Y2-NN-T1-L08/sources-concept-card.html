CONCEPT CARD

Teamwork

A team is a group of people who work together to reach a goal. In teamwork, each person has tasks. Teamwork achieves goals and saves time. Bees are a good example: they all work together in the hive.

I work well with others in a team.

Words to know

togetherwith each othertasksjobs we need to do
workto do a job or taskbeehivethe home of bees

Page 57 · Activity 1

In the village where you live, there is a nasty dragon. There is only one way to save the village and the people in it. You must stand in a straight line in order of height, from the shortest to the tallest. You can’t speak. If you speak, the dragon will wake up. When you are all standing in line, everyone shouts, ‘BOO’ at the same time. This will scare the dragon away!

Page 58 · Activity 2

One day, Adam decided to go for a walk. He was interested in nature and wanted to explore. He walked for a few minutes and discovered a garden full of flowers. There was a shed in the garden. Adam tried to see what was inside the shed by looking through a hole in the wall. He saw a beehive inside.

The bees were all busily going about their work. One group of bees was making beeswax. Another was guarding the hive. A third group was cleaning. While this was all going on, the queen bee was laying her eggs. Then Adam looked at the honeycomb with its hexagonal cells. He saw a sticky yellow liquid oozing out. He knew that this was the bees’ honey and he tasted it.

Page 58 · Activity 2

He said, ‘Wow! It’s so delicious!’ He tasted it again. ‘It really is delicious!’

Adam watched the bees go about their work. He watched without making any noise. He did not want to disturb the worker bees. Adam sat and watched the bees for a long time. He was not bored because the bees were always moving and busy working together to make their delicious honey. Then, he went back home with a lot of questions. He told his mother about the bees.

She smiled and said, ‘Of course, teamwork achieves goals and saves time.’
