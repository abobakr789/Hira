CONCEPT CARD

UAE culture

Culture is the way a group of people live. Emirati culture includes the Ayala dance, Sadu weaving and animals such as falcons and camels. Emiratis like to share their culture at festivals.

Culture is something we can share.

Emiratis show their culture in different ways. One way is the Ayala dance. Men stand in lines and dance with sticks.

The men stand in two lines. They stand opposite each other. Drummers play music and the men dance to the music. Emiratis do the Ayala dance at weddings, and on other special days.

Page 117 · Activity 2

Weaving is another way Emiratis show their culture. A long time ago, Bedouin women did Sadu weaving. Sadu is made from camel hair.

Women used Sadu to make blankets and other things. Traditional colors were red, black, white, and green.

Some animals are important in Emirati culture. Animals like horses, camels, donkeys, falcons and the salukis.

Page 118 · Activity 2

Falcons used to help people hunt for food in the past. Today falconry is a sport or hobby.

Saluki dogs are desert dogs. They used to help people hunt in the past. They used to take care of the caravans.

Emiratis like to share their culture. Special festivals share Emirati culture with visitors and people who live in the UAE.
