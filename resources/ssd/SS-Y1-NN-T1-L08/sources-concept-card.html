CONCEPT CARD

Showing my feelings

We have good and bad feelings. We can show them with our face, body and words, and we can say what makes us feel this way.

I can show my feelings in a kind way.

Words to know

angryvery cross about somethingupsetsad or unhappy
scaredafraidgood feelinga happy feeling

Page 61 · Activity 1

⭐ Pick a card.

⭐ Act out the feeling on the card. Your classmates guess the feeling you’re acting out.

⭐ Get into two groups.

⭐ If you acted out a good feeling, get in Group 1. If you acted out a bad feeling, get into Group 2.

Page 62 · Activity 2

My brother Silvio is upset all the time. He cries in the day, and he cries at night. Every time he cries my mum runs to his room to take care of him. It makes me angry. I want to shout at him. I want him to be quiet.

One day, Silvio cries and my mum runs to his room. She holds him in her arms. She looks worried. She tells my dad that Silvio is sick. He needs a doctor.

Page 63 · Activity 2

I go to school. At school, I think about my mum, my dad and Silvio. I feel lonely.

My mum and dad take care of my brother and hug him. I feel jealous. I want to shout at Silvio.

In the playground I see my friend Mona. She’s crying. I ask her why she’s sad. She tells me that her little sister is sick. She’s worried about her. She wants school to end. She wants to go home to see her sister.

I feel scared. I hope Silvio feels better soon. I love him so much.

Page 64 · Activities 3 and 4

Activity 3: Look at the good and bad feelings below. Write what makes you have these feelings. Talk about them with your classmates.

Activity 4: Look at the pictures. How do they feel? Write a feeling under each picture.

⭐ It’s very loud!

Let’s talk
