CONCEPT CARD

What am I good at?

A talent is something I am very good at. Everyone is special and can be great at something if they try hard and practise.

Everyone has a talent. I can try hard to get better.

5. Can you make a kite? 6. Can you name five different types of fruit? 7. Can you say ‘red lorry, yellow lorry’ ten times? 8. Can you say five words that begin with the letter ‘d’?

Page 75 · Activity 2

Today there’s a talent show at school! The teacher asks us what we are good at. The teacher wants us to share our talents. All the children in school, and their families come to school. They watch the show.

Shaima is my friend. She’s good at art. She shows everyone a beautiful drawing. Philip acts out a story. He’s a good actor. Carl reads a poem. The poem is about his mum. His poem makes everyone happy.

Page 76 · Activity 2

I didn’t do anything in the talent show. I don’t think I have a special talent.

I’m good at science. My favourite day is the school science competition! There are lots of children in the competition. I answer all the questions correctly. I’m in the final of the competition!

My friend Amer is in the final too. The teacher asks us one more question. We both know the answer to the question, but I am fast! I win the competition! I feel very special.

I think everyone can be great at something. We just have to try hard!

Page 77–78 · Activities 3 and 4

Activity 3: Draw something you are good at. Draw something you are not good at. Then, talk about your pictures. (I am good at… I’m not good at…)

Activity 4: Talk about the pictures with your classmates. Then, answer the questions.
