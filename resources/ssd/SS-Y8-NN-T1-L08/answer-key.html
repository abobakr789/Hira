🔑 Teacher answer key

For teachers only. Accept any sensible answer for open questions.

Worksheet Level 1

Task 1 (matching, in row order): 1 A stereotype; 2 Discrimination; 3 Prejudice; 4 A class charter. Task 2: circle stereotype, bias, discrimination, appearance, prejudice. Task 3: a relevant drawing and sensible completed definition; accept reasoned answers.

Worksheet Level 2

Task 1: 1 True · 2 False · 3 True · 4 True. Task 2: Suggested: 1 heard · 2 prejudice · 3 values. Task 3: stereotype: a fixed idea about a group of people; bias: liking or disliking people unfairly; prejudice: judging people unfairly before knowing them. The private scale and the role play are teacher-led.

Worksheet Level 3

All tasks are open. Look for: a paragraph with clear reasons; sensible actions; a clear poster.
