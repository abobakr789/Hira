CONCEPT CARD

Sharing is caring

When we share, we give others a part of what we have. Sharing shows we are caring and fair. A selfish person thinks only of themselves.

Sharing is caring.

Words to know

sharegive some of what I have to otherstree housea little house in a tree
ideaa thought or a planfriendsomeone I like to play with
caringbeing kind and helping otherslunchthe meal in the middle of the day
selfishonly thinking about myselfturnmy time to play or speak

Think and talk

① Why is it important to share? ② What do I share with my family? ③ What do I share with my friends?

📖 Book text

Student Book Vol. 1 · Original text from the Student Book.

Page 37–38 · Activity 1

The teacher asks the class to sit in a circle. She wants them to do an activity. Kumar doesn’t want to sit next to Saki. He doesn’t want to work with her.

The teacher asks Kumar why he won’t work with Saki. “Saki doesn’t work hard. She doesn’t share her ideas. She’s selfish!” Kumar says.

Page 39 · Activity 2

Rashid goes home. There is a new tree house in the garden! His dad made a tree house! Rashid is very excited.

“Rashid this is your tree house now,” said Rashid’s dad. “But you must share it with your friends. Remember, sharing is caring!”

Rashid plays in the new tree house every day.

Page 39–40 · Activity 2

One day, Uncle Salem and Rashid’s cousins visit. Rashid is happy to see his cousin, Majed. Rashid asks Majed to play in the tree house.

His little cousin Hamad comes to the tree house. He wants to play too! Rashid shakes his head and shouts to Hamad.

“You can’t play in my tree house! You’re too little!”

Then, Rashid’s cousin Hessa wants to play in the tree house. Rashid looks down and shouts to Hessa. “You can’t play in my tree house! No girls in my tree house!”

Page 40 · Activity 2

Then, Rashid’s neighbour wants to play. His name is Adam. He asks Rashid if he can play in the tree house. Rashid laughs and shouts to Adam. “You can’t play in my tree house! You don’t like football!”

Rashid’s mum calls everyone for lunch. After lunch, Hamad, Hessa and Adam run to the tree house and start to play.

Rashid and Majed come to the tree house. Hamad, Hessa and Adam shout together… “Don’t be selfish Rashid! We can share the tree house! Sharing is caring!”

Page 41–42 · Activities 3–5

Activity 3: Read the story again. Think about what happens next in the story. Draw a picture.

Activity 4: Work with a partner. Look at the picture and talk about what is happening. How can Molly be fair? Act it out with your partner. (Molly has a new necklace. She won’t let her sister borrow it.)

Activity 5: Only four people can play a classroom game. You and your friends are in a group of four. Sony asks if he can play with you. How can you and your friends be fair to Sony?

Page 43 · Activity 6

Play ‘The Sharing Game’ with your classmates.

⭐ Sit in a circle with your classmates.

⭐ Pass a ball around the circle.

⭐ When you have the ball, say one thing you like to share and one thing you don’t like to share.

Let’s talk

① Is Rashid fair to Hamad, Hessa and Adam? ② How do you think Rashid feels at the end of the story? ③ What do you think happens next?
