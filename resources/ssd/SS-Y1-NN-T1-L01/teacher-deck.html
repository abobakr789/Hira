💬 Talk

⏱ 5 minutes

Let’s talk about the story

💬 Let’s talk – answer with a full sentence.

1. Does Rashid love his family? How do you know?

Yes, he does. He says, “I love my family!” and he is happy to see them.

2. What did Rashid say about Samer, Amer and Hessa?

He likes cousin Samer and will play with him. He says Amer and Hessa are funny.

3. When do you see everyone in your family?

Answers will be different, for example: at weekends, on special days, at family lunch.
Everyone Around Me
Ask each question and let children answer in pairs, then as a whole class. Model sentence starters: "Rashid loves his family because ...". Show the model answer only after the children have answered.
