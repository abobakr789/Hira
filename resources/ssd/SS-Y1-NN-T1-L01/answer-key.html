🔑 Teacher answer key

For teachers only. Accept any sensible answer for open questions.

Worksheet Level 1

Task 1: Answers can vary. Suggested: Mum → cooks for me; Grandpa → tells me stories; Friend → plays with me; Teacher → helps me learn.

Task 2: Kind words: helpful, kind, polite, friendly. (Not: rude, angry.)

Task 3: Open. The child names a person and draws them.

Worksheet Level 2

Task 1: 1 True · 2 False · 3 True · 4 False.

Task 2: Open. Example: “My friend is important to me because we play together.” · Third sentence: polite.

Task 3: helpful → gives a hand to others · polite → says please and thank you · friendly → nice to meet and play with.

Worksheet Level 3

All tasks are open. Look for: a person named, a reason with because, a kind action that is possible this week, polite words in the thank-you note.

Let’s talk (Student Book p. 11)

1. Yes. He says “I love my family!” and he is happy to see them. 2. He likes cousin Samer and will play with him; Amer and Hessa are funny. 3. Open (for example at weekends or on special days).

True or false (deck)

1 True · 2 False · 3 True · 4 False · 5 True.

Quick quiz (deck)

1 a (Rashid) · 2 b (his favourite food) · 3 c (the whole family) · 4 a (polite) · 5 b (funny).

Activity 5 matching (deck)

Omar → polite (or helpful) · Mia → friendly · Sara → helpful · Noor → kind. More than one word can fit if the child explains why.

Exit ticket

Open. Check each child names a person, gives a reason and gives an example of a kind action.
