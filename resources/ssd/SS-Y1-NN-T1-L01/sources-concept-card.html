CONCEPT CARD

Important people

People who are important to me are the people who love me, help me and care about me. They can be in my family, at school or in my community.

I can show they are important when I am helpful kind polite friendly.

📖 Book text: Rashid’s Family

Student Book Vol. 1 · page 10 · Activity 2. Dotted words are explained below.

Rashid wakes up. He listens to his mum in the kitchen. What is she doing?

Rashid gets up and walks to the kitchen. He looks at the table. He sees lots of food on the table.

Rashid’s mum is busy. She makes all Rashid’s favourite food!

Rashid’s mum makes harees, thareed and machboos. Rashid is very happy.

“Wow, Mum! There’s a lot of food!” Rashid says.

“Today, everyone in our family will eat lunch with us. It’s important we eat together. We can enjoy the food and we can talk,” says Rashid’s mum.

“That’s great! Everyone is coming?” asks Rashid. “Do you mean Grandpa Khaled, Grandma Samira, Grandpa Saleh and Grandma Khawla?”

“Yes,” says Rashid’s mum. “Your grandparents, uncles, aunts and cousins.”

“Great! I like cousin Samer – I’ll play with him. I’ll play with Amer and little Hessa too. Amer and Hessa are funny!”

“I love my family! It’s great to see them,” said Rashid.

What the words mean

hareesa soft food made from wheat and meatimportantsomething that matters a lot
thareedbread soaked in a tasty stewgrandparentsyour mum’s and dad’s parents
machboosrice cooked with spices and meat or fishuncles / auntsyour mum’s or dad’s brothers / sisters
cousinsthe children of your uncles and aunts

Let’s talk (Student Book p. 11)

① Does Rashid love his family? How do you know? ② What did Rashid say about Samer, Amer and Hessa? ③ When do you see everyone in your family?
